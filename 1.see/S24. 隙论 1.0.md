# S24. 隙论 1.0

**Title**: S24. Gap Thesis 1.0

归属：五道一法｜识部 See (S)｜向外看世界第四篇

万物之本为变。
The essence of all things is Flux.

变与隙共存；隙是恒在无垠基底，是容度的本体视角。一切存在，必依托隙方能舒展存续；若无隙，变无从开展，便归于死寂。疏密远近，仅是隙之内变排布之显相。
Flux and Gap coexist; Gap is the eternal, boundless substrate, the ontological ground of capacity. All existence must rely on Gap to unfold and endure; without Gap, Flux has no ground to act, and falls into dead stillness. Density and sparseness, distance and proximity, are but the manifest patterns of Flux rearranged within Gap.

从本体观之，基底广延常在，万千变于此迁移重排；构为锁束排布之力，不作功，如轨道，划定变的可行边界，可挤压局部容变之隙，却不能抹除基底本身。此为寰宇底层公理，贯通一切域。
From the ontological view, the substrate’s extension eternally abides, and myriad Flux migrate and rearrange within it. Construct is the force that locks and arranges; it does no work, like an orbit, drawing the feasible boundaries of Flux. It can compress the local Gap of capacity, but cannot erase the substrate itself. This is the underlying axiom of the cosmos, pervading all domains.

构落定，变循轨而定，排布凝成形貌，是为象。循恒在之隙探入延展，铺展新域，是为拓；芜杂壅滞，汰除冗余、廓开容变之隙，是为敛；敛蓄力以待再拓。敛拓交驰，生生绵延，是为续。挤压容度、封死通路，逼变内卷互噬，是为锁，趋入死寂。
When Construct settles, Flux follows the orbit and stabilizes; arrangement congeals into form — this is Manifestation. Following the eternal Gap to probe and extend, unfolding new domains — this is Expansion. When overgrowth and stagnation clog, eliminating redundancy and opening the Gap of capacity — this is Contraction; Contraction stores force awaiting further Expansion. Expansion and Contraction alternating, life flowing on — this is Continuation. Compressing capacity, sealing all passages, forcing Flux to turn inward, coil, and devour itself — this is Lock, tending towards dead stillness.

生命失隙，则生机枯竭；社会失隙，则流转僵滞；经济失隙，则困于内卷；地缘失隙，则冲突滋生；人与人相处失隙，则纷争暗生。凡此种种，皆是容度被持续挤压的征象。
Where life loses its Gap, vitality dries up; where society loses its Gap, circulation stiffens; where economy loses its Gap, it is trapped in involution; where geopolitics loses its Gap, conflict breeds; where human relations lose their Gap, disputes simmer. All these are symptoms of capacity under sustained compression.

隙为恒在无垠基底，变在其中迁流排布。万物演化的基本规律：向内挤压、锁闭容变之隙，终将归于死寂；向外探拓、依托恒在基底持续铺展，则可得永续生机。寰宇一切演化，皆是变、隙与构之间的本源张力。
Gap is the eternal, boundless substrate; Flux flows and arranges within it. The fundamental law of all evolution: to turn inward and lock the Gap of capacity leads ultimately to dead stillness; to reach outward, relying on the eternal substrate to unfold continuously, yields perpetual vitality. All evolution in the cosmos is the primordial tension among Flux, Gap, and Construct.
