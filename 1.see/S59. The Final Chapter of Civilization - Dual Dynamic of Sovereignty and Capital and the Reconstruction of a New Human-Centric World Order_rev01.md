## Preface

Across millennia of rises and falls and century-scale transformations, no geopolitical conflict, economic fracture, class division, technological barrier or global disorder is an accidental event, and none is a partial problem caused by a single policy or ideology. Every global malady traces back to a structural mismatch and alienation within the underlying mechanisms of human needs.

This theoretical system follows an order of inference from root to fruit and from within to without:

- The **A11 Three-Tier Attribution Axiom of Human Needs** anchors the first-principle foundation, fixing the innate rules of human nature.
- **S51** validates the cyclical laws of civilizations through historical context.
- **S57** fixes the coordinate system of spiritual values: Truth, Goodness and Beauty.
- **S58** builds a stable social structure through a two-tier order.

As the closing chapter of _Forward-Looking Civilization_, this paper completes the final elevation: from individual human-nature mechanisms to social operating rules, from the dual-system dynamic to the pathologies of the old global order, and ultimately to a new world order built on three-track separation and positive-sum reorientation, establishing a symbiosis of sovereignty, capital and human beings and achieving a complete causal closure of human civilization.

## Part I. Tracing the Root of Civilizational Imbalance: Human-Nature Axioms and the Pathologies of the Old Order

### I. First-Principle Foundation: The Mechanistic Differences of the A11 Three-Tier Human Needs

All civilizational orders, economic models and global dynamics obey the innate physical rules of the three-tier need framework. The three tiers differ in mechanism and must never be conflated or interchanged.

#### 1. L0 Survival-Needs: Finite Saturation and Rigid Universal Baseline

**Mechanistic Origin**: Generated by human metabolic physiology; covers food, shelter, healthcare and fundamental security. **

Core Properties**: Finite, stock-bound, non-predatory and universally necessary. Possesses a hard physical saturation threshold; once met, demand terminates and further increments add no value. 

**Civilizational Role**: The **baseline of Goodness** of civilization, a non-negotiable, non-predatory and non-tradable foundation for all humanity.

#### 2. L1 Neuro-Emotional Needs: Moderate Increment and Positive Competition

**Mechanistic Origin**: Generated by neuro-biochemical feedback; covers experiential pleasure, self-growth, value creation and technological exploration. 

**Core Properties**: Bounded, incremental, iterable and positively competitive. Individual differences and a floating range exist; positive incremental returns are obtainable through labor, innovation and contribution. 

**Civilizational Role**: The **Truth-seeking momentum** of civilization, the core driver of technological progress and productivity upgrading.

#### 3. L2 Communal-Identity Needs: Unbounded Zero-Sum and Relative Rivalry

**Mechanistic Origin**: Generated by positional comparison within social groups; covers status, social circles, prestige, discourse power and international standing. 

**Core Properties**: Infinite, zero-sum, involutive and inherently exclusive. The metric is not self-satisfaction but relative position; another's advancement devalues one's own, and the competition has no end. 

**Civilizational Role**: The **aesthetic aspiration** of civilization, confined to the public-evaluation track and strictly barred from the redemption of material resources.

### II. The Core Pathogen of Civilization: Structural Mixture of the Two Tracks

The single underlying mechanism of millennia of civilizational involution is the forced binding and mismatch of two tracks.

- **Material-Necessity Track (L0/L1)**: finite, inclusive, positive increment
- **Identity-Recognition Track (L2)**: infinite, zero-sum, exclusive rivalry

The old order forces these two tracks of diametrically opposed properties into one monetary, economic and resource metric. The physically inevitable consequence follows: unbounded zero-sum L2 status rivalry continuously siphons, occupies and depletes the finite L0 survival-resource pool.

This is neither a moral defect, an institutional loophole nor a policy failure; it is the necessary outcome of mechanistic mismatch.

Even if the AI era eliminates material scarcity and satisfies L0 abundance for all, involution will never cease so long as the tracks remain unseparated. Once material scarcity dissolves, social energy floods into zero-sum L2 positional rivalry, producing a rigid pattern of universal label-chasing and stagnant innovation, of which the European model is the archetypal evidence.

### III. The Global Dual Architecture: The Natural-Property Dynamic of Sovereignty and Capital

Modern global civilization comprises two core systems. When each fulfills its own function, civilization remains stable; when their boundaries are breached, civilization tears.

#### 1. State-Sovereignty System: Guarding Boundaries Toward Goodness (The Shield of Civilization)

**Core Properties**: public, inclusive, bounded, fairness-oriented. 

**Core Function**: safeguard universal L0 survival-needs, define baseline order, curb unregulated predation, sustain social stability. 

**Value Correspondence**: S58 baseline-welfare axiom; carries the Goodness baseline of civilization.

#### 2. Global-Capital System: Seeking Truth Through Efficiency (The Instrument of Civilization)

**Core Properties**: mobile, innovative, profit-seeking, efficiency-oriented. 

**Core Function**: expand technological boundaries, iterate productivity, enrich material supply, drive social increment. 

**Value Correspondence**: the human instinct for exploration and innovation; carries the Truth-seeking momentum of civilization.

**Stable Ideal Order**: sovereignty defends the baseline, capital creates increment, Truth and Goodness complement each other, boundaries remain clear, and the two co-evolve symbiotically.

**Misaligned Pathology of the Old Order**: capital crosses public boundaries and alienates into monopoly and rent-seeking, placing private gain above public justice; sovereignty falls into reactive involution, raising barriers to resist predation, trading collaboration for livelihood security.

### IV. The High-Order Global Pathology: State-Level Alienation of the L2 Identity Economy

Individual-level L2 involution escalates into state-level positional rivalry, the most insidious global disease. 

**Mechanism**: international standing, civilizational circles, hegemonic prestige and historical ranking belong to the public L2 evaluation track. 

**Old-Order Loophole**: national identity privilege and civilizational positional hegemony are permitted to exchange directly for tangible material resources, technological privileges and trade priority. 

**Derived Consequence**: without equivalent production or public-value contribution, historically acquired high-status positions can permanently extract global dividends and squeeze the L0 survival-and-development space of late-developing nations.

This is more hidden, more stubborn and more durable than capital overreach: state-level zero-sum status rivalry preys upon the universal survival resources of all humanity.

### V. The Four-Fold Systemic Imbalance: The Collapse Trend of the Old Order

Capital overreach, sovereign involution, identity alienation and civilizational dissipation jointly erode the operating efficiency of modern civilization.

- **Capital-Overreach Imbalance**: trans-national capital monopolizes technology, extracts resources and privatizes collective innovation; the Truth-seeking instrument abandons the Goodness baseline and compresses global inclusive space.
- **Identity-Alienation Imbalance**: hegemonic nations monetize L2 status privilege, extracting the development rights of late-developing nations through positional advantage; normalized zero-sum rivalry produces severe global inequity.
- **Sovereign-Involution Imbalance**: nations raise technological barriers and shut down collaboration to defend against capital predation and status suppression; global Truth-seeking collaboration fractures.
- **Civilizational-Dissipation Imbalance**: the L0 necessities of ordinary people are persistently squeezed; elites and powerful states profit from privilege labels rather than public contribution, dwelling in suspicion, defence and rivalry, deprived of dignity and enduring peace.

### VI. The Destiny of the Three Major Economic Models (Judged Against the Human-Nature Axioms)

Against the mechanisms of track-mixture and L2 zero-sum rivalry, existing economic models receive their qualitative verdict.

- **American Monopoly Economy**: the dual alienation of capital overreach and national-status hegemony; private gain crushes public justice, Truth abandons Goodness, and zero-sum rivalry dominates. Weak sustainability; destined for decline.
- **European Identity Economy**: society is locked into the L2 identity track, absorbed in class labels and positional comparison; zero-sum dissipation suppresses innovation. Stagnant and rigid; incapable of advancement.
- **Chinese Inclusive-Welfare Economy**: upholds the sovereign baseline of Goodness, guarantees universal L0 welfare, accommodates L1 innovative development and restrains identity alienation. Consistent with the human-nature axioms and aligned with the direction of civilizational evolution.

Any order that permits symbolic-status tracks to interfere with material allocation and lets zero-sum rivalry dominate civilization will, in all likelihood, be eliminated as human civilization evolves.

## Part II. The Civilizational Breakthrough: Track Separation and Positive-Sum Reorientation, Building a New Human-Centric Symbiotic World Order

### I. The Underlying Failure of Old Remedial Paths

Past reform schemes never touched the root pathology of track-mixture and L2 zero-sum alienation, and therefore remain superficial cures.

- **Radical-Confrontation Path**: pursues justice through struggle, but destroys L1 innovative productivity, tears the social fabric and forfeits the Truth-seeking foundation.
- **Welfare-Patch Path**: stabilizes society through fiscal transfers, but only mitigates surface inequality; it cannot separate the identity track or end zero-sum dissipation, so the source of alienation persists.
- **Neo-Liberal Laissez-Faire Path**: allows unconstrained capital expansion and status-privilege redemption, amplifying the dual-track mismatch and accelerating fracture and class solidification.

The common flaw of old thinking: it adjusts distribution outcomes without reforming track mechanisms, and treats surface conflicts without curing human-nature alienation, trapping society in cycles of zero-sum games.

### II. The Core Breakthrough: Centring Human Agency and Re-Establishing the Rules of Three Tracks

The core cognition of a human-centric civilization: the human being is the subject of civilization; nations, capital, institutions, wealth and status are all instruments serving humanity. Building on the A11, S57 and S58 axioms, the operating rules of three needs, three tracks, each with its own boundary and no conflation, are established to weaken the risk of zero-sum alienation.

#### 1. L0 Survival Track: Goodness as the Baseline, Universal Inclusion (Rigid Baseline)

**Operating Rule**: collaborative global sovereign frameworks guarantee a universal baseline; the subsistence and fundamental development rights of all peoples are a common human bottom line, independent of status, class, national standing or capital power. 

**Implementation**: strictly implement the S58 baseline-welfare axiom, reduce the encroachment of status hegemony and capital monopoly upon survival-critical resources, and uphold the Goodness of civilization.

#### 2. L1 Development Track: Truth as Progress, Stratification by Endowment (Reasonable Flexibility)

**Operating Rule**: capital returns to its neutral-instrument essence, focusing on technological innovation, productivity iteration and material abundance. **Implementation**: allow individuals and nations to earn differentiated developmental returns through endowment, innovation and positive contribution; preserve the Truth-seeking vitality of civilization and achieve orderly stratification and benign competition.

#### 3. L2 Recognition Track: Beauty as Symbiosis, Positive-Sum Reorientation (Core Innovation)

**Operating Rule**: sever the redemption channels between L2 identity labels and material resources, economic privilege or development rights, and redirect unbounded zero-sum positional rivalry toward positive-sum co-creation grounded in public contribution. 

**Old L2**: zero-sum rivalry, advancement atop others' losses, existence within shadows; superiority built on others' disadvantage, victory breeds enemies and brings no peace. 

**New L2**: positive-sum symbiosis, prestige earned through altruistic contribution, dignified existence under sunlight; honour derives from advancing public welfare and civilizational progress, flourishing while lifting others, achieving unconflicted dignity. 

**Implementation**: honour and reputation rest upon communal consensus, public appraisal and civilizational contribution, decoupled from material privilege, resource monopoly and positional predation, thereby eradicating identity-driven economic alienation.

### III. The New Global Dual Arrangement: Sovereignty as Root, Capital as Instrument, Track Separation, Tri-Partite Symbiosis

The coming new order neither abolishes sovereignty, dismantles capital, nor denies humanity's innate need for recognition. It only redraws boundaries, recalibrates priorities, separates tracks and transforms the properties of the game.

#### 1. Sovereignty System: Upholding Goodness as the Shield of Civilization

**Core Responsibility**: build collaborative global sovereign frameworks, entrench universal L0 baselines, unify capital-regulation rules, block channels converting identity privilege into tangible benefits, and curb state-level zero-sum predation. 

**Value Criterion**: the common welfare of all humanity.

#### 2. Capital System: Seeking Truth as the Instrument of Civilization

**Core Responsibility**: strip away the alienated properties of monopoly, overreach and predation, and preserve the positive values of innovation, efficiency and iteration. 

**Value Criterion**: direct technological advancement and wealth accumulation toward universal prosperity and civilizational elevation.

#### 3. Identity System: Returning to Beauty as the Soul of Civilization

**Core Responsibility**: individual prestige and national honour are earned exclusively through public contribution, inclusive value and collaborative dedication. 

**Value Criterion**: escape zero-sum comparison and shadow-bound rivalry, and achieve full-dimensional symbiosis under sunlight.

Sovereignty upholds Goodness, capital advances Truth, identity aspires to Beauty. The triad of Truth, Goodness and Beauty achieves full unification, and human civilization completes a mechanistic-level leap.

### IV. The Value of the New Order: Emancipation for All, Win-Win Outcomes, Reduced Dissipation

Rebuilt from the human-nature axioms, this new order emancipates every social agent and relieves long-standing civilizational dissipation.

- **Grassroots populations and late-developing nations**: freed from survival-driven involution and resource predation; L0 necessities secured, with equal-opportunity development and dignified existence.
- **Elites and powerful states**: no longer dependent on monopoly and predatory calculation; freed from suspicion and defensive shadows, they attain dignity and prestige under sunlight through public contribution, achieving both material abundance and inner peace.
- **Human civilization as a whole**: capital overreach, identity dissipation, class fracture and global confrontation are eased; collective energy shifts from zero-sum comparison to positive-sum creation, opening a perpetually ascending civilizational cycle.
