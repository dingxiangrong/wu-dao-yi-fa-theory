# S25. 本体与显象 1.0

**Title**: S25. Noumenon and Manifestation 1.0

归属：五道一法｜识部 See (S)｜向外看世界第五篇

万物之本为变。
The essence of all things is Flux.

变依托隙而行，构循轨而定；凡本体之动，皆发生于容变之隙。
Flux moves upon the Gap, and Construct follows its orbit; all motions of the Noumenon unfold within the Gap of capacity.

装，是时序上，依托构划定边界，将本体锁定于构内。所谓势能可装，是所装本体以势能这一度量标尺来衡量的表述。
Containment, in temporal sequence, relies on Construct to draw boundaries and enclose the Noumenon within it. To say potential-energy is containable means the enclosed Noumenon is gauged by potential-energy as its metric.

叠加，是同隙之内多类显象的共存；显象只居空间维度，不可作时间上的装存。
Superposition is the coexistence of multiple Manifestations within one Gap; Manifestation belongs only to the spatial dimension and cannot be contained and preserved across time.

“装” 的分判：本体可装；显象不可装。
Distinction of Containment: the Noumenon may be contained; Manifestation cannot be contained.

装，是以构为瓶，于时序上锁定本体，可积累，可漏失；叠加，是以数为笔，于空间上记录显象的表征，是空间快照 —— 一切显象皆然。
Containment takes Construct as its vessel, locking the Noumenon in time, subject to accumulation and leakage. Superposition takes number as its brush, recording the signatures of Manifestation in space; it is a spatial snapshot — true for all Manifestation.

本体之中，构即是瓶。构落定，边界成型，势能之量便可收纳约束于隙内；构一旦消解，瓶破，所装之势能度量随即散逸，复归于隙中迁流。
Within the Noumenon, Construct is the vessel. When Construct settles and boundaries form, the measure of potential-energy may be contained and bounded within the Gap. Once Construct dissolves, the vessel breaks, and the enclosed potential-energy dissipates, flowing back into the Gap.

装与叠加，为工具之基；一切工具皆由此二类派生。装主时序锁定本体，叠加主空间记录显象 —— 敛拓交驰，即是此二类工具往复运作的本体机制。
Containment and Superposition are the foundation of all instruments; all instruments derive from these two archetypes. Containment governs locking the Noumenon in time; Superposition governs recording Manifestation in space. Alternation of Contraction and Expansion is the noumenal mechanism by which these two instruments operate reciprocally.

人易混淆此二者：把显象的空间叠加，误认为本体在时间上的装存。力是构与变互动的空间快照，属显象，非本体之量，故不可装，一切显象皆然。
This is the common confusion: mistaking the spatial superposition of Manifestation for the temporal containment of the Noumenon. Force is a spatial snapshot arising from the interplay of Construct and Flux. It is Manifestation, not a measure of Noumenon, and thus cannot be contained. This holds for all Manifestation.

诸般显象，皆为空间快照，为隙内叠加，不能封装寄存。快照仅捕捉当下空间层面的叠加态；一帧帧快照依次排布，方在时序上显出序列；序列是记录，不是显象。
All Manifestation are spatial snapshots, superposed within the Gap, incapable of being sealed and stored. A snapshot captures only the superposed state in space. Successive snapshots, ordered one after another, form a sequence in time. A sequence is a record, not Manifestation.

唯有本体可被构之瓶所装；势能，是丈量所装本体的度量标尺。显象纵然连缀成序列，亦无瓶可盛。
Only the Noumenon may be contained within the vessel of Construct. Potential-energy is the metric by which the enclosed Noumenon is measured. Even if Manifestation are strung into a sequence, there exists no vessel to hold them.

辨本体与显象，首在守住这一条：
To distinguish Noumenon and Manifestation, this rule must be upheld foremost:
**世间可装者，唯本体；一切空间快照之显象，无瓶可盛。**
**Only the Noumenon is containable in the world; no vessel can hold Manifestation, which are spatial snapshots.**

知事维度可形式化、可量化，属显象叠加；知物、知人、知传边界无限，属本体度量之装。
The dimension of knowing affairs is formalizable and quantifiable, belonging to the superposition of Manifestation. Knowing things, knowing persons, and knowing transmission have unbounded boundaries, belonging to the containment and measurement of the Noumenon.
