## General Preliminaries
Order and disorder are first-order pre-concepts prior to all realities, primordial entities and constructs.

All ontological units are collectively named: reality.

Classification of realities: realities are divided into primordial realities and derivative realities:
1). Primordial realities: Flux (C), Construct (S). The two are parallel co-existing ontological substrates of the world, neither preceding nor deriving from the other, existing innately as the underlying foundation.
2). Derivative realities: Vibration, Propagation, Deliver, Container-Construct.

Revision note: Field and Force are not ontological realities; both belong to relational descriptive tools. Macro Inter-Construct State is a descriptive concept for system emergence.

Order and disorder are not equivalent to thermodynamic entropy or entropy-increase. They describe constraint states of evolutionary paths of realities.
- Ordered: At any ontological level, evolution is constrained by higher-order rules; evolutionary paths converge within rule-defined bounds.
- Disordered: At any ontological level, there is no higher-order rule constraint; evolutionary paths are unbounded and open-ended.

## Part I: Primordial Ontological Core Terms (Permanently Locked - V1.7)
### 1. Flux
**English Name**: Flux

**Exclusive Abbreviation**: C

**Definition**: 

As the primordial source, Flux is perpetually in motion. Static Flux or purely static Flux cannot exist. Its essence is ceaseless becoming, the underlying substrate for all dynamical change in the world and the ultimate origin of all motion and structure.

**Note**: 
> Deprecate former naming conventions; includes conductable and non-conductable modes.

Formal mathematical form: Flux is a time-dependent complex ontological entity.

$C(t)=M(t)+iE(t)$.

$M(t)$: real-part projection (Mass, function of time),

$E(t)$: imaginary-part projection (Energy, function of time).

$C(t)$, $M(t)$ and $E(t)$ adopt Yun uniformly as the unit of measurement.

### 2. Construct
**English Name**: Construct

**Exclusive Abbreviation**: S

**Definition**: 

A Construct is a structure possessing locking-retention capacity. In formal mathematical representation, a Construct can be treated as a constraint function with critical domain boundaries. Inputs are structural parameters of one or more entities; output is a numerical value representing this construct¡¯s capacity to lock-retain Flux.

The function has strict critical boundaries: within boundary intervals, configuration remains stable and locking-retention is valid. Once parameters exceed critical thresholds, locking-retention fails, configuration collapses and the function domain is truncated and invalidated.

A Construct represents any configuration capable of locking-retaining primordial Flux $C(t)$. It acts as a carrier that constrains, contains and stabilizes primordial Flux states, enabling Flux to transition from disordered primordial state into ordered concrete manifestation.

**Illustrative Example: Bamboo-Dragonfly Model**

Within a reasonable rotational-speed range, the bamboo-dragonfly remains structurally intact and stably locks-retains Flux states, sustaining its configuration and motion. If driven beyond the critical structural boundary, Flux load exceeds locking-retention capacity, the bamboo-dragonfly fractures and disintegrates. This intuitively demonstrates that construct functions are bounded and collapse upon threshold violation.

### 3. Mass
**English Name**: Mass

**Exclusive Abbreviation**: M

**Definition Status**: 

Real-part projection of complex Flux $C(t)$, represented by time-dependent function $M(t)$.

Boundary Note: A flux component locked and stabilized by constructs, which cannot be transferred across constructs. Its unit of measurement is Yun.

### 4. Energy
**English Name**: Energy

**Exclusive Abbreviation**: E

**Ontological Status**: 

Imaginary-part projection of complex Flux $C(t)$, represented by time-dependent function $E(t)$.

**Boundary note**:
 
A flux component that dynamically circulates and can be conducted and transferred across constructs. Its unit of measurement is Yun.

**Key attribute note:**
- Energy is the only ontological projection admitting differentiability. Differentiation applies solely to transmission paths and rate-of-change of Flux across constructs, manifesting as Flux transfer rate per unit time.
- Differentiation must not be applied to isolated constructs or static Flux decoupled from inter-construct transmission. Differential operations are only legitimate within dynamical descriptions of Energy transfer across constructs.

### 5. Yun
**English Name**: Yun (system-specific proper noun, untranslated)

**Definition**: 

Yun is the unified metrological reference (unit-of-measure) of this system. It is neither an ontological reality nor a variable or function. The time-dependent complex Flux $C(t)$, its real-part projection $M(t)$ and imaginary-part projection $E(t)$ all adopt Yun as the unified measuring unit.

**Note**:
> Yun must never be used as an independent variable in constructing differential equations; Yun serves purely as a metrological scale.
>
> Ontological status: metrological descriptive reference, not a primordial ontological reality.

## Part II: Fixed Derivative-Reality Terms
### 6. Vibration
**English Name**: Local Vibration

**Definition**: 

Vibration is a derivative reality, a special kind of construct. When two constructs couple into a larger composite construct and sustain steady reciprocal oscillation, Vibration arises. As an objective derivative reality, its dynamics externalize inter-construct relations. The Field (Dual-Relation Descriptor) only mathematically describes such relations; it does not constitute, generate or drive Vibration.

### 7. Propagation
**English Name**: Propagation

**Definition**: 

Propagation is a derivative reality, a special kind of construct. Propagation occurs when a construct-entity such as Vibration undergoes holistic spatial displacement and travel.

### 8. Deliver
**English Name**: Deliver

**Definition**: 

Deliver is a derivative reality, a special kind of construct. Deliver describes cross-construct transfer: a construct stays in-situ without bodily relocation, yet passes its hosted Flux onto another independent construct.

### 9. Container-Construct
**English Name**: Container Construct

**Definition**: 

A Container-Construct is a special ordered construct complying with all underlying axioms of Construct (S). Unlike ordinary multi-body constructs which bind and merge into single high-order composite entities via two-body, three-body or multi-body coupling, a Container-Construct will not fuse with multiple independent constructs into one giant unified construct. Instead it establishes one-to-one steady locking-retention dual relations with each independent construct in parallel. These multiple dual-relation sets coexist without merging, annihilating or overriding one another.

### 10. Macro Inter-Construct State
**English Name**: Macro Inter-Structure State

**Definition**: 

Numerous independent constructs each maintain steady one-to-one locking-retention dual relations with a Container-Construct to form the system¡¯s stable substrate. Meanwhile independent constructs engage transient non-binding reciprocal interactions without structural fusion or new-construct generation. The holistic stable systemic state emerging from superposition of steady pairwise dual-relations and interleaved transient internal interactions is defined as Macro Inter-Construct State.

## Part III: Descriptive Tools (Non-Reality)
### 11. Field
**English Name**: Dual-Relation Descriptor

**Definition**: 

The Field is a descriptive tool and not an objective ontological reality. It describes and characterizes stable dual interaction relations among constructs.

### 12. Force
**English Name**: Force

**Definition**: 

Force is a descriptive tool and not an objective ontological reality. It characterizes tendency relations between constructs and falls within the descriptive framework of the Field. This system does not re-define units or symbols for Force; compatibility with established modern-physics conventions is maintained.

- **Force does not generate, consume or transform Flux ($C$). It only describes magnitude and direction of inter-construct tendencies.**
