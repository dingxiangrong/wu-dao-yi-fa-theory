### English Description

This collection contains the complete manuscripts of *The Boundary-Expanding Method*, including the master outline A60 and six main treatises A61–A66. It forms an autonomous analytical system for boundary evolution. The core inquiry of this system: how an agent can controllably expand its existing boundary, consolidate and restructure internal systems, and rebalance internal potential energy.

Three fundamental propositions, two primitive paths (cognitive outward expansion, internal structural reconstruction), and three states of boundary motion (forward expansion, steady boundary maintenance, backward boundary collapse) are defined. The action sequence follows purpose-setting, direction-holding, rule-formulating and procedure-implementing. Boundary expansion is graded into short-range, medium-range and long-range tiers. It specifies risk categories including allied-substructure risk, cognitive boundary risk, purpose-right risk, discourse-field risk and resource constraint risk, with the flexible truncation principle as the termination criterion.

Included manuscripts:
A60 The Boundary-Expanding Method — General Outline (top-level meta-document, terminology benchmark, logical anchor)
A61 Treatise 1: Four Disciplines (Charter of boundary-expanding action, with a preface, four core theoretical subtopics A61-1 to A61-4 and A61-5 Case Analysis, sharing the same DOI as the main treatise)
A62 Treatise 2: Universal Mathematical Model of Evolution; A62-1 Measurement, Organization and Assessment
A63 Treatise 3: Dual Theory of Scientific Paradigm and Engineering Paradigm
A64 Treatise 4: Path of Human Cognition
A65 Treatise 5: Five-Level Progressive Cognitive Methodology of Point-Hand
A66 Treatise 6: Four-Unit Unified Paradigm of Human Activities

Closed logical chain: A60 → A61 → A62 → A63 → A64 → A65 → A66.
This autonomous V1.2 edition does not attach to external ontology or elaborate on the Five-Tao interpretation; the axiomatic framework is built exclusively around boundary-expanding.
