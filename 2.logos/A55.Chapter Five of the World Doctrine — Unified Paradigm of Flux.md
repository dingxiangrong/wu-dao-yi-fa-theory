It breaks the fragmented understanding of matter, motion, field, energy and information in classical physics. Taking “all reality is fluxional change” as the core ontological axiom, it realizes unified modeling for the fundamental existential forms, structural rules and action mechanisms of the universe. This paradigm redefines the underlying logical hierarchy of the universe, strictly distinguishing six core dimensions: ontology, structure, form, field domain, measurement and information. It eliminates semantic ambiguity and logical loopholes in traditional physical concepts, forming a brand-new theoretical system covering natural philosophy and fundamental physics.

Research indicates that there are no absolutely static rigid entities in all cosmic reality. Matter and entities within human cognitive scope are essentially steady-state flux with self-restraint, locking and conservation. All physical phenomena originate from internal alternation, external coupling, spatial migration and outward spillover transmission of flux.

All flux follows the dual-phase configuration of kinetic-potential phase ($A(\theta)$-$B(\theta)$). Orthogonal and non-orthogonal phase couplings are intrinsic structural attributes of flux, independently cross-adapting with the two extrinsic forms: steady-state flux and circulating flux.

There exist only two field domains in the universe: mechanical field and spatial field. The mechanical field forms a network channel through gravitational coupling among flux entities, unifying and subsuming gravitational field. It explains the underlying mechanisms of medium transmission, mechanical interaction and heat generation by flux dissipation. The spatial field is the primordial background flux of the universe. It supports vacuum circulating transmission without medium and closes the theoretical loop for electromagnetic phenomena and light radiation.

This paper establishes an independent flux measurement system, including energy measurement to calibrate the scale and intensity of flux action, and phase-structure measurement to investigate the intrinsic morphology of $A(\theta)$-$B(\theta)$ dual-phase configuration. At present, only conceptual definition is completed for phase-structure measurement; concrete quantitative schemes are deferred to subsequent quantitative chapters. This paper clarifies the essential distinction between “flux ontology” and “quantitative measurement”. Meanwhile, it identifies flux as the sole carrier of information. Information generates and transmits relying on configuration, phase and coupling differences of flux, completing the three-level division of ontology-measurement-content.

This paradigm can coherently interpret classical physical phenomena including object steady-state persistence, macroscopic displacement, medium wave propagation, heat transfer and temperature rise by acoustic dissipation. It fundamentally resolves the ambiguity of the traditional concept “motion”, and builds a logically closed underlying physical system without loopholes. This research provides a brand-new ontological framework and axiom system for fundamental physics paradigm reconstruction, and offers theoretical support for the unification of mechanics, thermology and wave-field theory.

## Ⅰ. Core Ontological Axiom (Ultimate First Principle)

All reality is essentially change.

There are no absolutely static, rigid entities in the universe. What human cognition regards as matter, mass, object or carrier are not “unchanging being”, but highly-converged, self-locking, long-term stable steady-state flux structures.

Simplest ontological dichotomy:
- Entity = inward-locked, self-conserved steady-state flux
- Phenomenon = internal alternation, external coupling, spatial migration and outward transmission of flux

## Ⅱ. Fundamental Dual-Phase Structure of Flux (Universal Underlying Configuration for All Beings)

All flux, macroscopic or microscopic, entity or wave, obeys one universal dual-phase structure:
- **Kinetic Phase**: kinetic-oriented, outward-directed flux
- **Potential Phase**: field-energy-oriented, inward-restrained flux
- **Evolutionary Phase**: progress, rhythm and state of flux evolution

All existence, motion, wave and field state stem from opening-closing, waxing-waning, coupling and transformation of these two phases.

### 2.1 Two Coupling Modes of Dual-Phase Structure (Structural Attribute, Not Morphological Classification)

Orthogonality and non-orthogonality only describe internal phase relations between $A(\theta)$ and $B(\theta)$. They belong to intrinsic structural features of flux, and do not determine whether a flux is steady-state or circulating. Structural attributes and extrinsic forms realize cross-adaptation.

(1) **Orthogonal Phase Structure (Regular Symmetric Coupling)**
Paradigm example: ideal pendulum in vacuum
Kinetic phase and potential phase are phase-orthogonal, alternating symmetrically with strict trade-off. Total quantity is conserved, phase structure is regular, and cyclic evolution is highly ordered. In this example, flux is locked inside the system, performing only self-circulation without outward propagation.

(2) **Non-Orthogonal Phase Structure (Recessive Inward-Restrained Coupling)**
Paradigm example: flux of electrons at same energy level in ground-state atom
It also possesses kinetic-potential dual-phase configuration, satisfies conservation conditions and features inward self-sustenance. Without orthogonal phase constraints or harmonic symmetric alternation, it represents a microscopically deep, recessive ordered steady-state structure.

> Key Hierarchical Definition
> - Steady-state / Circulating flux: extrinsic existential forms of flux
> - Orthogonal / Non-orthogonal: intrinsic phase-structure coupling modes of flux
> Two sets of dimensions are mutually independent and can combine freely; hierarchies shall not be confused.

## Ⅲ. Two Top-Level Forms of Flux (Unique Cosmic Dichotomy)

There are exclusively two categories for all cosmic flux, no third category exists:
**Steady-State Flux (Restrained-Change); Circulating Flux (Transmission-Change)**

### 3.1 Steady-State Flux (Restrained-Change · Origin of All Entities)

Flux energy is inward-restrained, self-locked and conserved. It circulates mainly inside the system and does not actively depart from its origin for long-range propagation.

All macroscopic objects and microscopic ground-state particles are ultra-stable, low-dissipation deep flux structures that barely diffuse outward.

A steady-state flux may carry either orthogonal or non-orthogonal phase structure.

### 3.2 Circulating Flux (Transmission-Change · Origin of All Waves)

Disturbed by external coupling, partial flux detaches and spills over from steady-state ontology, acquiring the property to propagate independently away from its origin. This is circulation, namely wave.

Circulation is spillover, outward extension of flux, rather than new substance independent of flux itself.

Circulating flux can also possess orthogonal or non-orthogonal phase structure.

## Ⅳ. Flux Transformation Rules (Underlying Mechanism for Generation and Vanishing of Motion-Rest)

Without external coupling, any steady-state flux maintains self-circulation and long-term stability.

Once intervened by external interaction:
- Original steady-state ontology (retained substrate)
- New-born flux generated by coupling disturbance

The steady-state lock breaks, triggering three types of variations: internal phase alternation, spatial migration and spillover circulation.

### 4.1 Precise Concept Revision (Eliminate Ambiguity of “Motion”)

Abandon vague definition: delete “motion = flux interconversion”.

Revised normalized definitions:
- **Internal Operation**: spontaneous wax-wane alternation between $A(\theta)$ / $B(\theta)$ phases inside steady-state flux
- **Macroscopic Displacement**: spatial position migration of the whole steady-state flux structure within mechanical-field coupling network
- **Wave Circulation**: flux detaches from origin and propagates independently in space

## Ⅴ. Dual-Field Substratum of the Universe (Unique Dichotomy for All Field Systems)

Only two field substrata carry flux in the universe; no third field exists.

### 5.1 Mechanical Field

Mechanical field is a network structure formed by gravitational coupling among flux entities.

It is the explicit network of “flux pulling flux”. It serves as the exclusive physical channel for mutual locking, transmission, transformation and coupling between steady-state entities, or between steady-state and circulating flux.

- Macroscopic objects and mesoscopic particles are constrained homogeneously by mechanical field
- Gravitational field does not exist independently; it is merely the macroscopic manifestation of gravitational-coupling network formed by massive steady-state flux
- Classification: intrinsic mechanical field (intra-particle coupling network); extrinsic mechanical field (medium-propagation coupling network)
- Properties: substance-dependent, dissipative, transmitting by relay of medium nodes

Nature of dissipation: when ordered flux passes through gravitational network, it is torn by medium nodes and decomposed into disordered sub-flux of particles (generating heat).

### 5.2 Spatial Field (Primordial Background Field of Universe)

Space is not a geometric void. Space itself is underlying background flux field.

It is independent of substance, medium or particle coupling.

Electromagnetism, photon, light radiation and vacuum wave propagation are background circulation within spatial field. Only spatial field supports cross-vacuum transmission.

> Fluxes couple to form mechanics; decoupled fluxes propagate as space.

## Ⅵ. Measurement of Flux (Independent Core Chapter)

This chapter addresses how to characterize and quantify “change”. Energy shall not be equated directly with flux ontology.

### 6.1 Measurement Axiom

Flux is ontological reality; measurement is the human-made system of criteria to characterize flux’s intensity, scale, evolution progress and state.

Ontology is not equivalent to measurement, yet all flux is measurable.

### 6.2 Core Measurement Items

- **Energy Measurement**: quantifies action scale, accumulation and release intensity of flux;
- **Phase-Structure Measurement**: quantifies coupling patterns (orthogonal / non-orthogonal) of $A(\theta)$-$B(\theta)$. Presently only conceptual definition is accomplished; quantitative schemes are deferred to Chapter A56;
- **Phase**: quantifies evolutionary progress and rhythm of flux;
- **Dissipation Degree**: quantifies the extent of flux evolving from order to disorder.

### 6.3 Correspondence between Measurement and Flux Forms

- Steady-state flux: energy accumulates, phase evolves in internal circulation;
- Circulating flux: energy migrates outward along with flux, phase advances along propagation direction;
- Dissipation process: ordered configuration collapses and dissipation degree rises.

## Ⅶ. Relation between Flux and Information

1. Flux is the carrier of information.
Without flux, no medium exists to bear information; information cannot exist independently out of flux.

2. Information resides in configuration, phase rhythm and coupling differences of flux. Generation, bearing and migration of information are realized via variation and transmission of flux.

3. Flux on different fields carry different categories of information:
    - Flux on mechanical field carry vibration and acoustic information;
    - Circulating flux on spatial field carry optical and electromagnetic information.

> Note: Energy belongs to measurement dimension of flux; information belongs to content dimension borne by flux. Ontology corresponds to flux; measurement corresponds to energy, phase-structure etc.; borne content corresponds to information. Three hierarchies must be strictly distinguished.

## Ⅷ. Axiom on Nature of Heat (Final State of Flux Dissipation)

Heat is neither independent substance nor simple temperature appearance.

Heat = observable manifestation of disordered sub-flux of mesoscopic particles inside intrinsic mechanical field of matter.

Two transmission paths of heat:
- **Mechanical-field heat transfer (conduction, convection)**: relayed transmission by medium particles, cannot propagate across vacuum
- **Spatial-field heat transfer (thermal radiation)**: excites electromagnetic circulation and enables cross-vacuum transmission.

## Ⅸ. Closed-Loop Deduction with Classical Example: Temperature Rise by Acoustic Dissipation

Inside a closed cavity, sound wave is ordered circulating flux within mechanical field and cannot leak outward.

Continuous input of ordered flux → constantly torn by particle nodes in mechanical-field gravitational network → ordered flux converts into massive disordered mesoscopic sub-flux → thermal effect accumulates and temperature rises.

Dynamic equilibrium: Rate of input flux energy = Rate of energy leakage by spatial-field radiation, temperature remains constant.
