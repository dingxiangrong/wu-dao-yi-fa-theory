## Chapter 2 Primal Substrate and Fundamental Rules

This chapter furnishes the first-order bottom-level foundation for the whole system. It establishes dual-primal ontology, intrinsic constant constraints of Structure-Locks, and universal core axioms, which serve as preconditions for all structures, hierarchies and evolutions.

### 2.1 Dual Primal Source: Primordial Flux-Structure-Lock (System Core)

The bottom-level ontology of the world consists of two co-root realities, mutually serving as substrate; neither precedes nor derives from the other, they are innate realities.

#### 2.1.1 Primal Primordial Flux (C)

Primordial Flux is omnipresent bottom-level ontological reality. Unconstrained raw Primordial Flux has no fixed form, no order and no bounds; it is the source of all dynamics, motions and fluxes. Primordial Flux unconfined by Structure-Lock manifests as disorder.

#### 2.1.2 Order-Primal Structure-Lock (S)

Structure-Lock is the sole ontological carrier for all order, constraints, boundaries and structures. Order is not an independent rule; order is an intrinsic property of Structure-Lock. Where a Structure-Lock exists, order exists. Where a Structure-Lock collapses, order dissolves, Primordial Flux is released and returns to disordered primal flux.

System Thesis:-Primordial Flux supplies ontological dynamics; Structure-Lock supplies ontological order. Primordial Flux and Structure-Lock united give rise to all things.

### 2.2 Intrinsic Constant of Structure-Lock: Lock-in Threshold

All Structure-Locks share one innate quantitative constraint, a universal cosmic constant independent of structural hierarchy, morphology and scale.

#### 2.2.1 Threshold Definition

Every Structure-Lock possesses a fixed Primordial-Flux-bearing threshold. It relies on this threshold to lock-in Primordial Flux and sustain ordered steady-state existence, which is the quantitative boundary-condition for survival of all ordered structures.

#### 2.2.2 Two Threshold Regimes

1. Primordial-Flux-load below threshold: locking remains effective, structure stays stable, order self-sustaining.

2. Primordial-Flux-load exceeds threshold: locking fails, structure collapses, locked-in Primordial Flux is released back to primal disordered state.

Remark: This constant is a universal bottom-level property shared by all Structure-Locks, not exclusive to any particular base unit. S- base-units, composite-, higher-order-, Container-Structure-Locks all obey this identical threshold rule.

### 2.3 Five Universal Core Axioms

Axiom-1: Primordial-Flux Conservation Axiom
Primordial Flux cannot be created ex-nihilo nor destroyed. Collapse of Structure-Locks only alters the constrained form of Primordial Flux; total quantity of Primordial Flux remains constant. Analogous to conservation-of-substance: Primordial Flux is primal invariant quantity. ※Unbounded§ means Primordial Flux acts without spatial bounds; ※conserved§ means its primal magnitude is invariant.

Axiom-2: Over-Load Collapse Axiom
Operates based on the intrinsic Structure-Lock threshold constant. All Structure-Locks are bounded constructs with finite bearing-capacity and cannot accumulate Primordial Flux infinitely. When Primordial-Flux-load surpasses lock-in threshold, locking fails, structure collapses and Primordial Flux returns to disorder.

Axiom-3: Difference Axiom
Perfectly homogeneous, undifferentiated assemblies cannot spontaneously generate order. Difference is a necessary precondition for emergence of order. This ontology only establishes existence-condition; quantitative thresholds belong to the Measurement Division.

Axiom-4: Primary-Secondary Axiom
Ordered systems inevitably develop primary-secondary differentiation. Without hierarchy, checks-and-balances and rank-order, structures lack constraints and steady-state and cannot sustain themselves. Primary-secondary differentiation is core stabilizing-condition for ordered structures. This ontology only establishes existence-condition; quantitative thresholds belong to the Measurement Division.

Axiom-5: Nested-Hierarchy Axiom
Structure-Locks possess natural nested-compatibility. Structure-Locks may enclose other Structure-Locks. Nesting is the sole generative mechanism for all higher-order ontological levels.

Human cognitive-system roots in human-being＊s ontological identity as Subject-Structure-Lock.

### 6.1 Prototype-Unit of Representation: Dual-Relation

2. Directions-of-Study
Against the three derivative forms arising from dual-coupling 〞 Local Vibration (V) and Propagation (P) which are directly generated by dual-relations, plus Deliver (D) whose generation-mechanism has two categories: Dual-Relation Descriptor-effect-loss-origin (triggered by present dual-relation) and decay-origin (from intrinsic Structure-Lock operation) 〞 Dual-Relation Descriptor studies all three forms.

- Local Vibration-(V): local reciprocating oscillation, Structure-Lock-entity without overall spatial-translation; dual-balancing-forces manifest chiefly through Local Vibration;
- Propagation-(P): bodily movement-travel of Structure-Lock-entity inside spatial-domain;
- Deliver-(D): Structure-Lock-entity stays in-situ; only cross-spatial hand-over of Primordial-Flux-and-Yun-quantities takes-place.

First, intra-form transformation e.g. change of oscillation-amplitude-mode of Local Vibration itself.
Second, cross-form transformation: Yun-flux mutually circulates-converts-among Local Vibration, Propagation, Deliver.

As representational-tool, Dual-Relation Descriptor depicts: respective-states-of-two-Structure-Locks, spatial-variation, transform-relations-between-them, dual-locking-state, states-of-V-P-D, intra-form-evolution and cross-form Yun-flux-conversion. 3. Participation-Conditions
Only two independent Structure-Locks participate-in-dual-relation; neither annexes-the-other; both sustain their intrinsic locking-state. Each Structure-Lock must remain-within-critical-boundary-domain to keep locking-effective-and-configuration-stable.
