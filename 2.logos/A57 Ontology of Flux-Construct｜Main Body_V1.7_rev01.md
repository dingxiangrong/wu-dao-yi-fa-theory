## Chapter 1 Primordial Substrates and Underlying Rules
This chapter constitutes the first-order underlying foundation of the whole system, establishing dual-primordial ontology, construct-primordial-constant constraints and universal core axioms — prerequisites for all structures, hierarchies and evolutions.

### 1.1 Dual Primordia: Flux-Construct (Core of System)
The underlying primordia of the world consist of two ontological realities, co-existing and mutually grounding one another; neither precedes nor derives from the other, existing innately as realities.

#### 1.1.1 Primordial Flux (C)
Flux is the universally persistent underlying ontological reality. Innately unconstrained, formless, disordered and unbounded, it is the source of all dynamics, motions and fluxes. Flux unconfined by Construct manifests as disordered state.

#### 1.1.2 Order-Primordial Construct (S)
Construct is the sole ontological carrier of all order, constraints, boundaries and structures. Order is not an independent rule but an intrinsic property of Construct. Where Construct exists, order exists; where Construct collapses, order dissolves and Flux is released back into primordial disordered state.

System core proposition: Flux furnishes ontological dynamics; Construct furnishes ontological order. Flux and Construct unite to give rise to all beings.

### 1.2 Primordial Constant of Construct: Locking Threshold
All constructs share an innate quantitative primordial constant, a universal constant invariant across structural hierarchy, morphology and scale.

#### 1.2.1 Threshold Definition
Every construct possesses a fixed Flux-bearing threshold. By this threshold Construct locks Flux and sustains steady order, serving as quantitative boundary condition for the persistence of all ordered structures.

#### 1.2.2 Binary States of Threshold
1. Flux-load below threshold: Construct locking remains valid; structure stable, order self-sustaining.
2. Flux-load exceeds threshold: Construct locking fails; structure collapses, locked Flux is released and returns to primordial disordered state.

Remark: This constant is a universal underlying property for all constructs and is not exclusive to any single elementary entity. $S_0$-primitives, composite constructs, higher-order constructs and Container-Constructs all obey this identical primordial-threshold rule.

### 1.3 Five Universal Core Axioms
**Axiom-1: Flux-Conservation Axiom**

Flux cannot be created ex nihilo nor annihilated ex nihilo. Construct-collapse only modifies the constrained form of Flux; total quantity of Flux remains constant. Analogous to conservation-of-matter: Flux is a primordial constant. “Unbounded” means Flux acts without spatial bounds; “conservation” means the primordial quantity of Flux is invariant.

**Axiom-2: Overrun-Collapse Axiom**

Operates relying on the primordial threshold constant of Construct. All constructs are bounded structures with finite bearing capacity and cannot accumulate Flux without limit. Once Flux-load surpasses locking threshold, construct-locking fails, structure collapses and Flux returns to disordered state.

**Axiom-3: Difference Axiom**

A completely homogeneous, undifferentiated set of units cannot spontaneously generate order. Difference is a necessary precondition for emergence of order. This axiom only establishes existential condition; quantitative thresholds are delegated to verification division.

**Axiom-4: Primary-Secondary Axiom**

Ordered systems inevitably develop primary-secondary differentiation. Without primary-secondary relations, checks-and-balances and hierarchical ordering, structures lack constraints and steady self-sustaining existence is impossible. Primary-secondary differentiation is core condition for ordered-structural stability. This axiom only establishes existential condition; quantitative thresholds are delegated to verification division.

**Axiom-5: Nested-Hierarchy Axiom**

Constructs possess innate nested-compatibility. Constructs can nest within constructs; structures can enclose structures. Nesting constitutes the sole generative mechanism for all higher-order hierarchies.

## Chapter 2 World-Formation and Evolution
Building upon Chapter-1’s primordial-Flux-Construct ontology and underlying axioms, this chapter takes construct-system-evolution as its sole core. It fully deduces the complete formative process of the world: from primitive substrate-constructs, inter-construct interactions, structural coupling-nesting up to the formation of global macroscopic systems.

Flux may reside in disordered chaotic configuration: all Flux follows no unified rules, acts independently, possesses no fixed locked-in structures and no stable local configurations; Flux exists in free-diffusive state. $S_0$, local ordered constructs, emerge as ordered forms against this chaotic background.

### 2.1 Primitive Substrate: $S_0$ Fundamental Construct
Prior to all complex structures and inter-construct interactions, the sole primitive substrate of ordered systems in the world is $S_0$.

#### 2.1.1 Definitional Position
Local ordered-construct formed when multiple portions of primordial Flux follow identical rules and achieve synchronous equivalent locking-in. It is the pre-ordered unit antecedent to all higher-order structures.

Spin-locking is a special case of this configuration and does not define the essence of $S_0$.

**Paradigm Note**:

$S_0$ denotes only the structural form “multiple portions of Flux obeying identical rules”, without specifying concrete rule-content; different independent instances can be generated:
- $S_0$A: fundamental ordered-construct obeying Rule-A
- $S_0$B: fundamental ordered-construct obeying Rule-B

Different $S_0$ populations differ in rules yet share identical structural form; each can independently lock-in and solidify primordial Flux.
Constructs of identical rule may possess positive-negative dual-opposite configurations; when dual-opposite individuals encounter one another, mutual-annihilation is triggered.

#### 2.1.2 Basic Properties of $S_0$
1. $S_0$ is the most elementary unit of ordered systems, indecomposable and with no subordinate sub-structures.
2. $S_0$ is purely construct-ontological and obeys dual-primordial Flux-Construct rules.
3. Possesses formation-threshold (lower-bound threshold): if aggregated primordial Flux fails to reach this threshold, $S_0$ ordered-construct cannot form.
4. Congeneric $S_0$ share uniform fixed bearing-threshold (upper-bound threshold); construct collapses when load exceeds threshold.
5. Iso-frequency convergence tendency: several con-specific $S_0$ co-existing under coupling of identical rules will spontaneously tend toward identical states.
6. Self-consistent under single rule, locally bounded, self-sustaining structure.
7. Diverse convergent-rules give rise to multiple populations of fundamental-construct units.
8. Homogeneity within population, heterogeneity across populations, furnishing innate preconditions for subsequent differential inter-construct interactions.

### 2.2 Inter-Construct Interactions
All hierarchies, complex structures and macroscopic morphologies in the world originate not from elementary entities themselves but exclusively from interactions between constructs. Constructs are steady ontological beings; inter-construct-interaction is the sole driving force of evolution. Field is used to characterize this set of inter-construct-interactions and dual-balancing relations.

When arbitrary independent constructs meet, only three fundamental outcomes arise subject to Difference, Primary-Secondary and Nested-Hierarchy axioms.

#### 2.2.1 Mutual-Annihilation
Two constructs with counter-balancing rules and equivalent potential collapse simultaneously. Local order is erased; locked-in Flux is released returning to primordial disordered state.

#### 2.2.2 Assimilation-Absorption
Two constructs of unequal potential: the stronger construct constrains the weaker one. The weaker construct collapses and reconstructs; Flux quantities are re-locked-in and finally merge into a stable construct under single unified rule.

#### 2.2.3 Balanced-Reciprocal Coupling
Constructs of compatible comparable potential do not destroy or absorb each other and establish long-term balanced relations. This constitutes the core pathway for higher-order world-formation and divides into two evolutionary modes.

**Mode-1: Persistent Binding-Coupling (generates higher-order composite-constructs)**
Multiple independent constructs bind persistently, fuse structurally and unify their rules. Following Nested-Hierarchy Axiom, complex-structure sequences are generated step-by-step: fundamental-construct → two-body composite-construct → multi-body higher-order-construct.
Inevitable evolutionary feature: multi-construct fusion necessarily produces differentiation of primary-secondary relations and hierarchies, forming stable check-and-balance structures. Without primary-secondary relations and hierarchies, higher-order-constructs cannot sustain steady existence.

**Mode-2: Transient-Non-Binding Interaction (generates dynamic background substrate)**
Constructs interact transiently, exchange states without fusion and do not generate new constructs. All constructs retain independent ontology; only dynamic-state transmission occurs. Massive constructs continuously interweave transiently to form a global dynamic background sustaining world-evolution.

### 2.3 Special Higher-Order Construct: Formation of Container-Construct System
Characteristic of ordinary higher-order-constructs: multiple constructs bind and fuse into one single monolithic giant-construct.

Container-Construct is a special higher-order-construct fully complying with primordial axioms and threshold-constraints and possessing an independent evolutionary mode:
1. It does not engulf or merge other independent constructs.
2. It establishes one-to-one individual construct-to-construct locking-dual relations with every independent construct.
3. Multiple dual-relation sets co-exist in parallel: each steady, independent and non-interfering.

The emergence of Container-Construct marks a critical breakthrough for world-systems: independent-constructs sustain individual existence meanwhile global-order constraints apply universally. Structured-world evolves from local composite-structures toward global-systemic-architecture.

### 2.4 Mutual Evolution of Construct and Deliver
Construct is the sole ontological being; Deliver is not an independent ontological reality. Deliver is a derivative manifestation of Flux-transmission. Its generative mechanisms fall into two categories: decay and field-mediated loss. Yun serves as measure, not ontological Flux-being, calibrating magnitude of out-flow and carrying accompanying interactive signatures as incidental information.

1. Decay (Deliver generated by ordered construct-operation): carriers may be $S_0$ fundamental-constructs or macroscopic higher-order-constructs nested by multi-layer sub-constructs. When Flux operates internally within construct and decays, losses arise and Flux dissipates outward generating Deliver; primordial-construct undergoes dissipation. Without ordered internal Flux-operation and decay within construct, Deliver of this category cannot emerge.

2. Field-mediated loss: construct is situated in construct-to-construct dual-interaction (characterized by Field). Mutual check-and-balance, coupling and pulling (as characterized by Field) trigger additional locking-loss of construct; such loss out-flows to produce Deliver. Although triggered by Field-characterized interaction, loss originates from construct itself; Field does not directly generate Deliver.

3. Deliver acting upon construct: Deliver carrying out-flow Flux arrives at target-construct and is absorbed. Multiple absorption modes exist; target-construct may retain steady state after absorption. If incoming Flux-load exceeds construct-locking-threshold, structural collapse-reconstruction occurs, fully complying with Overrun-Collapse Axiom.

### 2.5 Final Global Morphology: Formation of Macro-Inter-Construct State
Macro-world steady-state is superposition of two layers, integrating motion-and-rest and sustaining perpetuity.

#### 2.5.1 Steady-Structure Layer
All independent-constructs lock one-to-one with Container-Construct forming global fixed-order framework. Safeguards world-structure against collapse and safeguards order, furnishing ontological steady-state for macroscopic world.

#### 2.5.2 Dynamic-Evolution Layer
Independent-constructs continuously engage in transient interactions; states update and flow dynamically. Construct-ontology remains unchanged, while inter-construct-relations keep evolving.

Final systemic conclusion: Construct constitutes the body of world; interactions constitute world-dynamics. Body persists constant; states evolve. Static construct-system bears order; dynamic inter-construct-interactions drive continuous world-evolution.

### 2.6 Closed Evolutionary Logic of This Chapter

## Chapter 3 Ontology of Cognition and Quaternary-Interaction-Paradigm
### 3.1 Ontological Position of Cognition
Human beings are autonomous steady-Subject-Constructs. As cognitive subjects humans belong to the ontological form of Construct. All human cognitive channels, observational modes and interactive perceptions have Subject-Construct as their subjective terminus. Humans can only interact objectively with Object-Construct and Deliver and cannot cognize the world escaping from the Construct-Deliver system.

Subject-Construct and Object-Construct both belong to Construct yet possess clear primary-secondary distinction: Subject-Construct undertakes active functions of cognition, benchmarking and identification; Object-Construct is the passive target to be observed and cognized.

### 3.2 Four Categories of Objective Interaction Relations
Taking Subject-Construct, Object-Construct and objective Deliver as elementary units, all objective interaction relations in the world strictly fall into four categories, covering all subject-object interactions and dynamic forms.

#### 3.2.1 Subject-Construct — Object-Construct
Subject-Construct occupies cognitive dominant position; Object-Construct occupies observed subordinate position. Subject-Construct perceives, benchmarks, matches and cognizes all steady-structured Object-Constructs in external world. It corresponds to human cognition of structural, existential and ordered ontological properties of world-beings.

#### 3.2.2 Subject-Construct — Deliver
Subject-Construct occupies cognitive dominant position; Deliver as flux-transmission-state occupies subordinate position. Subject-Construct receives, perceives and measures Deliver out-flowing from ordered-operation of external Object-Constructs. It corresponds to human cognition of dynamic, dissipative and transmissive aspects of Flux in world-beings.

#### 3.2.3 Deliver — Object-Construct
Objectively Deliver may actively act upon, modify and reshape Object-Construct; this constitutes independent objective-interaction-relation. Nevertheless Subject-Construct cannot directly perceive it and cannot generate innate cognition. To extract information from this law, man-made-construct intermediary interaction must be invoked to generate secondary-Deliver fed back toward subject; finally it converges back to perceptual pathway “Subject-Construct — Deliver”.

#### 3.2.4 Deliver — Deliver
Objectively Deliver can couple, superpose, counter-balance and resonate with other Deliver-entities; this constitutes independent objective-interaction-relation. This relation likewise cannot be directly perceived by Subject-Construct. To extract information, man-made-construct intermediate detection must be invoked; secondary-Deliver generated from interaction feeds-back and finally converges to perceptual mode “Subject-Construct — Deliver”.

### 3.3 Ontological Limitations of Innate Human Cognition
As autonomous steady-Subject-Construct, human beings generate Deliver spontaneously from ordered internal Flux-operation. Once Deliver flows outward and departs Subject-Construct-ontology, it no longer belongs to subject-being. Simple outward emission of Deliver without return-flow, feedback or closed-loop cannot produce cognitive perception.

Hence human innate cognition possesses only two direct, innate perceptual pathways: “Subject-Construct — Object-Construct” and “Subject-Construct — Deliver”.

Modern science-and-technology artificially build complete interaction-circuits via man-made-devices (man-made-Object-Constructs): devices actively emit Deliver acting upon external Object-Construct or external Deliver; interaction generates secondary-Deliver reflected back toward detector and further transmits into human Subject-Construct for cognition.

Science-and-technology furnishes extrinsic acquired compensation. It does not alter the underlying ontological property “human being is Subject-Construct and cannot self-generate self-returning Deliver”. Compensation has inherent boundaries and limitations and cannot achieve universal breakthrough.

### 3.4 Core Conclusion of Cognitive-Paradigm
Human cognitive-system is rooted in human-being’s ontology as Subject-Construct.

Subject-Construct-Object-Construct, Subject-Construct-Deliver, Deliver-Object-Construct, Deliver-Deliver constitute complete set of four objective-interaction-relations; no fifth possibility exists.

Innate human beings can directly perceive only first-two categories. The latter two are purely objective-interaction-relations inaccessible to direct human perception and must employ man-made-construct as intermediary tools; all finally converge to perceptual-mode of Subject-Construct receiving Deliver.

All innate human cognition and direct perception strictly fall within dual direct-paradigm: “Subject-Construct-Object-Construct, Subject-Construct-Deliver”.

## Chapter 4 Spatial-Domains
### 4.1 Division of Objective Spatial-Domains
Classification of spatial-domains is discrete description from cognitive-modelling perspective. Construct-density and Flux-stock inside objective-domain may distribute continuously; classification boundaries are set by human cognition.

Classification criteria: existence-status of tangible ordered-constructs and primordial Flux (C). Three objective spatial-domains are distinguished.

1. **Category-1: Construct-Bearing Spatial-Domain**
Tangible ordered-constructs exist within domain. Construct-density is introduced as first-order objective metric quantifying stock of tangible ordered-construct per unit domain-extension. Construct-density denotes density of primordial Flux (C), taking continuously variable numerical values. Stellar-cores and rock-bodies represent high construct-density; upper Earth-atmosphere represents low construct-density. Sun, Moon, Earth-atmosphere all belong to Construct-Bearing Spatial-Domain differing merely in construct-density magnitude.
- Domain simultaneously contains tangible-construct-ensemble and underlying disordered primordial Flux-C.
- Construct-density exerts retarding effect upon Propagation; higher construct-density produces stronger retardation.
- Allowed occurrences: Propagation, Primordial-Deliver, Material-Borne-Deliver.

2. **Category-2: Construct-Free Flux-Bearing Vacuum-Domain**
Construct-density =-0; no tangible ordered-constructs exist; yet domain remains filled with disordered primordial Flux-C.
- Absence of tangible-construct-medium prohibits Material-Borne-Deliver propagation.
- No retardation applies to Propagation.
- Allowed occurrences: Propagation, Primordial-Deliver; Material-Borne-Deliver forbidden.

3. **Category-3: Construct-Free Sparse-Flux Domain (Void-Sparse-Flux Domain)**
Construct-density =-0; no tangible ordered-constructs exist. Primordial Flux (C) persists universally and never vanishes because of domain-conditions. Yet its flux-density falls below $S_0$ formation-threshold (lower-bound critical threshold). Flux-density is extremely rarefied and cannot support locking-in, formation and steady-state persistence of any construct.
- Due to absence of constructs inside domain, carriers and locking-subjects are absent; inter-construct-dual-relations cannot establish, hence Field (characterizing inter-construct-relations) has no object to describe. Local-Vibration and Deliver (derivative construct-forms) cannot occur (Propagation also cannot exist for lack of construct-ontology; external constructs may enter and traverse this domain).
- No construct-medium inside domain exerts mechanical retardation upon incoming Propagation of external-constructs.
- Speculative Remark (non-axiomatic open conjecture): If tangible-construct abruptly enters Void-Sparse-Flux-Domain, loss of Flux-environment constraints and coupling may break original construct-locking-balance. Hypothetical risk of singularity-like expansion, disintegration and explosion of construct exists. This remains conjectural deduction and is not established proposition.

Objective reality is Spatial-Domain. The “space” perceived and comprehended in human mind is cognitive-representation generated by Subject-Construct based upon objective Spatial-Domain; the two shall not be conflated.

Objective Spatial-Domain possesses no built-in measuring-scales, no innately readable distance-calibrations and no innate time-calibrations.

Deliver belongs to Flux-action and transmission-relation. Propagation-motion, Deliver-transmission and internal evolutionary rhythm of constructs may all be modulated by environmental-conditions of spatial-domain. Whether displacement-rates and evolutionary-rhythms change under different-domains remains open-question and shall not be pre-judged.

All observational instruments (innate sense-organs or man-made apparatus) carry inherent limitations. Time-reading, velocity-reading and displacement-reading are all outputs of observational-systems.

Observed values of time, velocity and distance are cognitively-constructed measurements and are not equal to intrinsic state of objective Spatial-Domain-in-itself. We possess no a-priori grounds to assert oscillation or decay of certain substances may serve as universally-valid absolute time-baseline for entire universe.

Subject-Construct synthesizes diverse perceptual-information and through internal processing generates distance, scale and orientation in human-mind forming cognitive picture of space.

Differences among observational outputs may originate from modulation exerted by spatial-domain-environment upon Flux, Deliver, Propagation and intrinsic rhythm of observing-construct-itself, rather than deformation intrinsic to Spatial-Domain-in-itself.

“Space-curvature” derived from various-models is output of cognitive-modelling and is not intrinsic-property belonging to objective Spatial-Domain-in-itself.

Constructs distribute within extended substrate of Spatial-Domain. The most elementary unit of inter-construct-interaction-relation is two-body dual-relation. Two independent-constructs co-reside within same spatial-domain, confronting and forming check-and-balance coupling; this constitutes simplest prototype characterized by Field.

Object investigated by Field: changes of two-constructs under spatial-variation, their respective states, plus all transformational-relations between them.

> **Remark**:

Isolated intrinsic locking-state of single-construct also does not belong to investigative-object of this-Field.

2. **Investigative Scope**
For Local-Vibration and Propagation (externalized from dual-coupling), as well as Deliver (two origins: field-mediated-loss originating from this-dual-relation; decay originating from construct-intrinsic-loss), Field investigates all three categories:
correspondence-relations of each form with space, state-variation accompanying spatial-change, plus mutual-transform-relations among Local-Vibration-Propagation-Deliver.

Dual-coupling establishes dual-locking-state accompanied by check-and-balance-interaction and Yun-flux-exchange, externalizing two objective derivative-forms: Local-Vibration and Propagation. - Deliver: construct-ontology stays situated in-situ; only cross-spatial-delivery-transfer of Flux and Yun-flux occurs.

**First**: intra-form transformation, e.g. variation of amplitude and mode of Local-Vibration oscillation itself.


**Second**: cross-form mutual-transformation: Yun-flux may flow and convert reciprocally among Local-Vibration, Propagation and Deliver.

As characterizing-tool Field depicts: respective-states of two-constructs, spatial-variations, transform-relations between-them, dual-locking-states, states of Local-Vibration-Propagation-Deliver, intra-form-evolution and mutual-Yun-flux-conversions among them. Field only performs description and does not generate objective-forces.

3. **Participation-Conditions**
Only two independent-constructs participate in duality-coupling; neither swallows the-other. Each maintains its intrinsic-locking-state. Construct-itself must satisfy valid-locking-configuration within critical-boundary-range.

4. **Boundary-Conditions**
- Isolated single-construct inside spatial-domain: no counterpart-entity for duality-coupling; construct possesses merely intrinsic-locking-state. No inter-construct-transform-relations, no Local-Vibration / Propagation / Deliver and their transformations. Field has no investigative-object.
- When dual-constructs dissociate: dual-relation vanishes; all inter-construct-transform-relations and dual-locking-states dissolve. Corresponding Field-characterization, Local-Vibration-Propagation-Deliver and their transformations terminate simultaneously. If parameters breach construct-intrinsic-critical-boundary, construct collapses; locking-capacity vanishes and dual-relation ceases to-exist.
- Without Spatial-Domain bearing-constructs: constructs cannot co-exist; inter-construct-transform-relations do-not-exist; Field possesses nothing-to-describe.

Two-body-dual-relation serves as logical-prototype for Field-characterization. All complex-interactions in reality are various combinations of inter-construct-transform-relations, dual-locking-states, Local-Vibration-Propagation-Deliver and their transformations under this prototype.
