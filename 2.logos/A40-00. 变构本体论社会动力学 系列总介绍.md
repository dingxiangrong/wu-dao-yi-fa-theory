# 变构本体论社会动力学 系列总介绍（归档正文，修订版）

本系列以 A40-00《变构本体论社会动力学》V2.9、A40-01《构的演化》1.5 两篇文本作为整套体系的公理基座。A40-00 完成本原本体搭建，定义变、构、势能、应力、运力、界面、实体构、虚拟构、母构、场域等核心本原概念，确立开放性、能动性、自生长、无序、公私耦合五条底层公理，建立从流变本体映射到人类社会组织的统一建模范式；A40-01 承接本原框架，补充**八条**衍生因果公理，以**强权强度周期**、**暴力权身份载体演化**双维度，推演强身份、弱身份、无身份三类强权母构，以及多强权并联的无强权界面构的内生应力、演化路径、崩变范式，给出一套可复现的社会组织周期分析逻辑。

在这套统一本体与演化公理之上，衍生两组独立分析工具篇目：A40-02《政治场域》、A40-03《经济场域》。二者不新增底层公理，仅复用基座内本原定义，分别构建两套专用观测标尺：政治场域依托「察、传、势、序、持」模型，观测规则制定权场域下人心叙事、秩序迭代的闭环演化；经济场域依托「隙、认知拓界、地缘拓界、运力博弈」框架，观测经济支配权场域势能流转、资源占位、壁垒形成的动力学过程。

后续 A40-A 至 A40-F 为案例推演与观测篇目，是基座与分析工具的落地校验层：沿着人类族群起源、原始构生长、先秦礼制对位、华夏皇权构长程演化的历史脉络，再延伸至当代资本、地缘权力个案，用同一套统一标尺，对不同文明、不同形态的社会母构做对照推演与现象解释。

整套体系的底层主线：一切社会组织、权力更迭、制度变迁、文明兴衰，本质都是构的自生长本能与秩序约束持续对峙、应力不断累积与释放的动力学过程。本系列定位为开放的 Working Paper 合集，以自洽公理作为起点，贯通本体建模、范式工具、历史推演、现实观测四层层级，不依附既有社会学、政治学理论框架，提供一套跨场景、跨文明通用的社会动力学分析体系。

# Overview of the Flux-Construct Ontology: Social Dynamics (English, revised)

This series takes two documents, A40-00 *Flux-Construct Ontology: Social Dynamics V2.9* and A40-01 *The Evolution of Constructs 1.5*, as the axiomatic foundation of the whole system. A40-00 establishes the primitive ontology, defining core primitives including Flux, Construct, Potential Energy, Stress, Capacity, Interface, Physical Construct, Virtual Construct, Mother Construct and Field. It puts forward five fundamental axioms: Axiom of Openness, Axiom of Agency, Axiom of Self-Growth, Axiom of Disorder and Axiom of Public-Private Coupling, and builds a unified modelling paradigm mapping primordial flux to human social organizations. A40-01 inherits this primitive framework and supplements eight derived causal axioms. Based on the dual dimensions of **cycle of dominant meta-construct intensity** and **identity carrier evolution of violent power**, it deduces endogenous stress, evolutionary paths and collapse modes of three types of hegemonic mother constructs (identity-bound, weak-identity and identity-free), as well as non-hegemonic interface constructs formed by parallel multi-hegemon interaction, providing a reproducible logic for analyzing social organizational cycles.

Built upon these axioms are two analytical instrument papers: A40-02 Political Field and A40-03 Economic Field. They introduce no new primitive axioms, and reuse all core definitions from the foundational texts to form two dedicated observation frameworks. The political field adopts the *wu-shi* (five political operations: **Discernment, Transmission, Potency, Order, Adaptive Stewardship**) model to interpret the closed-loop evolution of narrative, consensus and institutional iteration within the rule-making field. The economic field adopts the framework of gap, cognitive expansion, geographic expansion and capacity competition to describe potential circulation, resource occupation and barrier formation within the economic-dominance field.

Subsequent papers A40-A to A40-F serve as case deduction and empirical verification. They apply the foundational axioms and analytical tools for long-range historical deduction, covering the origin of human groups, germination of primitive constructs, ritual systems in pre-Qin China and imperial power constructs of ancient Chinese empires, and further extend to contemporary observations of capital, geopolitics and power. All cases are examined with one consistent set of **unified analytical lens**.

The core thread of this system: all social organizations, power transitions, institutional changes and civilizational rise-and-fall arise from the perpetual tension between the innate self-growth tendency of constructs and institutional constraints, together with continuous accumulation and release of stress. This corpus is archived as an open set of Working Papers. Starting from self-consistent axioms, it consists of four layers: ontological modelling, analytical paradigms, historical deduction and real-world observation. Independent of pre-existing sociological or political theories, it offers a cross-scenario, cross-civilization general system for social dynamics reasoning.
