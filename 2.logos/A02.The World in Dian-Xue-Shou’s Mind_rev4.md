# A02. The World in Dian-Xue-Shou’s Mind ｜wu-dao-yi-fa · Theory Division
## Introduction
> Look inward to oneself, look outward to the world, look forward to civilizations, look backward to underlying laws.

Most conceptual frameworks are built by first erecting a skeleton and then filling in content, assembled top-down.

This framework originates from discrete independent insights, refined gradually alongside deepening understanding of essences. Scattered observations naturally interlock and organize. After years of accumulation, it evolves into a self-consistent, deducible cognitive framework. The following unfolds in five layers: fundamental ontology, human nature, mental operation, man-made systems, and civilization.

## I. Root of the World: Axioms of Disorder and Order, Binary Origin of Flux and Construct
A57 Ontology of Flux-Construct identifies the origin of the world grounded upon the axioms of disorder and order.

**The nature of disorder is not chaotic divergence, but an unpredictable future trajectory.**

It evolves continuously without predetermined paths or fixed destinations, much like an out-of-control spacecraft in deep space with no clear destination; or duckweed adrift on the ocean with no settled shore.

Unpredictable future change constitutes disorder.

**The nature of order refers to predictable flux confined within defined rule frameworks.**

Things operate within clear boundaries, established structures and stable rules. Their evolutionary trajectories, trends and outcomes can be deduced, forecasted and converged.

Disorder and order encompass all motion, generation and extinction, and evolutionary forms across the universe; they form the underlying axiom for analyzing all phenomena.

**Upon this foundation, the binary origin of the world is established**:
**Flux (C) is the source of perpetual motion; Construct (S) is the form that locks in order. The former supplies motive force; the latter defines order.**

All circulation, generation, extinction, transition and motion in heaven and earth stem from unceasing Flux.

All forms, structures, boundaries, steady states, orders and rules emerge from bounded Construct.

> Yun is the measure of Flux; Field describes relations between Constructs. Vibration stands for local reciprocating oscillation; Propagation refers to overall spatial migration; Deliver denotes cross-domain transmission.
>
> Force is a tending relation between two Constructs.
>
>Limit is a relative constraint relation across two or more dimensions of change.

All phenomena fall within four categories: Flux, Construct, Yun, and Field.

## III. Root of Mind: Causation for Response, Value for Decision
A22 and A23 reshape the boundary between subjectivity and objectivity.

**Vision is the world within one’s mind: spatial snapshots plus temporal sequences.**

Spatial snapshots define the structure, position, relations and states of all things.

Temporal sequences chain the evolution, causation, flow and trends of all events.

Gaps in pattern, cognitive bias and life trajectories between people

essentially arise from differing spatial snapshots, temporal sequences and causal chains.

**Mental operation runs along two improvable main threads:**
> **Causation responds to the world; value makes decisions.**

Causal capability is objective, trainable, iterative and refinable core computing power.

Value orientation is subjective, guideable, moldable and calibratable measurement scale.

Hereby the ontological status of culture is established: **Culture is soil that shapes human beings.**
> Culture shapes values; values determine choices; choices shape lives, forming fertile soil.
> Culture depends on human existence, absorbing nutrients externally and activating thought internally to nurture its ecosystem.

Once the mental closed loop forms, all disturbances in the world can be decomposed, attributed and organized.

## IV. Root of Man-Made Systems: Dual Bearing as Substance, Existential Purpose as Aim
A31 defines the Three Principles and Twelve Laws governing architecture, efficiency and attribution of man-made systems.
- **Dual Bearing — Environment as base, technology as body.**
- **Existential Purpose — all exist for their intended aim.**

No man-made entity exists without reliance or without purpose.

Software, institutions, organizations, tools and architectures do not exist according to preference, nor emerge for no reason. Yet some people substitute reality with power status and disrupt purposes by personal preference, forcing systems to run against natural laws, and ultimately suffer backlash.

> **Understand Dual Bearing, and you know whether a system can persist.**
>
> **Understand Purpose, and you know whether a system ought to come into being.**

## V. Root of Civilization: Subjects Standing in the World, Games Forming Situations, Rules Evolving the World, Three Axioms for Coexistence
**The natural world exists through “Flux | Construct”: Flux supplies force, Construct defines order.**

**Human society stands upon “Human | Culture”: Humans supply force, Culture generates order.**

### 1. Human Subjectivity and Dual Attributes of Culture
The primary prerequisite of civilization is the existence of **subjects**.

Individuals, organizations, enterprises, polities and nations are all independent subjects within the civilization field, equipped with core capacities for autonomous choice, gaming, creation and evolution. All social orders, institutional rules and civilization iterations originate from interactions among subjects.

Culture bears dual attributes:
- Endogenously, culture shapes people, rooted in group minds and calibrating value orientations;
- Exogenously, culture imposes constraints, forming invisible order and defining behavioral boundaries of subjects.

Humans create culture; culture nurtures human minds. This is the meaning of the statement: **Culture is soil that shapes human beings.**

### 2. Power Configuration
The essence of configuration is the relative power balance among multiple subjects.

Social structures, class patterns, gaming strengths and evolutionary directions of order are directly shaped and defined by shifts in power.

### 3. Meta-Rules and Ordinary Rules
**Meta-Rule: order emerging from collisions and games of multiple forces, without supreme adjudication power or top-level authoritative checks and balances.**
> Without predefined scales, power itself becomes the scale; without man-made rules, games generate rules spontaneously. The law of the jungle and pure free-market gaming are typical forms of meta-rules.

**Ordinary Rule: derived artificial rules negotiated and agreed within a framework of established top-level order and balancing forces.**
> Top-level forces first define boundaries, overall situations and order; then detailed rules are implemented at subordinate layers.
>
> Ordinary rules always depend on higher-order order and cannot break the boundary baseline of that higher order.

### 4. Four Core Social Contradictions
Contradictions of productive force, distribution, external conflict, and human-nature relation.

Tensions along these four dimensions drive all evolutionary cycles, rises and falls of human civilizations.

> Assets follow laws of long-term persistence; institutions follow the path of dynamic checks and balances.
>
> Debt is mortgage of time; civilization is collaboration through rules.

### 5. Three Axioms of Civilization — Axioms for Coexistence of Subjects (H04, H05, H06)
All cooperation, competition and conflicts among subjects ultimately converge to three top-level axioms, the underlying axioms for all subjects to coexist and develop.

**First: Defining entitlements to settle disputes, the principle of conflict cessation**
> “When Qin lost its deer, all under heaven chased it.” — this describes undefined entitlements triggering endless contention.
> 
> Without defined entitlements, boundaries lack basis and rights and obligations lack grounds.
> Many disputes in the world arise from this.

**Second: Redlines to uphold boundaries, the principle of coexistence**
> All things have boundaries and follow their own paths; if bottom lines remain unbroken, order will not collapse.
> 
> Even with defined entitlements, trespassing onto others’ domains triggers chaos and breakdown of order.
> 
> Boundary observance sustains order; boundary violation breeds disorder. Persistent redlines enable long-term stability.

**Third: Harmony in diversity, the principle of symbiosis**
> Similar entities tend to compete; heterogeneous entities can coexist symbiotically.
> 
> Grass does not compete with tall trees for height toward the sky; tall trees do not compete with grass for ground coverage.
> 
> Entities differ in form, endowment and path, yet can inhabit the same domain, complement and coexist for a long time.
> 
> Harmony in diversity is the natural way for diverse subjects to coexist, complement and thrive together.

### Summary of Civilization
**Humans are the foundation of civilization; culture is the soil of civilization.**

Games constitute the process of civilization; rules are the tools of civilization; the three axioms serve as the handbook of civilization.

This framework can explain fluctuations in economic cycles, rise and fall of institutions;

trajectories of class mobility, outcomes of great-power games;

and even the destination of civilizations and the way of life for individuals.

## VI. Characteristics of Framework Formation
This cognitive framework is not pre-planned and assembled top-down.

There is no preset blueprint or pre-written outline, nor is it pieced together from external theories.

It grows from discrete single-point insights, one observation and one principle at a time. Scattered viewpoints naturally interlock and organize. After years of accumulation, layers lock together and self-organize, eventually forming a self-consistent and deducible system spanning five layers.

It carries three features:
1. Fragmented insight, holistic formation: numerous axioms, models and judgments originate from single-point breakthrough insights, rooted in the same origin with closed logic.
2. Five-layer connectivity, full-chain deduction from top to bottom: physical ontology → human cognition → mental operation → man-made systems → social civilization. Layers nest with one another; the bottom layer can deduce the top layer, and micro phenomena can interpret macro patterns.
3. Global self-consistency: it can interpret nature, human minds, technology, construct institutions, analyze economy and depict civilizations. One principle illuminates many phenomena; one foundation establishes all manifestations.
