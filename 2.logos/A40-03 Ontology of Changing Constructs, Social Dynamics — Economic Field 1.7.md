## I. Fundamental Economic Concepts

### 1.1 Tracing the Origin

The economic field defined in this text corresponds to the field of economic dominion within the ontological taxonomy of A40-00. The other two primitive fields set out in A40-00 are the field of violent dominion, mapped to the military field, and the field of rule-making dominion, mapped to the political field of A40-02. The three fields interact through mutual stress and reciprocal constraint, jointly forming the complete social dynamical system.

### 1.2 Ontological Definition of the Economy

The economy denotes core social activity whereby economic agents deploy their own transport capacity, identify field cleavages, embed transport capacity, build value links, transmit and deliver value to other physical constructs, and ultimately achieve outward extension, iteration and growth of their own physical construct.

The essence of economic activity lies in the discovery, contest, seizure, barrier-latching and steady-state consolidation of cleavages within the field. All commercial iteration, industrial expansion and firm contestation revolve entirely around the generation, extinction and attribution of **cleavages**.

### 1.3 Boundary between Cleavage and Stable Circulation

Superficial market supply-demand gaps and commercial opportunities are merely external manifestations of cleavages, not the ontology of cleavage itself.
A mere physical gap or unfilled supply-demand vacuum does **not** constitute an economically effective cleavage.

A cleavage only translates from “potential space” into a sustainable commercial form after sustained embedding of agent transport capacity, continuous value delivery, persistent stress interaction, and formation of stable cross-agent value circulation relations anchored via interfaces. A stable circulation relation is a consolidated pathway emerging from repeated interface interactions, continuous stress dissipation and sustained value delivery. It is no longer a one-off exchange, but a reusable value link.

### 1.4 Functional Evolution of Stable Circulation Relations

Per the ontological definition in A40-00, the native function of interface interaction is to dissipate interactive stress between physical constructs. Within the evolutionary process of the economic field, these stable value-circulation relations develop a primary secondary function: carrying value circulation, supporting the expansion of physical constructs, and bearing positional contestation within the field.

### 1.5 Full Evolutionary Pathway of Economic Activity

After an economic agent identifies a cleavage and validates the viability of the contested terrain, its first move is to construct a value circulation link. Without links there can be no interaction, no exchange and no economic contestation. Links are the prerequisite carrier for all economic activity; positional rivalry must rest upon circulation pathways.

Standard full evolutionary sequence: latent field cleavage → cognitive boundary expansion (cleavage identification and barrier penetration) → geopolitical boundary validation (battlefield accessibility assessment) → construction of value links and circulation channels → incremental contention (swift occupation + barrier imposition) → stock contestation (forceful transport-capacity clearance) → sustained value delivery → positional consolidation / barrier locking → iterative expansion and continuous growth.

Links must be established on accessible geopolitical battlefields; hence geopolitical validation precedes link construction.

This sequence represents the standard evolutionary paradigm for the economic field and is not rigidly deterministic. The framework permits dynamic fault tolerance. Stagnation, reversion or dissipation may occur at any stage of the whole process, conditioned by agent transport capacity, geopolitical constraints and shifts in field stress.

Cognitive boundary expansion and geopolitical boundary expansion constitute the two foundational prerequisites of economic activity. The value link serves as the core circulation carrier running through the entire process; incremental contention and stock contestation form the core contestative cycle for agents within the field.

### 1.6 Definition of the Economic Field

The economic field is the interactive domain wherein economic agents engage in interaction, contestation and value circulation. Buyers, sellers and competing suppliers all qualify as economic agents. Agents continuously pull against one another, negotiate terms, compete, deploy transport capacity and exchange value. Constructs operate as latching mechanisms that constrain and consolidate the interactive order gradually emerging within the field. Through repeated interface interactions, market agents form stable interaction patterns. When a set of interfaces converges to consensus and gives rise to an independent unified identity, a construct takes shape and latches the interaction order into a durable social unit. Market evolution springs from tension and transformation generated by persistent interaction among these economic agents.

### 1.7 Distinction between Construct and Interface

Most economic agents participating in contestation and value circulation fall under physical constructs, which include social constructs and public constructs. Public constructs occupy a dual role: they are themselves economic agents while simultaneously acting as rule arbiters presiding over field order. Economic agents conduct interactive contestation via interfaces. An interface denotes an interaction channel; for example, mutually agreed loan terms constitute an interface arrangement. Interfacial conduct is bounded by virtual constructs, with laws and statutes serving as canonical examples. Interface agreements cannot cross boundaries set by virtual constructs; any transgression renders them invalid. An interface is not equivalent to a construct. A construct is a social unit formed once a cluster of interfaces reach consensus and spawn an independent unified identity.

## II. On Cleavage — Cognitive Boundary Expansion: Primordial Cleavage of the Economic Field

### 2.1 Ontological Definition of Cleavage

**A cleavage is accustomed satisfaction.**

Within consolidated, steadily operating value-circulation systems, circulation patterns, matching logics and supply configurations gradually settle into inertia. Market agents generally accept current conditions as viable and seldom conduct deep investigation, iterative calibration or exploration of latent value spaces. Collective steady-state satisfaction itself constitutes a structural cleavage within the economic field. For instance, in the era of feature phones, users’ settled expectation that “a mobile phone exists only to make calls and send text messages” was the largest cleavage preceding the rise of smartphones. Latent demand for more versatile communication tools already existed; old cognition simply locked demand into the response mode of “calling and texting”.

### 2.2 Dual-Layer Core Structure of Cleavage Space

Cleavage inherently comprises two successive, inseparable boundary-expansion dimensions that form the foundational operating framework of the economic field:
**First layer: Cognitive boundary expansion (mental dimension — prerequisite for cleavage identification)**
**Second layer: Geopolitical boundary expansion (real-world dimension — implementable battlefield)**

Cognitive boundary expansion is barrier penetration and dimensional elevation on the mental plane. Fundamental human demands remain constant; markets do not conjure new demands ex nihilo. All commercial innovation never creates demand. It penetrates old cognitive lock-in and delivers higher-precision, deeper and more comprehensive value responses to pre-existing demands. Cognition may expand boundlessly, cross domains and infer without limit. Cognitive breakthroughs reveal all structurally obscured cleavages embedded within steady-state systems and are the origin of every commercial opportunity.

Geopolitical boundary expansion denotes battlefield opening in the tangible realm. Cognition knows no borders or physical barriers, yet transport-capacity deployment, value delivery, market occupation and final realisation of cleavages are rigidly constrained by geopolitical domains. A geopolitical domain embeds political regulation, public-construct rules, cultural paradigms, trust regimes, policy barriers and industrial-chain stress, forming a composite system of implementation impediments. A cleavage visible to cognition is not guaranteed implementable within a geopolitical battlefield. Seeing a cleavage is an act of cognition; capturing a cleavage demands geopolitical and transport-capacity operational capability.

### 2.3 Essential Distinction between Cleavage, Market Vacuum and Commercial Opportunity

Cleavage is rooted in cognitive lock-in: it is a structural blind spot endogenous to system steady states and belongs to economic ontology.
A market vacuum refers to an untrodden space at the physical level and is a superficial manifestation.
A commercial opportunity is a subjective judgement formed by an agent after identifying a cleavage, hence a derivative outcome.

The three concepts follow a strict hierarchical progression: cleavage is ontological, vacuum is phenomenal, and opportunity is derivative inference.

### 2.4 Fourfold Constraint System of the Field

Persistent market cognitive lock-in and prolonged dormancy of unactivated cleavages stem from four rigid constraints:

1. **Technical boundary constraint (objective rigidity)**
Existing technology cannot yet support high-dimensional value responses. Insufficient capacity does not erase demand or eliminate cleavages; it merely prevents realisation under present conditions. Technology is raw foundational material and cannot generate commercial value directly. Cognition acts as the bridge linking technology to inherent demand to forge new market response pathways.
2. **Industrial paradigm and habit constraint (subjective inertia)**
Path dependence solidifies across an industry; existing business models and service forms are treated as default viable solutions, terminating deep exploration and cognitive boundary expansion.
3. **Return-expectation constraint (resource inertia)**
Basic demands are already shallowly satisfied. Deep iteration and paradigm transformation carry high cost, high risk and uncertain returns, prompting market agents to suspend value exploration. The technical boundary marks the ceiling of capability; return expectation marks the floor of incentive. The former represents inability; the latter represents unwillingness.
4. **Geopolitical domain barrier constraint (field-boundary rigidity)**
Even where cognition, technology and transport capacity are fully mature, multilayer geopolitical barriers may still lock off pathways for cleavage realisation and block external agents from entry and expansion.

### 2.5 Dual-Layer Barrier-Penetration Mechanism

Activation of a cleavage relies on two successive penetration stages, both indispensable:
**First layer: Self-penetration of barriers (internal cognitive boundary expansion)**
Breaking free from inherited experience, industrial paradigms and mental inertia, penetrating the superficial steady state of markets to perceive structurally latent cleavages beneath.

**Second layer: Penetration of others’ barriers (market-wide cognitive boundary expansion)**
Through value delivery, demonstrative implementation, paradigm validation and reconstructed narrative logic, dismantling collective cognitive lock-in across the market and achieving unified market cognition.

Cloud computing serves as a canonical illustration. Cloud technological building blocks were available long before adoption, and the underlying demand for cost reduction and efficiency improvement remained constant. Nevertheless, the whole market clung to the cognitive lock-in that “data must reside on local on-premises hardware to be secure”, leaving the cleavage dormant for years. Cumulative practical cases eventually breached collective cognitive barriers, completing dual-layer penetration and enabling the cleavage to activate, materialise and spawn new value links.

### 2.6 Independent Properties of Cognitive Boundary Expansion

Cognitive boundary expansion carries strong autonomy: cognition may emerge from internal breakthrough, be externally adopted, commercially acquired, replicated for iteration, or adapted after import. Cleavage identification is not exclusive to original innovators. Many transport-capacity-rich market agents may adopt cognition already expanded by others to achieve geopolitical implementation and market occupation.

Cognitive boundary expansion unlocks possibility; geopolitics and transport capacity deliver certainty. First movers who identify cleavages without matching transport capacity and geopolitical advantage are vulnerable to having their gains captured by powerful latecomers.

## III. On Transport Capacity — Geopolitical Boundary Expansion: Contestation on the Market Battlefield

### 3.1 Ontological Positioning of Transport Capacity

Cognitive boundary expansion answers the primordial question of whether cleavages exist and can be perceived.
Geopolitical boundary expansion addresses the boundary question of whether viable battlefields exist for implementation and entry.
**Transport capacity answers the contestative question of whether cleavages can be seized, positions defended, and rival spaces cleared.**

Cognitive boundary expansion unlocks possibility; geopolitics and transport capacity deliver certainty. Cognitive expansion is reconnaissance and sight; geopolitical expansion demarcates the battlefield; deployment of transport capacity constitutes combat and occupation. The market is a battlefield, geopolitical space is territory, and transport capacity is core combat power.

### 3.2 Dual-Stage Field Contestation: Contend (Incremental) → Seize (Stock)

All agent contestation within the economic field falls rigorously into two successive phases, a logic covering most commercial conduct:

#### Phase One: Contend — Incremental Expansion Phase

When a cleavage has just been activated by cognitive boundary expansion and geopolitical battlefields are open, the space is predominantly incremental, with limited direct stock-based involution.
The core actions of contention: swift advance + barrier imposition

1. Swift advance: Rapid deployment of transport capacity, product rollout, value delivery and preliminary link construction, pre-emptively occupying vacant response niches, closing market time windows, and establishing initial binding with users and markets.
2. Barrier imposition: Simultaneous construction of three tiered barriers forming a layered impedance system to secure occupied positions and obstruct rival entry:

- Tier one: Technical barriers. Locking core underlying technology, patents and process systems to block low-end imitation and shallow competitors, forming capability-layer defences.
- Tier two: Geopolitical barriers. Leveraging local policy, industrial supporting ecosystems, geopolitical rules and public-construct resources to build regionally exclusive protective boundaries, forming field-layer defences.
- Tier three: Brand barriers. Establishing exclusive occupation of user mental cognition, an intangible rampart of a higher order and harder to breach. Technical and geopolitical barriers are mostly physical and may be overcome by capital, technology or policy; cognition-based brand latching can sustain strong constraint under suitable conditions.

The essence of the contention phase: rapid occupation of vacant niches and layered defence construction, accumulating initial positional advantage within the incremental window.

#### Phase Two: Seize — Stock Clearance Phase

Once incremental cleavages are filled by many agents and high-quality niches near saturation, windows contract and contest intensity rises, shifting from deployment to force confrontation.

The core action of seizure: asymmetric compression enabled by aggregated transport capacity. Asymmetry denotes unequal distribution of transport-capacity aggregation, scale potentiality, supply-chain locking power and comparable dimensions. Dominant agents exert suppression via thicker transport capacity, which weaker agents cannot match, driving rivals to retreat or exit and redistributing limited stock niches. Contestation in the seizure phase is no longer centred on new deployment and barrier construction, but on striking the sustaining substrate of competitors, forcing contraction or withdrawal and triggering reconfiguration of the field landscape.

The essence of the seizure phase: reshaping stock configurations through aggregated force, marking the close of the incremental era.

### 3.3 Two Independent Success Paradigms of Cognition and Transport Capacity

The dual boundary-expansion and contend-seize contestation framework explains the logic behind firm success and failure via two core paradigms:

#### Paradigm One: Cognition First, Transport Capacity Follows (Innovation Breakthrough Paradigm)

An agent completes internal barrier penetration and cognitive boundary expansion, first discovering structural cleavages → validates geopolitical accessibility → executes incremental contention through swift advance and barrier imposition. If evolution continues into the stock phase, asymmetric transport-capacity seizure contestation is triggered. Small innovative firms and trailblazing sector participants mostly follow this pathway.

#### Paradigm Two: Transport Capacity First, Adopted Cognition for Implementation (Latecomer Occupation Paradigm)

An agent may not achieve endogenous cognitive boundary expansion or first-mover cleavage identification yet possesses abundant transport capacity → absorbs, acquires or replicates mature external cognitive outcomes → leverages transport capacity to penetrate geopolitical constraints and enter the field for positional contention. Upon entering the stock phase, such agents are well positioned to initiate seizure and reconfigure the field. Cognition may be imported; transport capacity is the critical reserve for contestation.

### 3.4 Screening Value of Geopolitical Domains: Underlying Explanation for Disparities between Domestic and Overseas Firms

A geopolitical domain is a composite constraint field superposing politics, rules, culture, public constructs, industrial chains and trust. Even leading overseas firms with complete cognition, technology and transport capacity may fail to monopolise local markets; the same applies to domestic firms expanding overseas, who encounter dual local locking mechanisms. Geopolitical domains are not impenetrable absolute walls; they operate as threshold-based screening filters. Barriers may still be breached once technological generation gaps cross the threshold, or when structural fractures emerge within local industrial chains. The smartphone displacement of feature phones exemplifies threshold-crossing technological superiority: when response precision jumps an order of magnitude, old cognitive lock-in and geopolitical barriers fail concurrently.

### 3.5 Dynamic Cleavage Generation and Extinction, and the Economic Iteration Loop

The complete iterative cycle of the economic field proceeds continuously: cognitive barrier penetration and expansion → emergence of new cleavages → geopolitical validation and battlefield opening → incremental contention by agents (swift advance + barrier imposition) → saturation of space and transition to stock seizure (transport-capacity clearance) → consolidation of industrial patterns and return of the system to steady-state cognitive lock-in → latent incubation of new cleavages → commencement of the next iteration.

Each steady-state consolidation represents superposition at the phenomenal level. Only measurements of transport capacity and potentiality may be contained within the vessel of the construct.

Technological iteration only furnishes underlying raw materials; geopolitical domains only define battlefield boundaries. Cognitive boundary expansion unlocks possibility, transport-capacity contestation unfolds in two phases, and multilayer barriers shape configurations. These form the core driving force for sustained self-generation and evolutionary iteration within the economic field.

All movements within the economic field ultimately reduce to **cognitive boundary expansion of cleavages** and **geopolitical contestation of force**. Cognition penetrates obscurity to generate cleavages; transport capacity occupies positions and latches constructs; interfaces carry circulation; virtual constructs delimit boundaries; multi-agent dynamic stress interaction collectively forms the complete dynamical mechanism enabling self-generation, self-iteration and self-stabilisation of economic society.

The political field defines rules and establishes order; the economic field opens cleavages, organises circulation and fosters growth. Rules set limits upon circulation, and circulation empowers rules. Steady order incubates new cleavages, and breakthroughs of cleavages reconstruct new orders. The two fields interlock bidirectionally and constrain one another, iterating reciprocally to drive construct extension of social systems and civilisational evolution.

This manuscript does not generalise from superficial commercial phenomena. It deploys uniformly the primitive deductive instruments of change, construct, cleavage, transport capacity, interface, stress and boundary expansion. It thereby advances analysis of economic activity beyond empirical summary into a closed, reusable and transferable ontological paradigm of social dynamics, completing foundational theoretical affirmation and systemic closure for the economic field.
