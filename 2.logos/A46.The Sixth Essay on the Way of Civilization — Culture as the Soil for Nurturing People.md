## Abstract
Against the background of prevailing industrial-centered interpretations of national strength, this paper puts forward a four-layer evolutionary architecture for sovereign civilization, namely ¡°cultural soil-human agents-comprehensive social capabilities-ultimate goals¡±. This study points out that culture acts as the underlying soil for nurturing people; human beings are the sole operational subject of society. Social comprehensive capacity consists of two mutually indispensable pillars: cultural capability with self-iterative dual-open mechanism, and industrial capability supported by fixed-asset investment and technology. Industrial capacity is only a derived result rather than the root cause of national power. A sustainable sovereign civilization must realize a closed-loop iteration from cultural genes to national goals, and strike a balance between soft cultural strength and hard industrial strength. This framework provides a holistic systemic perspective for analyzing national rise-and-fall and civilizational rejuvenation.

## Introduction
Within contemporary national-development research circles, there exists a prevalent cognitive paradigm that highly emphasizes the decisive value of industrial systems. It equates industrial capacity, manufacturing scale, and complete industrial-chain coverage with the core, or even the entirety, of national comprehensive strength.

This perspective attaches great importance to material production, hardware foundations and industrial hard power. ## III. Layer Three of Sovereign Civilization: Comprehensive Social Capabilities ¡ª A Dual-Pillar Framework
Populations cultivated and shaped by the cultural soil generate comprehensive societal capabilities through collaboration, organization, research-and-development, production and governance.

**Cultural Capability (Soft Power) + Industrial Capability (Hard Power)**

### 3.1 Cultural Capability: The Self-Iterating Soft-Power Pillar
Cultural capability acts as society¡¯s endogenous source of growth.

It covers educational systems, scientific-research training, intellectual vitality, value cohesion, civilizational appeal and talent supply.

Its defining feature lies in **self-growth, self-iteration and self-amelioration within a closed-loop mechanism**.

Its growth relies on a dual openness mechanism:
1. **External Openness**: Absorb nourishment from foreign civilizations, pursue inclusive learning and reject isolationism.
2. **Internal Openness**: Maintain open-mindedness within society, permit diverse viewpoints, enable exploration and encourage innovation.

Dual openness activates society¡¯s inner cultural vitality. It **nurtures, reforms and upgrades the underlying cultural-gene soil in a feedback loop**, renewing the soil that nurtures people and creating a positive civilizational cycle.

>Openness is a means for iteration rather than an ultimate end. Both external and internal openness shall observe the baseline of preserving the ontological existence of indigenous culture. When this baseline is breached, openness will instead erode the very soil upon which civilization depends.

### 3.2 Industrial Capability: The Material-Foundation Hard-Power Pillar
Industrial capability provides the material survival base for a sovereign society, delivering hardware guarantees for security and people¡¯s well-being.

Two pre-requisite hard factors generate industrial capability:
1. **Fixed-asset Investment**: Sustained investment in infrastructure, production lines and equipment, which furnishes the material groundwork for industrial deployment.
2. **Science-and-Technology Iteration**: Processes, engineering methodologies and underlying principles that set ceilings for industrial quality and efficiency.

Industrial capability is not innate. It is an output derived from human expertise, human investment and human organization.

>Both the willingness for fixed-asset investment and the agents of scientific-technological innovation ultimately stem from populations shaped by the underlying cultural soil described in Layer Two.

## IV. Core Limitations of Industrial-Centric Thinking ¡ª Core Thesis of This Paper

It prioritizes accumulated industrial stock yet fails to grasp the dynamic closed-loop of cultural self-growth, social self-renewal and civilizational self-evolution.
