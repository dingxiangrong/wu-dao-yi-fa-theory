## I. Three Ontological Primitives of Social-Economy
Setting aside derivative indicators, statistical metrics and superficial economic phenomena, social-economic ontology consists exclusively of three primitive dimensions; no fourth primitive exists.

### 1. Wealth Stock Reserve
Wealth Stock Reserve represents the cumulative heritage deposited by all historical societal production activities. It constitutes the static substrate of economic operations. It underpins societal risk mitigation, cross-cyclical buffering, and sustained production, serving as the precondition for all economic activities.

### 2. Wealth-Creation Capacity
Wealth-Creation Capacity denotes society¡¯s real rate of generating new wealth per unit time, synthesized across industrial systems, human resources and organizational arrangements. It embodies the integrated endowments and practical performance of societal production. GDP is merely the period-specific flow outcome of Wealth-Creation Capacity, and must never be conflated with the capacity itself.

### 3. Capacity-Evolution Composite Force
Wealth-Creation Capacity is not static. Its expansion or decay is governed by Capacity-Evolution Composite Force: Composite Force = Societal Positive Driving Force - Societal Negative Resistive Force. A positive composite force upgrades Wealth-Creation Capacity; zero composite force signifies stagnation; negative composite force brings about capacity recession.

The core logical proposition of the three primitives: **Wealth stock sets the baseline; wealth-creation capacity defines the present; composite evolutionary force shapes the future.**

## II. Three Core Derivative Variables Shaping Composite Evolutionary Force
All macroeconomic fluctuations and developmental contradictions converge upon three regulatory variables. Each may yield either driving force or resistive force, acting bidirectionally upon Wealth-Creation Capacity and determining economic prosperity or decline.

### 1. Allocation Mechanism (Demand-Side Variable: Bidirectional)
Societal productive potential is sufficient, and genuine consumer needs prevail across the population. Under equitable allocation, purchasing power is fully unleashed, demand absorbs output and generates driving force. Under allocation imbalance, segments of the population hold genuine wants yet lack purchasing power; real needs cannot translate into effective market demand. Goods pile up, production facilities lie idle, output contracts, and demand-side resistance emerges, suppressing the release of societal Wealth-Creation Capacity.

*Case in point*: Large-income-stratum-disadvantaged groups hold genuine subsistence consumption needs yet lack adequate income to spend, resulting in insufficient societal effective demand.

### 2. Investment Structure (Supply-Side Variable: Dual-Effect: Driving / Resistive)
Initially robust genuine market demand attracts massive capital inflows, amplified by society¡¯s formidable Wealth-Creation Capacity. Where investment flows toward Effective Reserve formation, long-run societal wealth accumulates and driving force emerges. When capital over-heats and expands output far beyond real-demand growth, generalized oversupply arises. Outputs and assets possess neither present-day market demand nor long-term reserve value, devolving into invalid production capacity and giving rise to supply-side resistance through structural investment imbalance.

*Case in point*: The real-estate sector once enjoyed vigorous baseline demand. Massive capital poured in and housing construction expanded relentlessly. Once demand saturated, substantial vacant and invalid housing stock accumulated.

### 3. Technological System (Variable: Dual-Effect: Driving / Resistive)
The technological system sets the upper boundary for Wealth-Creation Capacity and operates bidirectionally. Sustained innovation and bottleneck breakthroughs lift productive efficiency and industrial hierarchy, generating positive driving force. Where core-technology bottlenecks block autonomous iteration, ceilings lock industrial upgrading. High-end capacity remains unrealized, and Redundant Capacity accumulates within low-end sectors, constituting resistive force.

*Case in point*: Sufficient capital, engineering talent and industrial infrastructure exist domestically. Yet bottlenecks in core high-end chip technologies confine production to low-to-mid-tier segments, capping advances in Wealth-Creation Capacity.

## III. The Core Structural Contradiction of Modern-Era Social-Economy
Civilization has entered a new developmental phase marked by a foundational structural contradiction: the potential ceiling of societal Wealth-Creation Capacity has systematically and persistently outstripped the absorbing boundary of aggregate current-period societal consumption.

Oversupply-driven idle capacity is no longer merely cyclical fluctuation but an intrinsic feature of civilizational capability leaps. Conventional economics rests upon the built-in premise that *current-period consumption absorbs current-period productive capacity*, which carries inherent flaws. Whenever production capacity exceeds immediate societal consumption, idled capacity, corporate contraction, employment pressure and economic downturn follow. Formidable productive capability thus turns inward against itself and generates endogenous resistive force. This constitutes the root source of contemporary economic sluggishness, involution, low growth, and cyclical crises.

## V. Three-Axiom System for Judging Effective-Reserve Assets
Not all stock assets qualify as reserves. Invalid reserves continue to produce economic resistive force. Any Effective Reserve capable of absorbing excess capacity and sustaining long-run civilizational advancement must comply rigidly with three core axioms serving as adjudicative bedrock.

### Axiom One (Persistence Axiom): Long-Hold Capability, Low Attrition, Low Depreciation
An Effective Reserve is a societal stock asset possessing cross-cyclical persistence, physical low-attrition characteristics and low depreciation. Its essence consists of material foundations decoupled from immediate-period consumption, capable of long-term preservation and sustained civilizational advancement.

An Effective Reserve must simultaneously satisfy three rigid characteristics:
1. **Long-hold capability**: Insulated from rapid obsolescence driven by business cycles or generational technological turnover, endowed with cross-cyclical persistence;
2. **Physical low-attrition**: Requiring no exorbitant ongoing maintenance to preserve core functions and form over holding periods;
3. **Low-depreciation value property**: Intrinsic value does not decay linearly over time; it resists systemic inflation erosion and preserves worth across generations.

By contrast, assets with physical form that violate these criteria are excluded from Effective Reserve and rank as implicit liabilities:
- Short-lived goods and consumables: incapable of crossing temporal cycles, inherently disqualified as reserves;
- Vacant real estate and invalid infrastructure: marked by high attrition, high depreciation and heavy resource occupation; they continuously erode societal wealth;
- Technologically obsoleted capacity: vulnerable to generational replacement; only partial short-term liquidation or total decommissioning is feasible.

### Axiom Two (Human-Centric Axiom): Humans as the Source of Innovation and New-Demand Formation
Humans are innovators of production and pioneers of new demand.

The essence of Effective Reserve does not reside in sheer material stockpiling, but in its capacity to unlock this fundamental human attribute. Any stock asset that suppresses human innovativeness or confines human pioneering spirit counts as invalid reserve or even negative asset, regardless of its book value scale.

**Logical Deduction**:
Only humans can break existing production functions and bring forth new forms of wealth;
Only humans can transcend current-period-consumption constraints and delineate new demand frontiers.

Accordingly, the only legitimate destination for societal Redundant Capacity is transformation into reserves that ¡°enable humans to realize their humanity¡± ¡ª foundational support for sustained human innovation and pioneering exploration.

All stock assets shall pass a dual-test adjudication mechanism:
- Test under the Persistence Axiom (Persistence Check): Does it possess cross-cyclical, low-attrition, low-depreciation properties-
- Test under the Human-Centric Axiom (Human-Centric Check): Does it unlock human innovativeness and pioneering agency-

Assets validated through dual adjudication must orient toward three core directions:
1. **Human-centered**: Elevate human health, cognition and creativity; unlock human liberty of agency;
2. **Security-grounded**: Consolidate institutional and societal baselines; withstand systemic risks and safeguard intergenerational equity;
