## 02 Causal Chain: Systemic Maladies and Real-World Disorders
Credit is a neutral public instrument, whose original mission is to serve real economic expansion, balance social resources and sustain positive civilizational iteration. The degradation of the whole system arises from overlapping layers of root causes, which spawn observable capital-driven disorders.
### 2.1 Tier-One Root Cause: Fundamental Shift in the Purpose of Instrument-Wielding
The original intent of credit creation is to reconcile equitable cross-regional allocation and reasonable intertemporal scheduling, serving the growth of aggregate social wealth and overall civilizational progress.

Through long-term evolution, the modern US-dollar credit system has gradually diverged from its original purpose. Its core civilizational functions of inclusive balance and real-economy empowerment have steadily diminished, while its orientation has shifted toward stock-based financial extraction, intergenerational resource overdraw, and value monetization of the public. The instrument¨s positive civilizational attributes keep declining, while extractive capital characteristics grow stronger, forming the initial source of systemic transformation.

### 2.2 Tier-Two Root Cause: Regulatory Capture via Integrated Execution and Adjudication
The core reason that misalignment persists and becomes hard to self-correct lies in substantial flaws within the institutional architecture.
> Traditional American separation of powers tends to converge around capital interests, reducing the efficacy of checks and balances. It forms a closed capital interest community characterized by **unified executive and adjudicative powers**, eroding the system¨s capacity for self-correction.

The financial stewards who create credit, inject currency and expand debt concurrently hold authority over rulemaking, market supervision and risk adjudication. The wielder also acts as adjudicator; beneficiaries exert influence over rule-setting, external oversight becomes easily nominal, and maladies solidify into entrenched systemic defects.

### 2.3 Tier-Three Root Cause: Systemic Mismatch of Rights and Liabilities, Spawning Market Disorders
The combined effect of purpose misalignment and weakened regulation tends to disrupt the matching logic of social rights and obligations, giving rise to various market irregularities.

Capital circles redefine their role in market participation: from risk-bearers, real-economy service providers and creators of new value, to beneficiaries who profit from market volatility and risk speculation.

Gains, dividends and premiums accrue largely to core capital circles; risks, losses, debts and overdraws are disproportionately transferred to society, the general public and future generations.

**Examples of real-world disorders:**
- Financial speculation through empty circulation: capital tends to decouple from the real economy, with limited deployment for production growth, innovation or job creation. It leverages asset speculation and pursues gains purely from stock redistribution;
- Directional risk transfer: high-risk packaged assets are sold to retail investors. Capital exits before risks materialize, with losses largely borne by the public, fiscal budgets and posterity;
- Regulatory capture by capital: lobbying and interest alignment influence legislation and supervision, pushing for rule revisions and relaxed constraints to consolidate capital advantages;
- Narrative shaping of public perception: leveraging communication channels to frame financial speculation favorably, downplaying debt risks, amplifying bias around wealth disparity and distorting public value judgments;
- Intergenerational resource overdraw: sustained debt expansion maintains short-run prosperity, passing legacy debt, industrial hollowing and resource pressures to subsequent generations and compressing future civilizational development space.

Diminished rule effectiveness, mismatched risks and rewards, and distorted value perception stand out as prominent pathological features of this financial system.

## 03 Outcome Chain: Evolutionary Trends and Civilizational Cyclical Pressures
When accumulated systemic disorders approach the carrying threshold of civilization, a persistently expanding debt black hole may emerge, bringing civilizational cyclical pressures within the modern financial framework.
### 3.1 Micro Outcome: Sustained Extraction of Social Labor Resources by Capital
The alienated credit black hole exhibits a tendency to expand continuously and capture returns over time. Relying on institutional advantages, narrow capital circles continuously absorb labor value, savings and time resources of the public. Widespread labor gradually becomes subordinate to capital arbitrage activities, forming a social regime marked by financial extraction.

### 3.2 Macro Outcome: Pressures on Social Labor Reserves
The debt black hole carries a self-expanding tendency. Continuous credit expansion and accumulating debt gradually approach the boundary of what labor value can sustain.

Long-term individual labor accumulation may fail to keep pace with mounting debt pressure, and the carrying capacity of labor value becomes progressively strained.

### 3.3 Civilizational Outcome: Cyclical Strain of the Modern Financial Regime
The traditional historical cycle often manifests through land annexation, rigid stratification and regime turnover.

The US-dollar-dominated modern credit system constitutes a relatively covert form of financial extraction. Without direct violent coercion, it extracts resources at civilizational scale through institutional arrangements, credit overdraw and skewed distribution. It may weaken social innovative momentum, narrow class mobility, slow civilizational iteration and trigger systemic volatility risks.

## 04 Governing Principles: Dual-Track Approach, Addressing Symptoms and Root Causes
Systemic pathology follows a closed causal loop; governance requires parallel measures targeting both symptoms and root causes. Symptomatic interventions mitigate immediate disorders, while root-cause measures reduce future malformations.
### 4.1 Treating Root Causes: Preventing Structural Distortions
1. Recalibrate the original purpose of credit, restoring its public functions of balanced allocation, real-economy empowerment and civilizational value creation;
2. Separate integrated executive and adjudicative powers, reducing the closed-loop influence of capital over legislation, supervision and adjudication;
3. Restructure the matching of rights and liabilities to align rewards with risks, reducing the emergence of distorted capital disorders at the source.

### 4.2 Resolving Outcomes: Mitigating Pressures from Legacy Debt
Alleviate the distorted one-way expansion of the debt black hole, redirecting locked stock resources back to the real economy and public welfare; control the pace of debt expansion, gradually easing accumulated intergenerational burdens and slowing the rate of black-hole growth.

## 05 Institutional Restructuring: A New Tripartite Independent Check-and-Balance Architecture for Civilization
Capital capture within the American framework has weakened its original balancing mechanism. To address modern financial disorders, a **civilizational tripartite independent check-and-balance architecture** may be established: sovereign government, independent media, and financial stewards. The three parties maintain separated powers, minimal interest entanglement, mutual checks and defined original mandates.
### 5.1 Sovereign Government Independence (Public Governance Entity)
The government reduces susceptibility to capital capture and financial entanglement, returning to its core public governance mandate: safeguarding social equity, managing public order, ensuring livelihood security and coordinating civilizational planning, as the ultimate guarantor of public interests.

### 5.2 Independent Media Oversight (Soil of Civilization ， Guardian of Order)
Media forms the foundational soil of civilization and the core carrier of value continuity.

Media is not merely a supervisory tool, nor a vehicle for a single ideological narrative. Under a sound civilizational order, neutral media holds a core mission: **upholding open inquiry, safeguarding value pluralism, and mitigating monopolistic capital discourse**.

Media shall reduce capitalization and maintain neutral independence, operating within rigid legal boundaries: law prohibits disruption of public order and dissemination of false claims; independence enables exposure of malpractice, mitigation of monopolies and preservation of diverse perspectives.

> Without independent media, civilizational soil is vulnerable to capital erosion; without open inquiry, social value perspectives tend toward monolithic rigidity.

### 5.3 Financial Stewardship Layer (Professional Execution Mandate)
Charged exclusively with credit issuance, resource allocation and operational execution of finance. It retains only professional executive authority, with no legislative power or final adjudication power. Self-supervision is constrained, and power is bounded strictly within its operational mandate.

## 06 Distributive Remediation: Long-Term Constraints on Black-Hole Expansion
After institutional frameworks are established, distributive reform serves as a long-term lever to mitigate financial alienation and constrain debt black-hole expansion.
### 6.1 Restraining Unregulated Financial Speculation
Progressive high taxation and penalty frameworks target empty financial circulation and speculative arbitrage. Returns from productive real-economy innovation are distinguished from speculative financial gains, slowing the driving forces behind black-hole expansion.

### 6.2 Public Fiscal Recirculation for Public Oversight
Taxation and forfeiture revenues flow into public systems to fund independent media, public governance and social oversight bodies. This ensures financial and institutional independence of oversight entities, lowering reliance on capital funding.

### 6.3 Restructuring the Logic of Equitable Social Distribution
Establish the reciprocal principle: **those who reap gains shall bear corresponding risks**. This mitigates the imbalance where gains accrue to a small group while risks are socialized, reconciling horizontal distribution across social groups and vertical intergenerational distribution.

## 07 Civilizational Finality: Returning to Humanistic Primacy
This paper follows a three-stage developmental framework: **cultural reordering ★ subject repositioning ★ ultimate homecoming**. The goal of stabilization, remediation, institution-building and redistribution is not merely repairing the old financial system, but civilizational reorientation and return to humanistic primacy.
### 7.1 Cultural Reordering: Soil Restoration and Ordered Coexistence of Diversity
> Civilizational return does not mean imposing a single value doctrine. Instead, under legitimate order, open inquiry and neutral media safeguards, it enables **ordered coexistence of diverse values**.

Culture is the soil for nurturing human potential; value reorientation underpins civilizational reconstruction.

Open inquiry and value neutrality constitute the underlying architecture of mature civilization, not merely superficial gestures.

Neutral media acts as the guardian of civilizational order: it cuts through monolithic narratives dominated by capital and prevents any single actor from monopolizing the definition of civilizational values.

Plural coexistence is dynamic balance within rule-based boundaries, and open inquiry serves as a prerequisite for humanistic civilization. Civilizational ideals are developmental and iterative rather than static dogma.

### 7.2 Subject Reorientation: Centering Human Cultivation and Empowerment
> Civilizational development shall not treat capital accumulation or scale expansion as its sole objective. Instead, it takes **cultivation of human capability and positive civilizational iteration** as its core orientation.

Humans stand as primary agents of innovation, demand expansion and civilizational renewal.

Social systems, production logic and developmental priorities gradually re-center around human subjects, with the core mission to **cultivate, establish and empower individuals**.

Institutions, finance, resources and technology serve human beings, reducing passive subordination of humans to instrumental systems.

### 7.3 Ultimate Homecoming: Each in Their Proper Place, Sustainable Humanistic Civilization
> The developmental form of humanistic civilization: **Let stewards govern public affairs, operators deepen real industries, innovators push boundaries, and workers receive value matching their contribution**.

Individuals with varied endowments, expertise and aspirations have accessible developmental pathways and value belonging.

It gradually alleviates pathological civilizational symptoms including financial value extraction and intergenerational overdraw, and reduces volatility stemming from financialized historical cycles.

The eventual direction: **humanistic primacy, openness and pluralism, innovation-driven, sustainable civilization**.
