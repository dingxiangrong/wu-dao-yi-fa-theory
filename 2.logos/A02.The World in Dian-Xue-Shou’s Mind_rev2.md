### 1. Subjectivity and Dual Attributes of Culture
Independent subjects are the basic premise of civilizational operation.

Individuals, organizations, enterprises, regimes and countries are all independent behavioral subjects in the civilizational system, with independent choice, game, creation and evolution capabilities. All social orders, institutional rules and civilizational iterations originate from interactions among subjects.

Culture has dual operational attributes:
> Endogenously, it shapes group cognition and calibrates collective value orientation;

Humans create culture, and culture nurtures human groups. The core connotation of **"Culture is the soil that shapes human beings"** lies herein.

### 2. Power Pattern
The essence of pattern is the dynamic power balance among multiple subjects.

Social structure, class stratification, competitive advantage and social order trends are all shaped by the dynamic evolution of power relations.

### 3. Meta-Rules and Ordinary Rules
**Meta-Rules: Spontaneous order formed by the collision and game of multiple subjects without supreme authoritative constraints.**

Without unified artificial standards, power becomes the only measurement; without top-down formulation, game behavior generates spontaneous rules. The jungle law and pure free market competition are typical meta-rule forms.

**Ordinary Rules: Derivative norms formed by negotiation and agreement under the constraint of established top-level order.**

The superior system defines the overall boundary and order, and subordinate rules are supplemented and improved on this basis. Ordinary rules are always subordinate to the top-level order and cannot break through the bottom-line boundary.

### 5. Three Civilizational Coexistence Axioms
All cooperation, competition and conflicts between subjects converge to three top-level axioms, which are the underlying codes for the coexistence and development of all social subjects.

**First: Define Boundaries to End Controversies**

Undefined subject boundaries and unclarified rights and obligations will lead to endless contention. Most social disputes originate from ambiguous status and boundary confusion.

**Second: Guard Red Lines to Maintain Order**

All behaviors have boundaries, and all operations have bottom lines. Breaking boundary rules will trigger disorder and system collapse. Adhering to boundary norms is the foundation of long-term social stability.

**Third: Harmony in Diversity to Realize Symbiosis**

Homogeneous subjects tend to compete; heterogeneous subjects can coexist interdependently.

Trees do not compete with grass for ground coverage, and grass does not compete with trees for sky height. Different endowments and development paths can coexist in the same space, complement and nourish each other. Harmony in diversity is the essential law of multi-subject symbiosis.
