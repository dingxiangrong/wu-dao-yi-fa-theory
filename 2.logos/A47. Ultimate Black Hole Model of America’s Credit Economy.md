## Chapter 1 Minimalist Two-Agent Base Model (Underlying Logic)
Assume a market with only two agents, with a fixed stock of **100 units of base money** in circulation. The total quantity of base money remains unchanged; only ownership rights are transferred.

Round 1: Agent A lends 100 units to Agent B; Agent B spends all funds received, and the full 100 units flow back to Agent A. Bookkeeping record: Agent B owes Agent A 100 units.
Round 2: Agent A lends the same 100 units to Agent B again; Agent B spends all funds once more, and the money flows back to Agent A. Cumulative book debt: Agent B owes Agent A 200 units.

Repeating this cycle for 100 rounds:
- **Actual base capital: still 100 units (no increase)**

Core essence: Base money serves as the real carrier of circulation, while debt represents book-recorded superimposed creditor claims. ## Chapter 2 Two Foundational Axioms (First Principles of the Entire Economic System)
### Definition of Two Fundamental Types of Economic Activities
- **Transaction activities**: Market participants pay money to purchase goods and services to complete value-for-value exchange. They only transfer ownership of money, **and do not create new creditor rights or debt obligations**.
- **Lending-borrowing activities**: Market participants transfer the right to use funds, generating records of creditor rights and debt obligations. They create book-based creditor claims and liabilities, **and involve no exchange of goods or services for consideration**.

> Supplementary note: This paper strips out banks as an intermediate layer. The so-called money multiplier is merely a technical measure to manage risks for such intermediaries. Real underlying lending and borrowing occurs between individual market participants.

**Axiom 1: Axiom of Conservation of Monetary Circulation**
