# A00: General Outline of the Wu-Dao-Yi-Fa(Five Daos and the One Method) System
This general outline belongs to the **Department of Principle**. It carries the first-principles axioms of the entire system, the ontological framework of the Five Ways, and the axiom-level foundation of the Boundary-Expanding Method, serving as the sole logical origin for all deductions. The Five Ways and the Boundary-Expanding Method fall entirely under the Department of Principle, encompassing all ontological axioms, underlying definitions, and rigid deductive chains. The manuscripts of the whole system are archived into six major units, among which the Department of Principle is one. The remaining archival units each undertake their respective duties and maintain global self-consistency (details of other units are uniformly documented in the repository README and will not be elaborated herein).

## Preface
There exists no universal panacea, nor universally applicable methodology.
Existing theories are either confined to the frameworks of Western scholarship or trapped in empty abstract speculation. Few are built from the underlying mental mechanisms of human beings to form a complete closed-loop system spanning "cognition — knowledge — system — civilization — the world".

Over thirty years of engineering practice, I have reviewed the rise and fall of civilizations across generations. Painfully aware of the divide between ancient and modern theories and practical implementation, as well as the alienation between individual cognition and real-world action, I have inwardly observed my mind to gain insights and outwardly extracted practical laws. Combining primal intuition with rigorous axiomatic deduction, I have originally constructed the complete theoretical framework of **Wudao-Yifa (Five Ways and One Method)**.

Each of the Five Ways has its own origin and distinct foundation, traced separately as follows:
> First, **The Way of Cognition**, rooted in introspection and self-reflection. Primal insights arise from intuition, which are later systematically organized into an axiomatic cognitive system.
> Second, **The Way of Knowledge**. Mental intuition provides the direction of thinking, paired with logical deduction to fully construct symbols and cognitive chains.
> Third, **The Way of Systems**. Its core foundation lies in decades of frontline engineering practice. Concrete businesses, hardware and codes are stripped away for upward abstraction and refinement. Intuition only serves to penetrate appearances and capture core laws.
> Fourth, **The Way of the World**, originating from the primal core intuition — **Change equals Existence**. Anchored by this primary proposition, it extends outward layer by layer to deduce the underlying operating mechanisms of all things.
> Fifth, **The Way of Civilization**. Built upon the two underlying axioms of cognition and systems as the deduction base, it relies on intuition to capture emergent characteristics of long-term group evolution, and derives meta-rules and order logic of civilization step by step.

**The Boundary-Expanding Method** derives from the axiom set of the Five Ways. It is a practical axiom system directly deduced from ontological axioms, rather than a compilation of scattered techniques.

This system does not inherit old doctrines or piece together viewpoints of predecessors. It is a closed-loop rigid deduction from bottom to top. It is not empty scholarly discourse on theoretical principles, but a practical charter integrating knowledge and action to serve the world.
> The Five Ways form the substance; the One Method serves as application. Realization, practice, intuition and deduction support one another. Theories consolidate the foundation, and practice expands boundaries.

From this point onward, all discussions concerning cognitive evolution, knowledge carriers, system architecture, civilizational order, and the origin of the world may take this system as the underlying benchmark. Any deduced conclusion violating the axioms of this system shall be deemed questionable in its logical self-consistency.

This serves as the preface.

## I. Complete Ontology of the Department of Principle: Five Ways and the Boundary-Expanding Method (System Stereoscopic Structure)
The Department of Principle produces axioms, ontological definitions and rigid constraints. It does not output cases, historical facts, popular mental tactics or concrete operational procedures. It acts as the immutable theoretical source code of the entire system.

### 1. The Five Ways (Theoretical Science: Answering What It Is and Why)
The Five Ways constitute the core axiomatic foundation of the Department of Principle, building a complete interpretive framework from human beings to the objective world layer by layer. **All deductions within the whole system take The Way of Cognition as the sole logical origin.**

#### The Way of Cognition (Axiom Chapter｜Perceptual Ontology)
Taking human beings as the subject, it reveals endogenous laws, inherent features and natural limits of perceptual behaviors starting from multi-layered intrinsic human needs. Based on innate human cognitive ontology, it defines three irreducible primitive cognitive dimensions: understanding objects, understanding people, and understanding affairs. It establishes an underlying axiom system covering cognitive limits, structured computation, iterative division and integration, and value standard. Its core inquiries: why human beings generate perceptual impulses, what innate mechanisms they rely on to extract features of external things, and what insurmountable underlying rules govern perception and cognition.

This Way only unpacks the underlying ontological logic of "perception and cognitive behavior itself", and does not teach specific life skills. It provides the sole immutable human-centered logical origin for the whole system.
> Corresponding manuscripts: A11 Demand and Motivation, A12 Three Dimensions of Understanding, A13 Cognitive Axioms, etc.

#### The Way of Knowledge (Internalization Chapter｜Constructing the Inner World)
It takes raw perceptual materials collected by The Way of Cognition, screening, precipitating, refining and naming chaotic external symbols, information and experience. It strips redundant appearances and solidifies essential laws, gradually transforming scattered external information into structured true knowledge exclusive to the subject. Through continuous cognitive reconstruction and subjective calibration, it builds and iteratively updates an individual’s self-consistent inner world model layer by layer, completing the full mental closed-loop from external materials to internal cognition and from passive reception to active modeling.

> Note: The logic of processing historical materials in *Reflections on the Twenty-Four Histories* falls within the axiomatic scope of this Way. The complete manuscript and empirical deductions of *Reflections on the Twenty-Four Histories* are classified into corresponding archival units. Massive historical materials are merely external information. After precipitation, internalization, refinement and unification by the subject, historical essence is condensed and historical perception formed, which stands as the ultimate achievement of knowledge internalization.
> Corresponding manuscripts: A21 Knowledge System, etc.

#### The Way of Systems (General Dissection Tool｜Axioms of Man-Made Objects)
It exclusively studies all artificial systems actively constructed by human beings, **excluding naturally emergent systems**. Relying on the complete axiom system of Three Ways and Twelve Disciplines, it forms a general structural dissection tool: establishing the foundation and service orientation of systems via the Shoulder Theory and Boundary Definition Theory; accomplishing dimensionality reduction decomposition of complex structures and steady-state constraints through peeling decomposition and inter-layer boundary observance. The Four Laws of Efficacy enable system cost reduction, consumption lowering, balanced resource allocation and iterative evolution in accordance with trends. Relying on four laws of boundary convergence, root cause tracing, phenomenon discrimination and instrument prioritization, it completes system anomaly inspection, fault tracing and error-correcting iteration.

Regardless of industry, hardware/software, virtual/physical nature, any artificial architecture and organizational institution possessing boundaries, input-output, feedback mechanisms, steady-state dissipation and hierarchical dependence can be decomposed and deduced via this Way. It serves as the core intermediate operator connecting individual mental worlds and external human-made creations.
> Corresponding manuscripts: A31 Three Ways and Twelve Disciplines; A31_1-A31_4; A32 AI Autonomy Axiom Series.

#### The Way of Civilization (Humanistic Order｜Joint Construction by Mankind)
Built upon the objective reality constraints of The Way of the World, human groups jointly evolve and generate humanistic order systems by virtue of individual cognitive capabilities and internalized knowledge systems.

It explains the generation mechanism of order with the dynamics of **Pattern - Meta-Rules - Ordinary Rules**, distinguishes jungle meta-rules from civilizational meta-rules, and elaborates internal and external driving conditions for transition from natural cyclic circulation to civilizational steady state. It takes the universal sharing level of primal production factors among all people as the core evaluation criterion of civilizational hierarchy. Civilization ontology is **order building against the current**, with benevolence, righteousness, propriety, wisdom and trust as its underlying foundation, pursuing the advanced symbiotic form of **harmony without uniformity**. The large vessel model is adopted to analyze four secondary social contradictions, clarifying the fundamental contradiction between the impulse of unordered expansion of human groups and the upper carrying limit of nature, and revealing the constraint boundary for the rise and fall of civilizations. This Way studies status allocation, games, institutions, cultural heritage, community formation and the evolutionary rise and fall of civilizations.

> Note: Empirical deduction of civilizational history is categorized into corresponding archival units, carried by *Reflections on the Twenty-Four Histories*. Its underlying deduction foundation originates from The Way of Cognition, The Way of Knowledge and The Way of Systems.
> Corresponding manuscripts: A41-A44 Four closed-loop papers of The Way of Civilization.

#### The Way of the World (Objective Origin｜Base of All Flux and Change)
Independent of human perception, cognition and group consensus, it governs the ultimate objective ontology and evolutionary laws of all existence, serving as the first-principles foundation for all humanistic systems of cognition, knowledge, systems and civilization.

It establishes the ultimate ontological axiom: **"To exist is to change"**. It strictly distinguishes superficial extrinsic mechanical motion from endogenous ontological change, resolves the mind-matter dualism, and reconciles underlying contradictions between macroscopic classical physics and microscopic quantum physics. It defines the dual-coupled internal structure of ordered change, elaborates the apparent form of cyclic propagation, and completes structural classification and mathematical modeling of rheological phenomena. It constructs a dual information entropy evolution system independent of thermodynamic entropy. Based on the reverse restraint law of internal information entropy and identification information entropy, it distinguishes two evolutionary paths: the forward homogenization dissipation of non-living substances and the reverse structured order-building of living beings. Rooted in the essence of all things in flux, it anchors the core principle that tangible matter will eventually dissipate and only ontologically specific structured information can be preserved for a long time. It defines the ultimate value of life as full-cycle reverse construction and precipitation of unique ontological order, accomplishing a full ontological closed-loop from cosmic origin, structural laws and quantitative evolution to the value of life.
> Corresponding manuscripts: Preface A50, A51-A57 The Way of the World series (Change-Substance Ontology).

### 2. The Boundary-Expanding Method (Axiomatics of Engineering Practice: Answering How to Act)
**The Boundary-Expanding Method (Implementation Chapter｜Knowledge-Action Closed Loop｜Global Boundary Breaking)**
It inherits all underlying axioms of the Five Ways: Cognition, Knowledge, Systems, Civilization and the World. As a practical axiom system under the Department of Principle, it is the universal practical method of the entire *Wudao-Yifa* system for implementation, boundary breakthrough and evolutionary iteration.

The first Five Ways build the static cognition and objective law foundation from humanistic minds, artificial systems and group civilizations to cosmic origin. The Boundary-Expanding Method undertakes the ultimate function of **"verifying knowledge through action, expanding boundaries through action, and forming closed loops through action"**, bridging the gap from theoretical axioms to real-world achievements, individual growth, organizational iteration and civilizational advancement.

> The Department of Principle retains only the axiom-level definition of the Boundary-Expanding Method, including ontological axioms of the Four Laws and the ontological axiom of the General Evolution Model of All Things. Interpretations of mental tactics, scenario decomposition, and human-machine collaborative practical explanations are placed in corresponding archival units.

> The Five Ways constitute substance, defining origin, laws, cognition, order and value; the Boundary-Expanding Method serves as application, defining actions, paths, difficulty, efficiency and breakthrough. Substance is established before application functions; knowledge becomes clear before action reaches its destination.

Thus, the Five Ways establish the principles of heaven, earth and humanity; the One Method enables implementation of all affairs. The complete *Wudao-Yifa* achieves an uninterrupted, flawless, multi-dimensional ultimate closed-loop of **Ontology — Cognition — Order — Value — Action — Mathematics**.
