## I. Architecture General Outline

This is a fully endogenous ideological system. All theories, axioms, deductions, methodologies, experiments and records emerge naturally from raw perceptions and thought experiments. Like plants growing from fertile soil, it has no pre-built framework, no external templates and no inherited tradition.

Native cognitive progression chain: **Record ¡ú See ¡ú Logos ¡ú Norm ¡ú Experiment ¡ú Yield**

Brief comparison:

- **Record section**: Raw account of an event such as an apple falling, plus fleeting conjectures at the moment (material snapshots, informal thought notes).
- **See section**: Extract objective factual phenomena from large quantities of such records.
- **Logos section**: Refine underlying axioms and laws.
- **Norm section**: Deduce values and comprehensive interpretations derived from these laws.
- **Experiment section**: Construct thought experiments to test the self-consistency of conjectures.
- **Yield section**: Transform validated theories into practical, engineer-implementable outputs.

It precisely corresponds to the complete closed-loop human cognition: **Observation ¡ú Comprehension ¡ú Definition ¡ú Verification ¡ú Application ¡ú Archiving & Continuity**.

**Iron law of hierarchical flow (irreversible, no cross-boundary, no reverse order):**
See (raw source materials) ¡ú Logos (axiom foundation) ¡ú Norm (value-oriented deduction for all phenomena) ¡ú Experiment (simulation and falsification test) ¡ú Yield (practical boundary-expanding implementation) ¡ú Record (archiving for iteration and inheritance).

**Final-locked English naming for all sections (concept labels only; existing Axx document numbers remain unchanged)**

- See (S) = See-Observation of phenomena
- Logos (L) = Logos-Objective universal order, inherent principles of all things (includes Four Boundary-Expanding Disciplines and the neutral methodology of *Virtue is Self-Defined*)
- Norm (N) = Norm-Human-made value criteria and value formulation (contains three core sub-domains)
  - Humanity (Subcode-B): Individual subjectivity and spiritual values
  - Economy (Subcode-J): Industrial-economic operation and material processes
  - Civilization (Subcode-H): Civilizational evolution and spiritual order
- Experiment (T) = Experiment-Thought-experiment and deductive validation
- Yield (Y) = Yield-Practical outputs and real-world application
- Record (R) = Record-Chronological logs and continuity inheritance (original Chinese meaning: recording)

> Core jurisprudential maxim: *Logos provides the methodology for value-setting; Norm delivers substantive value and moral meaning.*
> The Logos section supplies neutral general rules for defining virtues; the Norm section fills substantive virtues and value curves for the three domains: Humanity, Economy and Civilization.

## II. Precise Definition of Six Tiers (Native final boundaries ¡¤ retains original four-tier core plus two newly finalized tiers)

### 1. See Section [Perception ¡¤ Raw-Observation Layer£üS=See]
**Cognitive position: Observation**

The sole native soil for all ideas within the system. Purely collects objective phenomena, thought-experiment scenarios, snapshots of changing processes and real-world factual events. It only keeps raw records; no interpretation, no insight generation, no axiom establishment and no value judgement. All original observational materials feed into this layer.

- **Core traits**: Primitive, objective, unprocessed, the soil of theory.
- **Core logic**: Only present observed facts; no explanation, no judgement, no theorizing. Aligns with S-series writing paradigm (formerly referred to as J-series): present phenomena first in opening passages.

### 2. Logos Section [Core ¡¤ Rule-Setting Layer£üL=Logos]
**Cognitive position: Comprehension ¡¤ Establishing laws**

The core backbone and foundational origin of the whole system. It extracts original insights from objective materials of the See section and formalizes the system. It embodies objective universal principles and immanent order of all things:

- Establish all meta-axioms and meta-propositions
- Define proprietary symbol systems and formal paradigms
- Unify core terminology and underlying logical rules
- Embed the Four Boundary-Expanding Disciplines including the neutral methodology of *Virtue is Self-Defined* (provides rules for value-setting without assigning concrete values).

- **Core traits**: Establishes axioms, defines boundaries, produces definitions; sole kernel of the system; objective and value-neutral.
- **Core logic**: Acts as the ¡°constitutional layer¡± of this system. Underlying rules must be fixed before any subsequent deduction, value-formulation, experiment and implementation.

### 3. Norm Section [Deduction ¡¤ Value-Formulation Layer£üN=Norm]
**Cognitive position: Definition ¡¤ Value-oriented deduction**

The panoramic extension and value-setting layer for all branches of the system. Strictly built upon objective axioms defined in the Logos section. Human subjects establish value criteria and moral trade-offs, and fill substantive values across three core domains. It conducts valid deduction, in-depth interpretation and pattern deconstruction for external phenomena:

- Humanity (Subcode-B): Definition of individual value, mental growth and human-centered claims
- Economy (Subcode-J): Industrial operation, economic logic and value weighing for the material world
- Civilization (Subcode-H): Civilizational iteration, cultural persistence and construction of spiritual order
- Deduction of cognitive patterns, deconstruction of flux models, interpretation of worldly phenomena, critique of social regularities.

- **Core traits**: Formulates values grounded in Logos; explains diverse phenomena from the kernel; extends without generating foundational axioms; assigns value content.
- **Core logic**: Reuses established Logos-axioms to assign values and interpret the world. Only expands outward and never creates new foundational axioms.

### 4. Experiment Section [Simulation ¡¤ Validation Layer£üT=Experiment]
**Cognitive position: Simulation verification**

The filtering gateway between theory and reality. It receives deductive conclusions output from the Norm section. Through thought-experiments, hypothetical modelling and scenario reasoning, it tests feasibility and self-consistency for all axioms, models and value propositions. Logically-conflicting or invalid conjectures are filtered out before delivery to the Yield section.

- **Core traits**: Does not generate foundational axioms or new values; only performs falsification, validation and screening.
- **Core logic**: Logos sets foundations, Norm conducts deduction, Experiment distinguishes truth from falsity. Conjectures must pass experimental validation before entering practical layers.

### 5. Yield Section [Practice ¡¤ Boundary-Expanding Layer£üY=Yield]
**Cognitive position: Practical application**

The value outlet and boundary-expanding engine of the whole system. Theoretical achievements from See-Logos-Norm-Experiment are transformed into reusable, practical and iterable speculative paradigms:

- Thought-experiment construction methodologies
- Practical paths for cognitive elevation
- Standard speculative workflows of the system
- Principles for implementing and expanding theories.

- **Core traits**: Unity of knowledge and action; guiding technique through principle; boundary-expanding growth; practical and implementable.
- **Core logic**: Theory shall not remain abstract. Outcomes from preceding four tiers are converted into reusable methodologies and engineering deliverables.

### 6. Record Section [Inheritance ¡¤ Archiving Layer£üR=Record]
**Cognitive position: Inheritance & preservation**

The closed-loop feedback node of the whole system. It accepts outputs from See, Logos, Norm, Experiment and Yield. It stores finalized manuscripts, version iterations, draft fragments, thought journals and system-evolution traces. While archiving, it feeds materials back to the See section as new observational input to complete the full cognitive loop.

> Original Chinese meaning: recording. More than static storage; emphasis lies on inheritance, continuation and traceability.

- **Core logic**: All outputs of the workflow are archived for traceability and fed back to upstream cognitive stages.

## III. Six Permanent Boundary Red Lines (bidirectional theory-engineering mapping)

- **See does not establish Logos**
Only stores source materials; does not create axioms.
Engineering mapping: S-series writing: present phenomena first, withhold premature conclusions.

- **Logos does not conduct concrete reasoning**
Only defines core principles; does not perform case-oriented deduction.
Engineering mapping: AI-system constitution: defines only bottom-level constraints and avoids concrete business-logic inference.

- **Norm does not generate foundational roots**
Only extends branches; does not originate foundational principles.
Engineering mapping: inference templates are embedded components and shall not become top-level entry points.

- **Experiment does not modify core axioms**
Only performs validation; must not alter underlying axioms.
Engineering mapping: simulation modules output only true/false judgements; forbidden to modify base symbols or meta-propositions.

- **Yield does not alter fundamental principles**
Only executes application; must not rewrite system kernel.
Engineering mapping: application implementation layer cannot mutate underlying axiom structures during runtime.

- **Record does not generate new theories**
Only receives and archives outputs; does not produce theoretical content.
Engineering mapping: archive & draft repositories serve only for storage and feedback; not treated as theoretical-production entry.

> Annotation: These constraints distilled from past engineering pitfalls are concrete instantiations of the six red lines within engineering practice.

## V. Document Classification Guidance

This chapter provides classification instructions for all documents of the system, defining content scope of each section as the archival-sorting standard.

1. **See section**: Records of objective phenomena, real-world snapshots and raw thought-experiments. Presents facts only; no axiom-building and no value judgements.
2. **Logos section**: Meta-axioms, meta-propositions, symbolic-mathematical definitions, terminology specifications and foundational rules establishing the whole system.
3. **Norm section**: All deductions, analyses and critiques built upon Logos-axioms. Interprets cognition, flux, civilisation, economy and social phenomena. Extends without generating new foundational origins.
4. **Experiment section**: Thought-experiments, hypothetical modelling and multi-scenario reasoning. Tests self-consistency and feasibility of models, axioms and value propositions.
5. **Yield section**: Speculative methodologies, practical paradigms, engineering implementations, case reviews and axiom-to-application transformations. Acts as the value-realization outlet of the system.
6. **Record section**: Archiving of finalized texts, drafts, versions, thought-journals and evolutionary traces. Receives full-workflow outputs and feeds back to upstream layers.

> **Classification principle**: If one manuscript covers multiple layers, classify it according to its originating standpoint. Observational materials ¡ú See; axiom definitions ¡ú Logos; interpretive deduction ¡ú Norm; simulation validation ¡ú Experiment; practical implementation ¡ú Yield; process traces ¡ú Record.
