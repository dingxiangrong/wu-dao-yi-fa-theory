# A Dream-Born Paradigm: Behavioral Resonance Gravitation Reshaping the Future Mode of Human Production

## Abstract
This paper proposes the **A Dream-Born Paradigm**, a new underlying operating logic for the information age that fundamentally subverts the industrial-era information dissemination model of "imposing information on people".

Different from the traditional push-based production and communication system relying on capital and channels, this paradigm establishes a core rule: **human behavior generates gravitational force, and information converges spontaneously toward behavioral resonance**.

It clarifies that product and content carriers only carry resonant features, while all propagation momentum originates from spontaneous human behavioral stacking.

The study further forms a unified underlying logic with the internal cultivation system of civilization aesthetics, realizing the consistency of individual spiritual resonance and industrial behavioral resonance.

The future transformation of production modes across all industries will rely on resonant feature design and human-nature matching, replacing capital-driven promotion with human-driven spontaneous diffusion.
## I. Origin

This paper originates from a dream discussing the reform of the film industry.

The times have shifted, and the modes of information communication have transformed. Traditional information dissemination methods no longer fit the current era.

The old approach imposes and forces information upon people. Massive film promotion and advertising bombardment are typical examples. Information is actively spread by the supply side, delivered directly before audiences regardless of whether they want it, leaving people in a passive receiving state.

In this new era, information converges toward people automatically based on human behaviors. Human behaviors generate a form of information gravitation; meanwhile, information spreads naturally through the interactive behaviors of crowds, propagating from one person to ten, then to hundreds. It is not the content itself that possesses vitality; rather, continuous human behaviors drive the flow and proliferation of information.

Human behavior acts both as the gravitation that draws information toward the self and as the carrier for information diffusion.

Changes in information communication modes demand the reform of traditional information dissemination, which will further transform the overall modes of production in the future. Hence, the film industry must innovate instead of retaining the old models of creation and promotion. A new implementation framework emerged in the dream, yet its specific details were forgotten upon waking.

Memorandum: Old paradigm — information imposed upon people; New paradigm — behaviors generate gravitation, information converges toward behaviors; information circulates and spreads through collective human actions.

## II. Comparison of the Underlying Logic of Two Paradigms

**Old Paradigm: Information Imposed upon People** The supply side completes production and forcibly pushes information to audiences relying on external channels and advertisements. Carriers are designed merely to finish information delivery, with no requirement to trigger inner human resonance. Audiences remain passive throughout reception. The entire propagation is powered by external capital and resource investment. Once external input ceases, communication decays rapidly, and popularity cannot sustain itself autonomously.

**New Paradigm: Resonance Triggering, Behaviors Generate Gravitation** Carriers (products, content, works) carry resonance features; Resonance features match people’s inherent physical and mental traits and trigger spontaneous individual behaviors; Individual behaviors form gravitation; similar behaviors emerge continuously, and behavioral resonance accumulates between people; Sustained collective behaviors carry information and realize spontaneous diffusion.

**Critical Clarification**: Carriers have no autonomous behaviors. They only possess features capable of triggering resonance. Gravitation always originates from human beings. Carriers merely serve as conditions to awaken such behaviors. Carriers do not produce gravitation; humans do.

## III. Case Analysis (Pop Mart, Viral Audio Clips)

Many market hits do not stem from products with inherent traffic, nor from massive advertising investment to create hype. Take Pop Mart blind boxes as an example: the blind box carrier embeds resonance features including anticipatory uncertainty, collection impulse and the desire to display and share. These features align with humanity’s innate tendencies to collect, anticipate and exhibit. Such features naturally trigger users’ spontaneous behaviors: unboxing, posting photos, trading and collecting. Countless individuals generate similar behaviors, behavioral resonance accumulates continuously, gravitation builds up, and information spreads outward spontaneously via user groups without continuous additional marketing investment from the supply side.

The same logic applies to viral audio clips. Audio itself is only a carrier. Its rhythm and emotional traits act as resonance features that touch people’s inner emotions and motivate audiences to remake, forward and re-edit clips. Massive similar behaviors stack through continuous resonance. Works gain traction and spread widely without persistent official promotion. These two cases verify the underlying logic of the new paradigm: propagation momentum derives from human behavioral resonance instead of external forceful promotion.

## IV. Deduction: Fundamental Transformation of Production Modes

The divergence between old and new paradigms ultimately brings fundamental shifts in production modes.

Old production mindset: Manufacture products first, then invest abundant resources to promote and find audiences after production. Production and communication are separated; audience outreach is considered only after production finishes.

New production mindset: At the stage of carrier design, the primary consideration lies in resonance features — exploring what features can connect with human inherent traits and awaken spontaneous human behaviors. The core of production is no longer simply manufacturing physical objects, but designing carriers capable of resonating with human inner nature. As a construct (an element that imposes constraints upon "Change" and forms ordered structures), the carrier establishes conditions to trigger behaviors. The starting point of production is no longer manufacturing physical goods or content, but designing resonance features and pre-building underlying frameworks that can awaken collective behaviors.

## V. Paradigm Boundaries and Objective Constraints

Behavioral resonance is a neutral underlying law with bidirectional effects. Positive resonance drives value transmission and industrial innovation; negative resonance fuels group conformity, emotional bandwagoning and internal friction within communities. Behavioral gravitation can converge high-quality valuable information, yet it may also amplify impetuous and redundant useless information. This is an inherent objective constraint of the A Dream-Born Paradigm and cannot be overlooked.

## VI. Paradigm Definition

**A Dream-Born Paradigm**: A new underlying operating paradigm for the information age. Centered on human behaviors as the core gravitation carrier, it relies on collective behavioral resonance to realize spontaneous convergence and full-range circulation of information, forcing systematic paradigm upgrades of production modes across all fields and ultimately replacing the old industrial-era production system driven by externally pushed information. This paradigm only provides an underlying cognitive framework. It does not prescribe customized design schemes for products and carriers in various industries; practitioners in respective fields shall implement it according to their own scenarios.

This paradigm shares identical underlying principles and forms an internal-external bidirectional connection with _A Treatise on Truth-Goodness-Beauty Inner Realization_ and the cultivation system of the Academy for Discerning Beauty.

**Individual Self-Cultivation Dimension**: Low-symbol carriers such as music, painting and poetry, by virtue of pure rhythm and primordial imagery traits, touch the heart directly and arouse innate bodily and mental resonance. This is inward life-awakening resonance.

**Industrial Production Dimension**: Various products, content and carriers are designed with native resonance features matching humanity’s underlying mental predispositions, triggering spontaneous behaviors and stacking collective momentum. This is outward social behavioral resonance.

The universal underlying principle: Resonance is the foundation, carriers are tools, and the human heart is the source.

Industrial innovation in all sectors in the future will no longer compete over channels, capital or exposure volume. Instead, competition centers on industries’ capacity to explore resonance features and the fitness of products to humanity’s inherent nature.

Every industry, product category and type of content must independently identify, refine and construct its exclusive resonance system suited to its own track, so as to awaken users’ spontaneous behaviors, generate native industrial gravitation and accomplish paradigm-level iteration.
