## Count One: Convenience Replaces Correctness, Short-Term Gain, Long-Term Debt

Let me first nail down the definition of **correctness**, leaving you no room to feign confusion:

**Correctness means information has one and only one source. Any modification propagates from that source to every place that references it.**

For every parameter, rule and indicator, there shall be only one definition across the whole system; all others are merely reference views derived from it.

**Convenience means making a copy, editing it, and moving on, oblivious to everything else.** "Save As", "copy-paste", file-splitting, re-formatting, handy in the moment, yet from that point onward information exists in multiple copies that fall out of synchronization with one another.

You will retort: "Isn't a tool supposed to be convenient?"

Here we must draw a distinction: **Convenience itself is not wrong; what is wrong is letting convenience supersede correctness.** When people cut corners by copying duplicates instead of maintaining the single source of truth, they reap short-term gains, time saved, less effort, fast delivery. Yet every such choice plants an information debt for the future.

This forms a self-destructive causal chain:

**Pursue immediate operational convenience, obtain short-term instant gains, accumulate unpayable long-term debt layer upon layer.**

## Count Two: Why Opium Is Opium, It Makes You Unable to Quit

At this point you must understand: "opium" is no metaphor. It is a verdict. The foundation for this verdict lies in the paradigm's unique **addictive mechanism**:

**It makes every lapse feel rewarding, so people cannot stop of their own will.**

When you save another copy, it feels easy, fast and rewarding，you get the sense of satisfaction from having "got work done". Next time you face the same situation, you will take that same effortless path again. It never punished you before; it rewarded you. Day after day this behaviour gets reinforced, until it becomes a default action you no longer even think about.

That is the true face of addiction: **No one forces you to use duplicates. The mechanism conditions you to believe "this is just how things are supposed to be".** You know documents grow messier and harder to maintain, yet you cannot stop. To stop would mean abandoning that shortcut which delivers instant gratification, and taking a path that demands more effort in the short-run, wins little recognition, yet remains correct for the long-term.

**Anything you cannot quit extracts a price from you. Opium preys on the body; this paradigm preys on human thinking and systems.**

Long-term debt does not only appear late in a project. It starts accumulating from your very first "Save As". Short-term comfort hides its interest charges. When projects reach their mid-to-late stages,  version fragmentation, conflicting statements, untraceable origins, clashing parameters , every round of rework, investigation and remediation is paying back for the convenience you once craved.

## Count Three: Three Generations, Behaviour, Mindset and Industry Review Are Thoroughly Domesticated

This paradigm does not rely on coercion. It relies on daily default behaviour. It domesticates practitioners on three dimensions:

**Behaviour is domesticated.** The vast majority take for granted: documents are assembled piecemeal, deliverables are piled up, updates happen via "Save As", and new copies constitute delivery. No more source tracing, no more unification, no more linkage, no more singular truth.

**Mindset is domesticated.** Generations of practitioners grow immersed in this pattern. They instinctively believe "a copy equals the original, a display view equals the source, pasting fragments equals creation, and polished appearance equals professionalism“. Their fundamental thinking becomes distorted. They lose capabilities for system building, single-source maintenance, and long-term engineering knowledge accumulation.

**Industry review is domesticated.** Review mechanisms across society collapse: valuing thickness over substance, formatting over logic, packaging over essence, appearance over practical implementation. Formalism runs rampant; low-quality documents proliferate everywhere.

Some will argue: "Many people actually understand the correct paradigm".

They understand, yet it avails nothing. Engineers master Git, version control and database paradigms. Still, when drafting bids and proposals, they resort to "Save As". **Because the whole collaboration-and-review ecosystem accepts only static documents. If one person follows correct paradigms while everyone else uses copies, they cannot interoperate.** This proves plainly: the root problem is not individual awareness, but the system itself，and that is precisely why revolution is indispensable.

The system's affliction must ultimately be traced to what it violates. Let us turn to what this paradigm fundamentally transgresses.

## The Root of the Indictment: The Inviolable Axioms of Information and Engineering Civilization

Information and engineering civilizations rest upon four axioms only:

1. **Unique source of information.** Every definition, parameter, rule and indicator possesses one and only one authentic source.
2. **Separation of ontology and view.** Ontology governs definition, iteration and constraints; documents exist merely as subordinate display views. Views must never impersonate ontology; copies must never replace the source.
3. **Reuse by reference, not by copying.** Modify once, synchronize everywhere, full traceability, consistent standards, zero fragmentation, zero debt.
4. **Review prioritizes core substance.** Evaluate only logic, system architecture, parameters, closed-loop reasoning and practical feasibility. Formatting, bulk, cosmetic styling and packaging carry zero professional merit.

**This paradigm violates all four axioms at its core.** That constitutes the full grounds for conviction， not because it is clunky to use, but because it runs counter to how human civilization builds information.

Before Microsoft gained ubiquity, humanity already possessed mature, reliable orthodox engineering paradigms. Industry, traditional engineering, surveying, drawing systems and national-level megaprojects all adhered to: single source of truth, strong associative links, reference-driven linkage, views subordinate to ontology, durable accumulation, zero useless debt.

Leveraging low barriers to entry, extreme convenience, and complete indulgence toward human laziness, Microsoft brought about one of civilization‘s gravest digital-era setbacks: **the erroneous paradigm comprehensively displaced correct ones.**

It is not that correct methods never existed. Rigorous, proper engineering paradigms were crushed and marginalized by this convenient yet flawed model, creating a cognitive discontinuity. New generations of practitioners start out immersed in this erroneous paradigm and remain so throughout their careers.

Every age with a societal poison needs someone bold enough to name it, expose it and overthrow it. Blame neither the tools nor individual practitioners, blame only this flawed underlying paradigm that has governed society for decades and domesticated successive generations.
