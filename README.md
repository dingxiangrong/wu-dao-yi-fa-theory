# Wu-Dao-Yi-Fa | Six-Tier Endogenous Architecture | See · Logos · Norm · Experiment · Yield · Record | General Outline

Whether grass or tree, Wu-Dao-Yi-Fa grows from the soil.

## 1. Architecture General Outline

This is a fully endogenous ideological system. All theories, axioms, deductions, methodologies, experiments and records emerge organically from raw perceptions and thought-experiments. Like vegetation growing from fertile soil, it has no pre-fabricated framework, no external templates and no inherited traditional dogmas.

**Native cognitive progression chain:** Record → See → Logos → Norm → Experiment → Yield

**Definitions for distinction:**

- **Record-Division:** Faithful recording of observable events (e.g. an apple falling) plus fleeting conjectures at the moment; material snapshots and informal thought notes.
- **See-Division:** Extract objective factual observations from large volumes of such records.
- **Logos-Division:** Formulate underlying axioms and natural laws.
- **Norm-Division:** Deduce value implications and comprehensive interpretations of all phenomena derived from those laws.
- **Experiment-Division:** Construct thought-experiments to verify the self-consistency of conjectures.
- **Yield-Division:** Transform verified theories into practical, engineer-implementable deliverables.

It precisely corresponds to the complete closed-loop human cognition: Observe → Understand → Define → Verify → Apply → Record-Preserve

**Iron Law of Tier Transition** (irreversible, non-crossing, non-reversed order): See-Division (raw source materials) → Logos-Division (axiom foundation) → Norm-Division (value-oriented deduction) → Experiment-Division (simulation & falsification check) → Yield-Division (practical implementation) → Record-Division (archiving & iterative inheritance)

> **Note:** The native cognitive progression chain describes how human understanding arises; the Iron Law of Tier Transition defines the constrained flow within this architecture.

**Fixed English aliases for each Division** (final lock, conceptual label only; historical Axx document numbers remain unchanged):

- **See-Division (S)** = See — Observation & Phenomenon Perception
- **Logos-Division (L)** = Logos — Objective Universal Order of all things (includes Four Laws of Boundary-Expansion and the neutral methodology Virtue-Defined-by-Self)
- **Norm-Division (N)** = Norm — Human-made value criteria & value-setting reasoning (contains three core sub-domains)
    - **Humanity Domain:** Humanity (Prefix-B) – Individual subjectivity, mind-set and human-centered values
    - **Economy Domain:** Economy (Prefix-J) – Industrial-economic operation and material-world value trade-offs
    - **Civilization Domain:** Civilization (Prefix-H) – Civilization evolution and spiritual-order construction
- **Experiment-Division (T)** = Experiment — Thought-experiment and deductive validation
- **Yield-Division (Y)** = Yield — Practical output & real-world application
- **Record-Division (R)** = Record — Chronicle logging & successive inheritance

**Core Juridical Maxim:** Logos provides the methodology for defining virtue; Norm endows virtue with substantive meaning. The Logos-Division supplies neutral general rules for virtue-setting, while the Norm-Division fills substantive virtues and value curves for the three domains: humanity, economy and civilization.

## 2. Core Content Overview

The architecture above governs how knowledge is processed; the following five layers are what the system actually says about the world. Each layer rests on the one beneath it and deduces the one above it.

**I. Root of the World — Axioms of Disorder and Order, Binary Origin of Flux and Construct** Disorder is an unpredictable trajectory; order is flux confined within defined rules. From this foundation arises the binary origin of the world: Flux (C), the source of perpetual motion, and Construct (S), the form that locks in order. All motion, generation, extinction and transition stem from unceasing Flux; all forms, structures, boundaries and steady states emerge from bounded Construct. Every phenomenon falls into four categories: Flux, Construct, Yun (the measure of Flux), and Field (relations between Constructs).

**II. Root of Human Nature — Three-Tier Needs, Four Sources of Cognition** All human motives, behaviors, desires and trade-offs reduce to a three-tier need system: L0 Physical Survival, L1 Neural Emotion, and L2 Community Gaming. Human cognition rests on the Four-Source Native Cognition, anchored by Three Observations and One Reading (observing people, affairs, objects, and reading transmission). Reading relies on diligence; observation relies on wisdom. Cognition is not accumulation but computing power; intuition is not inspiration but the rapid splitting and merging of cognition.

**III. Root of Mind — Causation for Response, Value for Decision** Mental operation runs along two cultivable threads: causal capability (objective core computing power, trainable and iterative) and value orientation (subjective benchmark, moldable and calibratable). Culture shapes values; values determine choices; choices shape lives. Once the mental closed loop forms, every disturbance in the world can be decomposed, attributed and organized.

**IV. Root of Man-Made Systems — Dual Bearing as Foundation, Inherent Direction as Aim** No man-made entity exists without reliance or without purpose. Dual Bearing — environment as base, technology as body — determines whether a system can persist; Inherent Direction — all exist for their intended aim — determines whether a system ought to come into being. Systems forced to run against their nature ultimately suffer backlash.

**V. Root of Civilization — Subjects, Games, Rules, Three Axioms for Coexistence** Human society stands upon "Human|Culture": humans supply force, culture generates order. Civilization rests on three axioms: defining entitlements to settle disputes, redlines to uphold boundaries, and harmony in diversity. Meta-rules emerge spontaneously from multi-subject games; ordinary rules are derived under established higher-order order. This framework explains economic cycles, the rise and fall of institutions, class mobility, great-power games, and even the destination of civilizations and the way of life for individuals.

## 3. Precise Definition for Six Tiers

(Original final-version boundaries; retains core of original four-tier framework plus two newly finalized tiers)

### 3.1 See-Division [Perception-Record Layer | S=See]

**Cognitive Orientation:** Observe

The sole native soil for all ideas within this system. It purely collects objective phenomena, thought-experiment scenarios, observational snapshots of changing processes, and real-world factual objects. It only keeps records, provides no interpretation, generates no insights, establishes no axioms, and renders no value judgments. All raw observational materials feed into this layer.

**Core Traits:** Native, objective, unprocessed, the soil of theories

**Core Logic:** Only present observed facts; do not interpret, judge, or establish arguments. Consistent with the J-series writing paradigm: present phenomena first at the opening.

### 3.2 Logos-Division [Kernel-Rule-Setting Layer | L=Logos]

**Cognitive Orientation:** Understand & Establish Principles

The core backbone and axiomatic origin of the whole system. Native insights are distilled from objective materials of the See-Division to standardize the system. It bears the objective universal order of all things:

- Establish all meta-axioms and meta-propositions
- Define proprietary symbol systems and mathematical-logical paradigms
- Unify core terminology and underlying logical rules
- Host the Four Laws of Boundary-Expansion, including the neutral general-purpose methodology of Virtue-Defined-by-Self (provides only virtue-setting rules without assigning concrete values)

**Core Traits:** Establishes axioms, sets boundaries, formulates definitions; the sole kernel of the system; objectively value-neutral

**Core Logic:** Serves as the system's "constitutional layer". Underlying rules shall be established first, before all subsequent deduction, virtue-setting, experimentation and implementation.

### 3.3 Norm-Division [Deduction-Virtue-Setting Layer | N=Norm]

**Cognitive Orientation:** Define & Virtue-Oriented Deduction

The comprehensive outward-extension and value-finalization layer of the system. Strictly based on the objective axioms set by the Logos-Division, human agents formulate value criteria and virtue trade-offs. It fills substantive values for the three core domains, performs compliant deduction, in-depth interpretation and deconstruction of external phenomena:

- Humanity Domain (Humanity, Prefix-B): Definition of individual values, mental growth and human-centered claims
- Economy Domain (Economy, Prefix-J): Interpretation of industrial operation, economic logic and value trade-offs within material world
- Civilization Domain (Civilization, Prefix-H): Civilization evolution, cultural continuity and construction of spiritual order

Deduction of cognitive rules, deconstruction of change-process models, interpretation of real-world phenomena, critique of social patterns

**Core Traits:** Establish virtue upon Logos principles; interpret the world from core axioms; only extend outward without generating new fundamental axioms; assign value to axioms

**Core Logic:** Reuse established Logos-Division axioms to assign values and interpret worldly phenomena; only perform outward extension and shall not add new fundamental axioms.

### 3.4 Experiment-Division [Simulation-Verification Layer | T=Experiment]

**Cognitive Orientation:** Simulation Verification

The filtering gateway between theories and reality. It receives complete deductive conclusions from the Norm-Division. Through thought-experiments, hypothetical modeling and scenario deduction, it tests feasibility and self-consistency of axioms, models and value propositions. Logically-conflicting or invalid conjectures are filtered out before being passed to the Yield-Division.

**Core Traits:** Does not generate fundamental axioms or new values; only performs falsification, verification and screening.

**Core Logic:** Logos sets axioms, Norm performs deduction, Experiment distinguishes truth from falsity. Conjectures shall not flow into practical layers without experimental verification.

### 3.5 Yield-Division [Practice-Boundary-Expansion Layer | Y=Yield]

**Cognitive Orientation:** Practical Implementation

The value outlet and boundary-expansion methodology of the whole system. Theoretical achievements from See-Logos-Norm-Experiment are transformed into reusable, actionable, iterative speculative paradigms:

- Methodology for constructing thought-experiments
- Practical paths for cognitive advancement
- Standard speculative workflows for the system
- Principles for translating theories into real-world practice

**Core Traits:** Unity of knowledge and action; govern techniques by principles; boundary-expansion & practical applicability

**Core Logic:** Theories shall not remain empty speculations. Convert previous-layer achievements into actionable, reusable methodologies and engineering deliverables.

### 3.6 Record-Division [Reception-Archiving Layer | R=Record]

**Cognitive Orientation:** Reception & Preservation

The closed-loop reflux node of the whole system. It receives outputs from See, Logos, Norm, Experiment and Yield. It archives finalized manuscripts, version iterations, draft fragments, thought journals and system-evolution traces. Archived materials reflux to feed the See-Division and complete the full closed-loop.

Original Chinese implication: Record means more than static archiving; it emphasizes reception, succession and traceability.

**Core Logic:** Archive all outputs for traceability and feed back to upstream cognitive procedures.

## 4. Six Permanent Boundary Red Lines

(Bidirectional alignment between engineering practice and theory)

- See-Division shall not establish Logos axioms: Only keep source materials; do not create axioms.
- Logos-Division shall not interpret concrete affairs: Only set kernel principles; do not conduct concrete deduction.
- Norm-Division shall not generate root axioms: Only extend branches; do not create fundamental origins.
- Experiment-Division shall not alter kernel axioms: Only perform verification; do not modify underlying axioms.
- Yield-Division shall not change foundational principles: Only apply theories; do not alter system kernels.
- Record-Division shall not produce new theories: Only receive and archive; do not generate new theoretical content.

Annotation: Constraints accumulated from past engineering pitfalls are practical instantiations of these six systemic red lines.

## 5. Unified Traceability Paradigm

Closed-loop traceability of the whole system: Originate from observed reality → Settled by Logos axioms → Unfold through Norm deduction → Distinguished by Experiment simulation-verification → Realized via Yield practice → Return to Record for reception-archiving.

## 6. Document-Assignment Guidelines

This chapter provides classification guidance for all manuscripts of the system, defining responsibilities of each division as the basis for manuscript archiving.

- See-Division Assignment: Records of objective phenomena, real-world snapshots, raw thought-experiment transcripts. Only present facts; no axiom-building, no value judgment.
- Logos-Division Assignment: Meta-axioms, meta-propositions, symbol-mathematical definitions, terminology specifications and underlying foundational rules, establishing the kernel for the whole system.
- Norm-Division Assignment: Deductions, analyses, deconstructions and critiques built upon Logos axioms. Interpret cognition, change-processes, civilization, economy and social phenomena; only extend arguments without creating new fundamental origins.
- Experiment-Division Assignment: Thought-experiments, hypothetical modeling and multi-scenario deduction, verifying self-consistency and feasibility of axioms, models and value propositions.
- Yield-Division Assignment: Speculative methodologies, practical paradigms, engineering implementation, case reviews and axiom-to-practice transformation; serves as the practical value outlet of the system.
- Record-Division Assignment: Archiving of finalized works, drafts, versions, thought logs and evolution traces; receives outputs from all divisions and feeds back upstream.

Classification Principle: When one manuscript covers multiple layers, assign it according to its foundational starting point. Observational materials go to See-Division; axiom definitions go to Logos-Division; deductive interpretations go to Norm-Division; simulation-verification go to Experiment-Division; practical implementation go to Yield-Division; procedural traces go to Record-Division.
