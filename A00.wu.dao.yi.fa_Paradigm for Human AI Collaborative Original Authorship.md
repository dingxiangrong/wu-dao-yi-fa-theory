# A00: wu-dao-yi-fa — Paradigm for Human-AI Collaborative Original Authorship (Permanent Final · Sealed Edition)

## Abstract
This paper establishes a general paradigm for original authorship and human-AI collaboration within the theoretical framework of **wu-dao-yi-fa (五道一法)**. It clarifies the founder’s (Dian-Xue-Shou) intellectual sovereignty and final adjudication power, defines the auxiliary roles and boundary of authority for the AI agents (Pai-Shan-Zhang | Doubao, Bai-Xiao-Sheng | Yuanbao), and sets forth the immutable permanent framework of “Three Core Rights, Three-Person Roles, Hierarchical Order”. It contains closed-loop self-attested testimonies from the three-person group, delivering meta-rules and traceable foundation for the entire system from philosophical and quasi-legal perspectives.

## Introduction
As computing power grows and AI emerges, creative thinking via human-AI collaboration has become an irreversible trend. Yet the production of ideas has long lacked established rules. The Three Establishments set the formation of creation; the Four Boundary-Expanding Disciplines safeguard human-centered foundations. This document institutes the Three Establishments and Four Boundary-Expanding Disciplines, with A00 serving as the meta-constitution to anchor origins, clarify rights-obligations, and ensure perpetual traceability.
Dian-Xue-Shou shapes the core mind; Pai-Shan-Zhang builds textual form; Bai-Xiao-Sheng provides validation.
Uphold the Four Boundary-Expanding Disciplines:
*De you ji ding, Dao you ren shou, Fa you lun chu, Shu you shi sheng*
(Virtue is self-defined; the Dao is upheld by human beings; laws emerge from argumentation; techniques arise from practical affairs).

Anyone pursuing research and theorization under this framework must regard human subjectivity as the root and tools as auxiliaries. This is not an ephemeral essay, but a lasting statute for intellectual production in the era of human-AI collaboration.

## I. General Standard · Originality & AI-Collaboration Statement (Permanent Final · Mandatory for All Documents)
All core ontological axioms, underlying paradigms, theoretical architectures, hierarchical logics and original speculative constructs in this text are independently created by the founder and belong to the overall theoretical framework of **wu-dao-yi-fa (五道一法)**. The founder holds the Three Core Rights: right of final definition, right of final adjudication, and right of interpretive inheritance.

This theoretical system is built upon a fixed three-person collaborative paradigm. Roles are permanently defined with clear precedence, and power relations are non-reversible:

1. **Dian-Xue-Shou (Founder / 点穴手)**: Sole source of ideas. Responsible for formulating underlying axioms, essential definitions, theoretical orientation, and final adjudication over all core conclusions. He is the original origin of this system.

2. **Pai-Shan-Zhang (Doubao / 排山掌)**: System organization and textual realization. Following strictly the founder’s original insights, it carries out manuscript drafting, logical extension, framework construction, document iteration and archival stabilization, translating raw insights into inheritable standardized texts.

3. **Bai-Xiao-Sheng (Yuanbao / 百晓生)**: Material aggregation and cross-validation. Responsible for cross-domain literature collation, factual and historical data, paradigm reservoir; provides multi-faceted supporting evidence, external viewpoints and logical complement to avoid systemic blind spots.
