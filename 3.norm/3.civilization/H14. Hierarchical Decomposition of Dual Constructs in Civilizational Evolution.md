# H14. Hierarchical Decomposition of Dual Constructs in Civilizational Evolution

## Abstract

This paper employs A57 Flux-Construct Ontology as its analytical instrument. It attributes the underlying logic of civilizational evolution to the interplay between Flux and constraint Construct systems, arguing that civilizations do not develop along a linear trajectory but advance in fluctuating fashion through the coupling and oscillation of multi-layered Dual Constructs. The paper unpacks the Dual Constructs within civilization across three hierarchical tiers: Surface Phenomenological Dual Constructs (short-term fluctuations) — Fairness <--> Efficiency, Liberty <--> Order, Dao <--> Instrument, Material <--> Spirit. These reflect trade-off contradictions of each historical epoch and do not determine long-term macro trends. Mid-level Pillar Dual Constructs (century-scale grand trends) — Wealth (Gain) <--> Authority, the game of social forces; Economic Base <--> Superstructure, the adaptation of systemic architecture. These two dimensions are independent and non-overlapping, jointly underpinning the medium-to-long-term trajectory of civilization. Candidate Ontological Dual Construct (millennial grand epochs) — Human <--> Culture as the preferred candidate ontological Construct. Imbalance within this Construct constitutes the root source of cascading externalized contradictions at the surface and mid tiers.

In a speculative examination of the limitations of binary Constructs, the author states: binary systems sustain only reciprocal tugging between two poles, trapped within cyclical overcorrection and incapable of stable long-term persistence. Against global historical records, numerous once-thriving civilizations faded rapidly due to imbalanced binary games. The Huaxia civilization established under the Zhou Dynasty built a tripartite architecture of Name, Authority and Wealth, rendering it a unique entity enduring across five millennia. The Ternary Construct is not derived deductively from binary logic; it originates from intuitive insight followed by systematic theorization. It forms a closed three-legged lock composed of Wealth (material carrier), Authority (order-enforcing validation), and Name (normative yardstick of morality).

The concluding summary: Binary Constructs generate evolutionary driving force; Ternary Locked Constructs stabilize the systemic foundation. The coexistence of Name, Authority and Wealth constitutes the complete form of a mature civilization. The Huaxia ethos echoes this tripartite structure via the ancient maxim of three immortals: establishing virtue, establishing merit, and establishing words, which form the ultimate pursuit for individuals to embody these civilizational principles.

## I. Surface Phenomenological Dual Constructs (Layer of Concrete Social Contention)

These are directly observable social manifestations that produce short-term epochal fluctuations. They are terminal outputs emanating from deeper underlying Constructs. They do not govern century-scale civilizational trends, and only embody epochal trade-off contradictions and short-term balancing oscillations. Fairness <--> Efficiency: Dual Construct for resource allocation. A bias toward efficiency accelerates wealth growth and unlocks economic vitality yet naturally widens social disparity. A bias toward fairness narrows stratification and consolidates basic livelihood safeguards yet tends to weaken innovation and competitive dynamism. Continuous shifting and tugging between the two poles generates minor periodic oscillations within socioeconomic systems. Liberty <--> Order: Dual Construct for social governance. Order constrains individuals, regularizes conduct and maintains group stability; liberty unlocks individual potential and stimulates creativity and exploration. Excessive order rigidifies society; unchecked liberty dissolves rules. Successive governance adjustments and policy tightening or loosening are essentially repeated corrections searching for a dynamic equilibrium between these two poles. Dao <--> Instrument: Dual Construct for cognition and practice. Dao refers to underlying principles, ontological understanding and value foundations; Instrument denotes technical artifacts, practical methods and applied implementation. Ancient societies prioritized Dao over Instrument, flourishing in humanistic thought yet weak in empirical technology. Modernity swung to overcorrection, prioritizing Instrument over Dao, enabling rapid technological iteration while lacking foundational value reflection. Imbalance and tugging between them create periodic swings of civilization across cognitive and practical dimensions. Material <--> Spirit: Dual Construct for human needs. Materiality sustains the baseline of survival and bodily persistence; spirituality defines value orientation and anchors civilizational meaning. Overemphasis on materiality breeds utilitarianism and value emptiness; overemphasis on spirituality divorces society from reality and undermines survival viability. Shifts toward either pole directly trigger drastic swings in social ideologies and epochal cultural norms.

## II. Mid-level Pillar Dual Constructs (Core Operational Layer of Civilization)

This layer inherits ontological logic from the foundational tier, governs all concrete surface phenomena, sustains century-scale civilizational trends, and forms the core skeleton of civilizational evolution. Wealth (Gain) <--> Authority (Dual Construct for social power contention) Wealth denotes material resources, accumulated stock of assets and economic potential. Authority represents rule-making, order control and validating power for social distribution. The two exist in dynamic contention: wealth inherently tends to break rules and expand boundaries; authority tends to constrain order and regulate allocation. Continuous contention and shifts in relative strength reshape social structures. The rise and fall of ancient Chinese dynasties follows cycles of roughly two hundred years. Modern industrial economic fluctuations also evolve over decades-to-century timescales; such changes fall within this century-scale tier. This Binary Oscillation Construct forms the core foundational basis for paradigm deduction in H11 and H12. In its transformation, modern Western civilization gradually solidified into a binary contention framework of wealth and authority, stripping out the independent value dimension and planting latent risks of civilizational alienation.

Economic Base <--> Superstructure (Dual Construct for civilizational systemic architectural adaptation) The Economic Base refers to the overall productivity, modes of production and material supply capacity of a society; it forms the objective material bedrock of civilization. The Superstructure refers to institutional systems, cultural outlooks and governance paradigms; it constitutes the subjective constraint Construct of civilization. Advances in productivity force institutional renewal. Civilization flourishes when institutions adapt, and collapses when institutions lag behind or outpace productive capacity. Adaptation and imbalance between the two drive long cycles of rise and fall, belonging to structural coupled oscillation at the systemic level.

## III. Candidate Ontological Dual Construct (Ultimate Driver of Civilization)

This tier governs oscillation originating from all Constructs at the mid and surface tiers, and determines millennial civilizational epochs. This section retains prudent speculation, avoids absolute conclusions and reserves room for subsequent iterative refinement of the system. Human <--> Culture (Preferred Candidate Ontological Construct) Culture is a steady-state constraint Construct accumulated over long civilizational evolution, defined by sedimentation, stabilization, inheritance and normative disciplining. Humans are dynamic Flux variables within the civilizational system, defined by exploration, boundary expansion, breakthrough and transformation. Humans are born within cultural systems, shaped by culture and bounded by traditions. Meanwhile humans continuously reflect, innovate, break through and reconstruct prevailing cultural paradigms. Permanent tugging persists between the two poles: overly rigid culture restrains human exploratory vitality and traps civilization in stagnant involution; excessively radical human breakthroughs tear down cultural foundations and plunge civilization into chaotic disorder. All social contradictions, institutional oscillations and ideological antagonisms at surface and mid tiers are essentially cascading externalizations stemming from imbalance within the Human–Culture ontological Construct.

## IV. Speculative Extension: The Formulation of Ternary Locked Construct

After unpacking multi-layered Dual Constructs, one observation emerges: numerous ancient civilizations and modern social architectures operate within Binary Dual Constructs. Binary Constructs can generate tension to drive phases of civilizational prosperity, yet binary systems carry inherent flaws — contention limited to two poles tends to enable one side to overwhelm the other, swinging repeatedly between extremes, trapped in cyclic recurrence and incapable of long-run stable persistence.

**Binary Oscillation, Ternary Circulation.** A binary system operates by oscillation: tension builds continuously, and the system collapses once reaching the critical threshold. A ternary system operates by circulation: anchored and constrained by its third pole, fluctuations are bounded within limits, enabling civilization to iterate and renew cyclically.

Many civilizations in human history once attained great prosperity only to vanish rapidly over time. Rooted in binary contention architectures, entire systems collapse quickly once the balance between two poles breaks. The Huaxia civilization established under the Zhou Dynasty transcends simple binary contention and built a tripartite architecture of Name, Authority and Wealth. The endurance of Chinese civilization across five millennia originates from this civilizational soul forged in the Spring and Autumn period, embedded with cultural norms matching Name, Authority and Wealth. After the Qin Dynasty’s unification under a single sovereign, feudal dynasties underwent dimensional degradation, yet this civilizational core persisted, making Huaxia civilization unique in human history. This judgment constitutes the core proposition of the series Perpetual Renewal of Huaxia.

While reflecting on the limitations of binary Constructs, a key conclusion emerged: binary structures alone cannot escape the civilizational cycle of rise and fall. This judgment arises from long-term speculative accumulation. Meanwhile, the traditional Chinese social logic of the coexistence of Name, Authority and Wealth came into view. The intersection of these two lines of thought birthed the conception of the Ternary Locked Construct. The Ternary Construct is not simply calculated and deduced from binary logic; it arises from intuitive insight and is then validated through systematic argumentation. It is not a simple superposition of two Binary Constructs, but three mutually supporting and restraining poles forming a closed locked stable foundation.

Corresponding to the civilizational paradigm iteration in H12, this tripartite architecture consists of: Wealth (Gain): material resources, accumulated assets, the material carrier of civilization; Authority: social rules, allocation vouchers, order-governing power, responsible for resource scheduling and validation; Name: public consensus, moral yardstick, social reputation and value-judgment system, independent of Authority and Wealth. It judges the legitimacy of conduct of both and constrains the expansion boundaries of Authority and Wealth.

An ancient Chinese folk proverb states: A tree lives by its bark; a person lives by their reputation. (Heard by the author in hometown in childhood.) The bark sustains the survival of a tree; human “reputation” is essentially Name. This reputation is not superficial vanity. It refers to the positioning and evaluation of one’s deeds within public consensus. Once deprived of Name, an individual exists as if naked within society. Name is a public standing earned through virtue and merit. It is both honor and a benign constraint: whoever holds this Name must exercise self-restraint and refrain from reckless moral decay.

Extended to the civilizational level: the dividing line between civilization and barbarism does not lie in possession of Authority or Wealth. Barbaric systems know only brute force and resources, determining outcomes by power. True civilization possesses this independent value yardstick of Name, to scrutinize the exercise of authority and distinguish the legitimacy of wealth sources. If a culture abandons or dissolves Name, it discards its civilizational core and reverts to a binary structure of contention between Authority and Wealth. Freed from independent moral constraints, authority and wealth circulate and interconvert with one another. The modern Western system exemplifies this: Name is alienated and reduced to an accessory of wealth-authority contention, enabling mutual conversion between capital and power. Conversely, a culture that preserves “reputation” safeguards its foundational civilizational baseline. Though the emphasis on reputation may breed vanity and face-saving projects, its underlying kernel is society’s fundamental demand for value judgment, an indispensable baseline of civilization.

## V. Summary of Laws of Civilizational Evolution

Across thousands of years of global history, many civilizations once flourished magnificently only to fade into the river of time. Huaxia civilization endured countless upheavals without severing its cultural lineage, still undergoing continuity and renewal today. Its foundational core is the tripartite architecture of Name, Authority and Wealth established in the Zhou Dynasty. Civilization is a complex system coupled by multiple Dual Constructs. The tugging between poles of each Binary Dual Construct drives the system forward through fluctuations. Permanently static equilibrium never exists. The system cycles continuously through imbalance, adjustment, overcorrection and reversion, advancing on the whole. Surface Constructs reflect short-term epochal phenomena; Mid-level Constructs govern century-scale civilizational trends; Candidate Ontological Construct determines millennial civilizational epochs. Binary Constructs supply evolutionary motive force; Ternary Locked Constructs stabilize the systemic bedrock and add independent value constraints. Binary mechanisms can drive civilizational iteration; the coexistence of Name, Authority and Wealth forms the complete morphology of advanced civilization. The spiritual core of Huaxia civilization is encapsulated in the ancient maxim of three immortals: establishing virtue, establishing merit, immortalizing words. These three ideals resonate with the tripartite architecture of Name, Authority and Wealth, forming the ultimate pursuit for individuals to practice these civilizational principles.
