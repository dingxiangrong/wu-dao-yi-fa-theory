A working theory series on virtual assets, the dollar system, and who controls the right to allocate resources.

This working paper establishes a new tripartite analytical framework of **Wealth–Entitlement–Authority** to reinterpret the essence of modern virtual finance. It reveals the fundamental dichotomy of the virtual era: **Wealth persists; entitlement can perish**.

Different from traditional economic theories focused only on material stock adjustment, this study demonstrates that global financial and geopolitical conflicts are essentially systematic contests between two supreme rule orders: sovereign entities and international financial capital entities.

The evolution of the U.S. dollar system and the future trajectory of the Federal Reserve are natural outcomes of this long-term structural game. This paradigm provides a new fundamental logic for understanding global virtualized asset civilization.
