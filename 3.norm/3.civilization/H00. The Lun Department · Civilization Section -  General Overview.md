The Civilization Section is woven from two main lines:

### Module One: The Foundation of Historical Perspective (H01) — Chinese History as the Bedrock of Reasoning

H01, _Reflections on the Twenty-Four Histories | Final Text of the Historical Department's General Outline_, opens with the "bones of history" sedimented through reading the histories, proposes the master verdict on Chinese civilization — "Zhou nurtures the soul, Qin casts the bone, Han fixes the form" — and combs through the imagery of twenty-four historical epochs. It establishes the core stance: history is not a heap of stories and records but a waveform of fortune, an imprint of the human heart; history is the substance, principle is the function, and sedimentation is the bridge, providing the indigenous historical grounding and source of thought for all civilizational reasoning in this section.

### Module Two: Cross-Civilizational Geopolitical Deliberation (H03–H05) — Decomposing Real Conflict, Distilling the Preconditions of Order

H03, _Civilizational Roots and Sovereign Cooperation: The Morphological Iteration Path of Islamic Civilization's Modern Revival_, takes Islamic civilization as its sample, distinguishes primary from secondary contradictions, and proposes transmission of the kernel with iteration of form — relying on its own civilizational roots, countering external intervention through sovereign, equal, intra-regional cooperation — exploring a modernization-revival path for non-Western-centered civilization. H04, _Settling Distinctions to Stop Strife: The Logic of Ceasing War — The Underlying Logic of "Settling Distinctions" in the Hundred-Year Israeli–Palestinian Conflict_, drawing on ancient Chinese principle, proposes the First Axiom · Settling Distinctions to Stop Strife: while distinctions remain unsettled, contention never ceases; the confirmation of rightful titles is the starting point of order. It clarifies secondary contradictions against the core legal-contradiction. H05, _Red Lines as Boundaries: The Logic of Coexistence — With an Explanation of the Russia–Ukraine Conflict_, proposes the Second Axiom · Red Lines as Boundaries: the red line is a subject's dynamically shifting core interest boundary; competition and gamesmanship must not breach the other's existential bottom line. It analyzes the tragic causes in the Russia–Ukraine conflict of great-power red lines and small-state misperception of position.

### Module Three: The Formation of Universal Axioms and Their Application in Reality (H06–H09) — Descending from the State to the Individual, Family, and Conduct

H06, _Harmony with Difference: The Logic of Coexistence_, completes the Third Axiom · Harmony with Difference, closing the loop of the three axioms. Settling distinctions is the root, holding boundaries is the floor, and harmony with difference is higher-order coexistence; it distinguishes "passively refraining from harm" from "actively bringing out the best in one another," maps it through the image of the great tree and the small grass, and critiques the disorder of coexistence in reality where large subjects press down upon the small and yang encroaches upon the position of yin. H07, _The Three Axioms of Civilization — A Record of the Birth Thread of Three Manuscripts_, reviews the complete process by which the three axioms came into being: the divergence between the order of thought (red lines → settling distinctions → harmony with difference) and the order of final composition; and it answers the challenge "the law is set, where lies its operation?" — distinguishing static law from dynamic operation. H08, _Case Analysis of the Dynamic Ecological Niche Supporting the Three Axioms of Civilization — A Deep Review of the Youzu Executive Case_, uses a real tragedy as a real-world case of validation, descending the three axioms from state and civilization to the workplace individual, pointing out that the ecological niche is in continuous flux, not static; where niches converge there is rivalry, where they diverge there is coexistence — a warning against the calamities of life that come from failing to grasp dynamic alignment. H09, _The Three Axioms of Civilization · A Version for Transmission Through the Family_, brings the line of order axioms to its closing summation, organizing the three axioms into a family-education and family-conduct guide to be passed down through generations, completing the full-scene convergence from international geopolitics, to organization and team, to individual and household.

### Module Four: The Virtual Age and Global Finance (H10) — A Real-World Sample of Paradigm Shift

H10, _The Endogenous Evolution Path of the Global Monetary System_, comprises five working papers forming a complete framework of reasoning. The core prior axiom: a vast stock of dollar-denominated financial capital, amassed over a century and distributed across sovereign borders, possesses the underlying attribute of being indestructible; no matter how the balance of sovereign power shifts, the global stock of financial capital will never accept the annihilation of overall wealth — and every change in the dollar system is, in essence, the search for a permanently sustainable vessel for this enormous capital. The module advances through a five-layer metaphor — "marriage of convenience — going separate ways — two paths — dual-anchor reconstruction — divorce" — from the honeymoon and deadlock of the contractual relationship between the U.S. government and the Federal Reserve, to the Fed's multi-anchor breakout for survival, to the dual-anchor evolution of "Treasury primary anchor + resource auxiliary anchor," finally settling on the stable state of "divorced but not leaving home" — global multi-stakeholder co-governance. The whole line of reasoning emphasizes evolution rather than revolution, growing new structures upon the old system, as the dollar gradually shifts from "America's currency" to a neutral vessel serving cross-sovereign global capital.

### Module Five: Underlying Axioms and Paradigm Shift (H11–H13) — From Finance to Civilization, Setting the Humanist Baseline

H11, _The Ultimate Nature of Virtual Assets: Two Parallel Systems — Wealth and Rights_, proposes the core thesis: a virtual asset is not wealth; it is a standardized bookkeeping credential for social rights. It builds the three-layer analytical framework of wealth — rights — permissions, and distills the fundamental law of the virtualized age: **wealth endures, rights can perish**. Wealth subsists upon material entities; rights cling to consensus and rules, and once consensus collapses they can be zeroed out. The essence of contemporary global contest is a game between two supreme rights-systems: sovereign entities and international financial capital. H12, _Iteration of Civilizational Paradigm: From Identity-Based to Credential-Measured Future Political Paradigm_, carrying forward H10 and H11, completes the third-order paradigm modeling. It defines the traditional political form as an identity-based rules-system: power attaches to persons, and identity allocates rights — carrying inherent defects of ossification and instability. It proposes the next-generation credential-measured rules-system: the underlying axioms unchanged, rights released from attachment to persons and converted into standardized units of rights, with a standardized two-way channel between rights and wealth that carries a pricing anchor, dual upper-bound constraints, and a neutral arbitration mechanism. The Fed's transformation is a preliminary rehearsal of this paradigm in the global financial domain. H13, _Iteration of Civilizational Subjectivity: The Origin, Boundaries, and Civilizational Advancement of Basic Rights_, the fourth-order deduction of the H-series civilizational research, returns the perspective to human subjectivity. It distinguishes primary basic rights (the right to survival, the right to knowledge) from socially derived rights: primary basic rights are not easily disposed of at the will of social rights and are isolated outside H12's rights-wealth exchange system; moreover, they are not static but progressively expand their boundaries with each leap of civilizational paradigm. This module supplies the humanist constraint behind institutional paradigms, preventing the rules-system from eroding the individual's primordial rights, and completes the closed loop of the entire evolutionary deduction.
