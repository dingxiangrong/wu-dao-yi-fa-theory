# H12. Iteration of Civilizational Paradigms: From Identity-Centric to Voucher-Metered Future Political Paradigm

## Abstract

Built upon H10’s topological analysis of financial phenomena and H11’s binary foundational axiom of wealth and rights, this paper develops the third-stage modeling of the civilizational paradigm system. It defines traditional human political formation as an **identity-centric rule system**: power is attached to persons, and rights are allocated by identity. Between wealth and rights there exists no public, standardized conversion channel, which creates inherent underlying defects of entrenchment and instability. This paper proposes the next-generation **voucher-metered rule system** as the direction of civilizational evolution. The underlying rule system remains intact, yet rights are decoupled from personal affiliation and transformed into standardized right units. A standardized bidirectional channel for wealth-right conversion is established, incorporating a pricing anchor, dual-side ceiling constraints and a neutral arbitration mechanism. Rights are confirmed through voucher metering rather than personal identity or position. The transformation of the Federal Reserve may be viewed as an early preview of this paradigm within global finance. This paradigm shift has no geographical boundaries and represents a shared evolutionary trend of human civilization in the virtualization era.

## II. Redefining the Essence of Superstructure

The superstructure of all human societies has **one sole ontological essence: the rule system**.

Laws, institutions, contracts, morality, social norms, governance orders and power architectures all originate from, attach to and belong to this rule system. States, governments, offices, identities, ranks and authority **are not the ontology of superstructure**. They are merely **carriers and external manifestations** of the rule system within specific historical phases.

The core thread of civilizational evolution lies not in tool, technological or economic iteration, but in: **the iteration of rule systems**. As rules change, civilization changes. The form of rules shapes the form of society.

## III. The First Civilizational Paradigm: Identity-Centric Rule System (Traditional Human Political Formation)

From the dawn of civilization up to the early modern period, humanity operated within **identity-centric rule systems**. Its core characteristics are as follows:

### 1. Rights Attached to Persons

All rights to allocate resources, distribute benefits, govern and adjudicate **are bound to personal identity, post, role and rank**. Monarchs hold ruling power, officials hold governing power, and lords hold territorial administrative power. Position equals power; persons bear authority.

### 2. Rules Serve Identity

Rules do not define rights; instead, **identity allocates rights**. Rule systems endorse, solidify and legitimize established identity hierarchies, identity structures and personal power.

### 3. No Unified Standard for Wealth-Right Conversion

Between material foundations (wealth) and superordinate power (rights), **there exists no universal, quantifiable, stable or transparent conversion mechanism**.

The exchange of rights for wealth and wealth for rights relies on personal connections, discretionary judgement, privilege, monopolization of identity privileges and personal influence. Society’s entire resource exchange system is personalistic, ambiguous, stochastic and non-standardized.

### 4. Authority Non-Detachable, Non-Transferable, Non-Recordable

Power rises and falls with posts and persons. Power exists when the person holds office and vanishes upon departure. Power cannot be quantified, divided, circulated or fairly vested.

**Ultimate Limitation of Identity-Centric Systems:** At the foundational level of civilization, there exists **no public, objective and universal order for wealth-right relations**. Politics, economy, distribution and intergenerational inheritance all operate through personalized power. Inevitably, this leads to stratification entrenchment, governance volatility, distribution imbalance and systemic instability. This constitutes an **innate underlying defect** of traditional human political civilization.

## IV. The Second Civilizational Paradigm: Voucher-Metered Rule System (Future Evolving Political Paradigm)

Building upon H11’s _Binary Axiom of Wealth and Rights_, this paper advances toward a final iteration, describing a structural leap for human civilization: **the rule system itself remains unchanged, yet the carrier form, vesting mechanism and operational logic of rights are thoroughly reconstructed.**

The new superstructure form for future civilization is defined as: **voucher-metered rule system**. Its core logic does not overthrow laws, institutions or public rules, but **reconstructs the carrier and mode of operation for rights**, forming a new political paradigm.

### 1. Rules as Foundation, Identity as Derivative

In future society: **Rules define rights; identity derives from rules.** Power is no longer obtained by occupying a post. Instead, rule systems directly define the boundary of authority for social actors. Identity, role, rank and stratum are all outcomes of rule operation, no longer sources of power.

### 2. Rights Decoupled from Personal Bondage

All resource allocation and social distribution rights **are no longer attached to any individual, post or role**. Liberated from personalized constraints, rights become standardized right units under public social rules.

### 3. Establishing Humanity’s First “Standardized Bidirectional Wealth-Right Channel”

This represents the paradigm’s **most central, most transformative and most milestone-defining civilizational breakthrough**.

For thousands of years across traditional civilizations, **material foundations and superstructure remained separated**. Political economy only states that “the base determines the superstructure, and the superstructure reacts upon the base”, yet it has never addressed, or even defined: how rights convert into wealth, how wealth converts into rights, and how to build a quantifiable, equitable, universal and socially recognized conversion mechanism.

The H12 paradigm delivers this ultimate complement: **Relying on unified public rules and standardized metering vouchers, rights and wealth achieve standardized, bidirectional, quantitative, transparent, recordable, transferable and notarized conversion.**

The material order at the lower level and the rule order at the upper level **are fully connected, completely closed-loop and operate as a unified whole.**

#### Framework of Principles for the Wealth-Right Conversion Mechanism

This bidirectional channel maintains stability via three sets of core constraint parameters, without preset fixed constants; all parameters are dynamically calibrated by public rules:

1. **Pricing Anchor**: The conversion ratio is anchored to the total stock of usable physical wealth across society and the aggregate volume of effective social governance authority. Calibration is continuously performed by public rules and cannot be unilaterally decided by any single actor.
2. **Ceiling Constraints**: Bidirectional conversion thresholds are set. Caps are imposed on holdings of governance authority to prevent unlimited conversion of wealth into discretionary power. Meanwhile, a total cap is placed on the conversion of rights into wealth to avoid one-time depletion of large social material reserves.
3. **Arbitration Rules**: When disputes arise among actors over conversion or vesting, rulings do not depend on the personal or positional authority of individuals. Judgements are delegated to the neutral arbitration mechanism embedded within this public rule system.

### 4. Metering Vouchers Are Merely Representations; Rule Systems Are the Core

A strict distinction must be drawn between core substance and carrier: **Vouchers are not rules; metering is not the system.** Standardized accounting units and social vesting symbols **are only quantitative representations of rights.** 

What truly constitutes the future superstructure remains the full system of laws, institutions, contracts and public rules. Vouchers handle “metering, registration, solidification and transfer”; rules handle “definition, authorization, constraint and governance.”

This avoids a critical misunderstanding: it is not metering vouchers that govern society. **It is the standardized rule system, through metering vouchers, that delivers implementable, quantifiable and equitable new political governance.**
