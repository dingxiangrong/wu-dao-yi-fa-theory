## II. Historical Root Cause: “Name Forced to Attach to Matter” in Scarcity Eras

Ancient human civilizations over thousands of years were constrained by material scarcity. Limited output and tight survival resources prevented spiritual symbols, honor, virtue, merit and Name from existing independently.

All forms of “Name” had to be attached to land, noble titles, taxation and hereditary privileges. This created a long-running cycle: Name was bound to material distribution rights. Society converged into a single ranking track centered on material gain, yielding zero-sum games, class solidification and an identity-dependent resource allocation model.

Industrialization in modern times alleviated material scarcity and created conditions for spiritual independence and value realignment. Nevertheless, Western capital systems evolved an alternative alienation pathway: “Name”, originally attached to human beings, carrying moral weight and housing subjective telos, was severed from subjects and grafted onto commercial objects such as brands, IP, traffic and corporate symbols.

A new social operating logic emerged from this arrangement: attaining Name yields material gains, and greater influence of Name translates to higher premiums and stronger capital appreciation potential.

When reputation can be monetized, virtue packaged, public praise marketed and Name traded, civilizational value scales become vulnerable to capture by capital logic. This constitutes a major source of restlessness, fabricated personas, traffic fraud and value distortion in contemporary society.

## III. Civilizational Inflection Point: Material Saturation Enables Bifurcated Dual Paths

Industrialization gradually satisfies basic subsistence supply, bringing a structural shift to human civilization. Material needs have physical upper bounds, while spiritual needs hold room for continuous expansion.

A single material value system cannot accommodate humanity’s diverse value pursuits. Society will diverge into two evolutionary pathways of distinct nature:

- Material Path: marked by stock constraints and zero-sum properties, requiring boundary setting.
- Spiritual Path: endowed with room for growth and symbiotic expansion.

A viable direction for advanced civilization: bifurcated dual paths operating in parallel with insulated boundaries.

## IV. Rigid Definition of the Two Tracks: Each in Its Proper Sphere, No Mutual Erosion

### 4.1 Material Track: Institutional Safeguards, Market Circulation, No Value Kidnapping

Attributes of the material track: covering food, clothing, shelter, wealth circulation, production and trade; falling within the Authority–Wealth binary Dual Construct system; bounded and capped, inherently zero-sum.

Functional positioning: guarantees basic subsistence and carries no role in personality judgment, civilizational valuation or merit ranking. Wealth may be accumulated, traded and competed for; yet wealth no longer determines one’s social rank nor defines an individual’s civilizational value.

### 4.2 Spiritual Track: Rule-Based Naming, Factual Validation of Merit, Non-Profit Red Line

The spiritual track serves as the practical carrier for the dimension of “Name” introduced in H15. Core definition: Name is a public value yardstick, a record of civilizational merit and the locus of human subjectivity. It is strictly protected against commercialization, capitalization and premiumization.

Permanent rigid constraints:

- Name cannot be bought or sold
- Name cannot carry premium value
- Name cannot be monetized for profit
- Name cannot be packaged or speculated upon by capital

Name may only come with capped, limited, baseline welfare benefits. The welfare cap is judged against the criterion of preventing hereditary class privilege and new monopolies, guarding against windfall gains, monopolization and intergenerational class entrenchment.

The spiritual track supports a pluralistic value system. Every profession has its own Name, merit and station. Craftspeople, physicians, scholars, farmers and teachers each earn their own recognition. Value tracks expand in diversity, so society no longer competes for a single material pyramid.

## V. Core Practical Breakthrough: Name Granted Neither by Human Judgment Nor Power Bestowal — Generated via Public Rule Autonomy

### 5.1 Resolving the Ultimate Question: Who Defines Name? Could New Power Monopolies Emerge?

This is the most critical practical issue for implementing the ternary system. This paper offers an implementation pathway: within an advanced civilizational system, Name is not conferred by individuals or institutions, but generated through rule fulfillment. Mature real-world precedents already exist: Olympic competition, Go dan ranks, piano grading, academic peer review and world record certification. No single entity may privately grant, revoke or alter assessment outcomes. Victory rests on competitive performance, ranks on long-term competence, records on objective data, and achievements on public verification.

### 5.2 The Ultimate Validation Mechanism for the Spiritual Track

Publicly disclosed rules, pre-set standards, transparent processes, verifiable outcomes, merit-based validation, and community autonomy.

1. Rules disclosed in advance: professional communities across industries formulate stable, long-term, reproducible evaluation criteria fully open to the public.
2. Automatic validation upon merit: meeting criteria earns Name; accomplishing deeds earns merit; records establish rank. Name arises from factual achievements rather than discretionary bestowal.
3. Power enforces boundaries, not rankings: administrative power only safeguards track fairness, penalizes fraud and defends baseline rules. It has no authority to alter industry grades, revise rankings or revoke public reputation.
4. Decentralized community autonomy — decentralized in the sense of non-bureaucratic community self-governance, not blockchain-based decentralization: occupational reputation, professional prestige and skill rankings evolve naturally from peer consensus, long-term performance and industry records; non-centralized, non-monopolistic, resistant to bureaucratization.

### 5.3 Root-level mitigation of capture by power and capital

Since Name cannot deliver excess returns:

- Capital loses motive for manipulation;
- Power loses rent-seeking space;
- Incentives for individual fabrication and hype diminish.

No windfall profit from Name yields integrity; decoupling from gains yields impartiality; rule-based autonomy yields independence. This fulfills the ancient ideal: merit brings its proper recognition, and Name matches reality.

## VI. Positive Transformation of Zero-Sum Games: Preserve Competition, Replace Its Object

Human competitive instinct need not be eliminated. Competition itself is not evil; the root problem lies in confining competition to material stock.

Bifurcated dual paths upgrade the logic of competition:

- Competition on the material track: securing subsistence with moderate restraint
- Competition on the spiritual track: contesting achievement, craft, virtue and contributions to civilization

Analogous to athletic competition: contests produce winners and losers, yet the prize is honor, records, rank and merit rather than seizure of others’ material wealth. Humanity’s drive for advancement is diverted from zero-sum struggle over existing stock into incremental momentum for civilizational iteration.
