# H15. Deduction of the Advanced Civilizational Paradigm: The Complete Locked Construct of Human — Culture — Name

## Subtitle — Iterative Deduction Based on H10–H14 Civilizational Topology

### Abstract

This paper constitutes the fifth-stage deduction in the H-Series Civilizational Topology research. It builds upon the full chain of findings from H10 Topology of Financial Phenomena, H11 Binary Axiom of Wealth and Authority, H12 Institutional Paradigm of Voucher Metering, H13 Origin of Civilizational Subjectivity, and H14 Hierarchical Decomposition of Civilizational Dual Constructs. The evolution of human civilizations in prior eras has long been confined within oscillatory systems of binary Dual Constructs. The two-way game between Authority and Wealth forms the core driving force of social operation, yet such systems carry inherent structural defects: the absence of an independent public value yardstick, prone to dimensional deficiency and systemic imbalance triggered by polar coupling, giving rise to various forms of civilizational disequilibrium.

This paper accomplishes three core lines of argumentation. First, it systematically reviews the academic genealogy of H10 through H14, clarifying the evolutionary pathway and core deficiencies of human civilization from phenomenal binary, axiomatic binary, institutional binary to oscillatory binary. Second, it analyzes three layers of evolutionary deviation within modern Western civilization: driven by multiple historical factors, Name has gradually been severed from the Human subject, subsequently materialized, and ultimately alienated through commercialization, diverting civilization away from the logic of higher-order iteration. Third, it proposes an architectural model for advanced civilization: the Complete Locked Construct of Human — Culture — Name. It defines the core attributes, authority-interest boundaries and check-and-balance mechanisms of the three dimensions, addressing longstanding deficits of binary systems: the lack of a value anchor, subjective telos and normative baseline, and advancing the closed-loop structuring of civilizational evolution theory.

**Keywords**: Civilizational Paradigm Deduction; Ternary Locked Construct; Human Subjectivity; Value Yardstick; Deviations of Binary Systems; Closed-Loop Structure; Steady-State

## I. Genealogical Retrospection: Evolution and Deficiencies of the Binary Civilizational System in H10–H14

Reviewing the research lineage of the first five installments of the H-Series, the modern evolution of human civilization may be interpreted as a history of continuous tugging, unidirectional iteration and progressive imbalance within binary Dual Constructs. Social oscillation, institutional turnover and civilizational contention can largely be explained by the inherent limitations of binary systems. Prior studies have not yet established a stable architecture for advanced civilization.

### 1.1 H10: The Layer of Financial Phenomena — Binary Game of Authority–Wealth in Global Capital

Taking the Federal Reserve and the global US dollar system as core observational samples, H10 reveals a deep reality of the modern world: top-level contention among global civilizations is not merely confrontation over national territory and population, but binary tug-of-war between sovereign Authority and international financial capital. The iteration, restructuring and differentiation of the global financial system draw their core momentum from mutual checks and adversarial interplay between power and interest. Civilizational observation at this stage remains at the superficial social phenomenal layer, featuring an underlying regime dominated by Authority-Wealth games and devoid of independent value constraints.

### 1.2 H11: The Layer of Underlying Axioms — The Fundamental Binary Rule Governing Civilization

H11 elevates theoretical abstraction and distills the core underlying axiom of civilizational operation: Wealth persists, while Authority is perishable. From this axiom, it constructs the core analytical framework of Wealth (Gain) — Authority binary duality. The paper explicitly defines that virtual assets are not physical wealth, but standardized bookkeeping vouchers for social Authority. The operational logic of top-tier human societies revolves around two dimensions: accumulation of resource Wealth and validation of social rules via Authority.

The underlying structure of modern civilization is thereby anchored: social operating rules, institutional design and resource allocation revolve mainly around the two core variables of Authority and Wealth. There exists no dimension of value judgment, moral constraint or subjective telos independent of them. The underlying genetic trait of binary systems is thus entrenched.

### 1.3 H12: The Layer of Institutional Paradigms — Maturation of the Objectified Binary System

H12 completes iterative modeling of civilizational institutional paradigms and puts forward the historic transition of human civilization from identity-centric systems to voucher-metered systems. Under the new paradigm, Authority gradually detaches from personal bondage and transforms into standardized, transferable and convertible social vouchers, establishing an institutional channel enabling bidirectional exchange between Authority and Wealth.

Nevertheless, this paradigm bears structural flaws. The whole system approximates a purely objectified toolset. Rules, vouchers, capital and Authority primarily serve resource scheduling and interest circulation, answering the question of _how to allocate Authority and Wealth and improve circulation efficiency_, while relatively neglecting human subjectivity, the legitimacy of conduct and the value telos of civilization. The more sophisticated the institutions become, the more hollow the civilizational core tends to grow.

### 1.4 H14: The Layer of Construct Decomposition — Civilizational Evolution Characterized by Binary Oscillation

Built upon A57 Flux-Construct Ontology, H14 carries out hierarchical decomposition of Dual Constructs across the full scope of civilization, dividing civilizational evolution into three tiers: surface phenomenal duality, mid-level pillar duality and underlying ontological duality. It proposes Human <--> Culture as the underlying candidate Dual Construct, without permanently fixing ontological conclusions.

Coupled oscillation among multiple binary Constructs — Fairness vs Efficiency, Liberty vs Order, Dao vs Instrument, Material vs Spirit, Authority vs Wealth, Economic Base vs Superstructure, Human vs Culture — propels civilization forward through fluctuations. Yet a weakness persists: binary Constructs are suited to generating evolutionary driving force but cannot independently sustain a steady-state civilization. With only polar tugging absent a third value anchor, overcorrection, coupled polar imbalance and systemic destabilization readily emerge, forming a boundary constraint that traditional and modern Western civilizations struggle to transcend.

In summary, H10–H14 demonstrate a shared trait of low- and mid-tier civilizations: they are dominated by oscillatory binary systems of Authority–Wealth, lacking an independent value dimension, and therefore deprived of stable subjective telos, moral baseline and steady-state bedrock. This constitutes one of the fundamental structural triggers of war, wealth disparity, capital monopoly and civilizational disequilibrium.

## II. Deviation Analysis: Modern Western Civilization — Alienation of Name and Dimensional Degradation of Civilization

Based on the genealogical tracing of binary systems from H10 to H14, we may analyze the evolutionary deviation of modern Western civilization. The Western regime did not deliberately design this trajectory; rather, driven by multiple historical factors, it gradually reshapes Name sequentially through three phases: severance, materialization and commercialization, diverting civilization from the path of higher-order iteration.

### 2.1 Deviation 1: Severance of Name from the Human Subject

Originally attached to humans, Name functions as the inherent public moral marker of human subjects. The first transformation of this regime severs Name from the Human subject. Through corporate legal personality, commercial shells and capital structures, the modern Western system achieves separation between human beings and Name. Controllers of capital and top Authority subjects hide behind objectified entities such as enterprises, brands and institutions. The object of public value judgment shifts away from the human subject itself toward external carriers. Subjects no longer bear full moral costs for their acts of seizing Authority and Wealth. When brand goodwill collapses, entities may reorganize anew; asset risks may be isolated and partitioned; unjust Wealth may be concealed and preserved through institutional structures. Consequently, humans partially escape the constraints of Name, and the Authority–Wealth game partially evades moral oversight.

### 2.2 Deviation 2: Materialization of Name

After severance, Name — once bound to humans as a marker of subjectivity — becomes attached to external objects such as commodities, brands and intellectual property, undergoing materialization. Within traditional Huaxia civilization, the proverb “A tree relies on its bark; a person relies on reputation” describes Name as a protective layer for human subjectivity, a lock restraining conduct and a locus of value telos. Following materialization, Name adheres to artifacts and commercial carriers and no longer fully carries humanistic value, thereby weakening the independent public value yardstick.

### 2.3 Deviation 3: Commercialization of Name

Upon materialization, Name is further transformed into a profit-generating instrument. Originally tasked with judging the legitimacy of Authority, calibrating civilizational direction and restraining subject conduct, Name becomes commercialized and capitalized. Brand reputation, public prestige and personal personas become commercial assets that can be packaged, marketed, monetized and traded. Cultivating reputation is no longer merely about upholding morality, self-perfection and contributing to civilization; it frequently becomes a means of acquiring greater Wealth and expanding Authority.

As a result, yardsticks serve interest, morality attaches to capital, and value becomes subordinate to profit. Binary Authority–Wealth systems already carry risks of destabilization. When this dimension of value check-and-balance is alienated, bought and exploited, Authority and Wealth readily shed baseline constraints, bringing capital monopoly, wealth polarization, social disorder and civilizational hollowing as potential outcomes.

In summary, modern Western civilization operates within a binary framework. Within this regime, Name undergoes systematic alienation, solidifying the Authority–Wealth binary game and diverting human civilization from higher-order iteration.

## III. Paradigm Deduction: The Complete Locked Construct of Human — Culture — Name

Building upon the candidate underlying Dual Construct Human <--> Culture from H14, Name is introduced as the third pole to form the Complete Ternary Locked Construct. This architecture accommodates the Flux-Construct interplay within the Human–Culture duality, while supplementing the missing value anchor absent in binary systems.

This Ternary Construct is not a simple superposition of dimensions. Instead, it is a civilizational architecture where each pole fulfills distinct roles, mutually checks and balances one another, forms closed-loop locking and tends toward steady-state perpetuation. It attempts to address core dilemmas of binary systems: oscillatory destabilization, lack of value anchor and absence of subjective telos. It represents a type of advanced civilizational architecture with steady-state potential.

### 3.1 Pole One: Human — The Sole Active Subjective Origin of Civilization (Carrier of Flux)

**Core Definition**: Humans constitute the core subject, ultimate end and telos of all civilization; they are the only dynamic variables within the civilizational system endowed with capacities for exploration, breakthrough, boundary expansion and innovation. **Core Functions**: (1) Bear the innate fundamental rights established in H13 and serve as the starting point and destination of all civilizational value; (2) Break rigid constraints of Culture and dismantle institutional barriers, driving continuous iterative upgrading of civilization; (3) Receive value judgment via Name, align subjective conduct with the moral yardstick, and realize the unity of self-value and civilizational value. **Essential Attribute**: Embodies **Flux**, the source of dynamism enabling civilization’s perpetual renewal and boundary expansion, preventing civilizational stagnation.

### 3.2 Pole Two: Culture — Aggregate of All Constraint Constructs across Civilization (Carrier of Construct)

**Core Definition**: Culture is the collective designation for the full system of constraint Constructs accumulated over the long evolution of human civilization, including social institutions, rules and order, traditional consensus, value paradigms and Authority-Wealth circulation mechanisms. It forms the foundational bedrock for civilizational persistence. **Core Functions**: (1) Adopt the constraint logic of A57 Flux-Construct Ontology to reasonably regulate human dynamic boundary-expanding conduct and prevent unordered expansion; (2) Accommodate the Authority–Wealth binary operating system from H11–H12, providing an institutional framework for social resource allocation, Authority circulation and economic operation; (3) Preserve civilizational experience, inherit civilizational lineage, and sustain social stability and ethnic continuity. **Essential Attribute**: Embodies **Construct**, the constraint bedrock for maintaining civilizational order and inheritance, preventing chaotic turbulence.

### 3.3 Pole Three: Name — The Independent Value Yardstick of Civilization (Carrier of Equilibrium)

**Core Definition**: As a public moral yardstick, Name is logically independent of Humans, Culture and the Authority-Wealth game system. It is one dimension within advanced civilization for judging right and wrong, calibrating boundaries and defining value telos. It must resist objectification, commodification and commercialized alienation. **Core Functions**: (1) Check Authority: judge the legitimacy of the acquisition and exercise of Authority, restraining private abuse of public power and monopoly of Authority; (2) Restrain Wealth: identify the moral boundaries of Wealth accumulation and circulation, curbing unjust wealth and capital predation; (3) Calibrate Culture: rectify rigid defects and institutional loopholes within cultural systems and drive positive iteration of Culture; (4) Anchor the Subject: ground the value of human subjectivity, addressing the question of subjective immortality through establishing virtue, merit and words, and providing individuals with civilizational telos. **Attribute Boundary**: Institutional design ought to strip Name of direct material conversion functions so it operates independently solely as a public value yardstick. Name itself also requires dynamic verification by Humans and Culture to prevent the yardstick from rigidifying into new authoritarian dogma. **Essential Attribute**: Embodies **Equilibrium**, the balancing dimension distinguishing good from evil, calibrating boundaries and sustaining perpetual steady state, preventing coupled polar imbalance.

## IV. Operating Mechanism of the Ternary Locked Construct: Closed-Loop Logic of Steady-State Advanced Civilization

The three dimensions of Human, Culture and Name form a three-dimensional closed loop of _Dynamism — Constraint — Equilibrium_. It seeks to transcend the limitation of unidirectional oscillation of binary systems and construct advanced civilization tending toward steady state: Humans generate dynamism through boundary expansion: relying on subjectivity, humans break outdated cultural fetters, optimize Authority–Wealth allocation models and supply continuous innovation and developmental momentum for civilization. Culture defines order through constraint: Culture regulates the boundary of human conduct, institutionalizes Authority–Wealth operation and safeguards the baseline order for social persistence. Name defines morality through check-and-balance: the independent value yardstick constrains both Humans and Culture, preventing unregulated boundary expansion by humans, rigid alienation of Culture, and above all, coupled imbalance of the Authority–Wealth binary.

Mutual locking and dynamic equilibrium: Flux (Human), Construct (Culture) and Equilibrium (Name) are interdependent and mutually restrictive. Humans and Culture jointly verify and iterate Name to prevent rigidification of the value yardstick; Name in turn calibrates Humans and Culture and restrains Authority-Wealth games, pushing civilization toward dynamic steady state and perpetual advancement.

## V. Conclusion

Low- and mid-tier civilizations operate primarily via Human–Culture binary oscillation and Authority–Wealth binary games. Lacking an independent value yardstick, they readily fall into cycles of imbalance, oscillation and alienation. The systematic alienation of Name within modern Western civilization represents one form evolved under the binary framework.

The core deviation of modern Western civilization lies in severing Name from the Human subject, followed by materialization and commercialization, which weakens this value check-and-balance dimension and diverts civilization from higher-order iteration pathways.

The advanced civilizational architectural model deduced in this paper is the Complete Locked Construct of Human — Culture — Name. Humans carry dynamism, Culture carries order, and Name carries value. The three-dimensional closed loop enables perpetual check-and-balance, forming a civilizational architecture capable of realizing humanistic telos and orderly steady state.

The core logic of civilizational iteration: Binary Constructs enable civilization’s "growth"; only Ternary Constructs can bring about civilization’s "maturity". The future direction of human civilizational upgrading is essentially the return to and realization of the Human — Culture — Name ternary steady-state paradigm.
