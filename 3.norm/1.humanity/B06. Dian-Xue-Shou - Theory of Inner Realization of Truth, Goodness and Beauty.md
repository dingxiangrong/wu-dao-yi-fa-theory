## General Thesis

All civilizational activities of human society are, in essence, the pursuit of Beauty. Every act of creation, every institution, every mode of production, every art, every quest for understanding — all originate from humanity's instinctive inclination toward the Beauty of the rhythmic pulsation of the Dao of Heaven.

Across the millennia that followed, the world inverted this order and confused the meanings of the words: it placed Truth first, treated Goodness as Virtue, and reduced Beauty to mere ornament. It narrowed "Goodness" into worldly kindness, moral forbearance and passivity. It forcibly absorbed the ontological reality of "Virtue" into the category of Goodness. In the end, this produced an inverted ordering, a blurred ontology and a schism between knowing and acting.

Now the original ordering of the innate mind is reset: **Beauty comes first, Truth is the core, Goodness is the method.** Virtue is returned to Truth; upright conduct is established in action. Rectify the source, clarify the root, so as to align with the Dao of Heaven.

## I. Beauty, as the Origin of the Dao of Heaven (The Beginning of Civilization, The Beginning of Perception)

Beauty is the inherent rhythmic pulsation of Heaven and Earth, the manifesting breath of the Great Dao. All things that stir the heart, all moments in which awareness arises, all undertakings that commence — begin with Beauty. Beauty is humanity's first layer of intuition, its first gateway, its first source of arising toward the Dao of Heaven.

**Force itself is Beauty.** The essence of the Dao of Heaven is the ceaselessly generative pulsation, potential energy and transforming force. Real, stable and well-grounded force is itself the supreme Beauty.

For a person to cultivate the self and align with the Dao is to let the rhythm of one's own life approach the pulsation of the Dao of Heaven, becoming ever more full, resolute and powerful — great Beauty then arises of itself.

Force, though great, must not be wielded recklessly. Force is rooted in Truth, and its boundaries rest upon Virtue. To lose Virtue is to lose Truth; to lose Truth leaves force without roots, and it will ultimately dissipate and wither.

## II. Truth, as the True Substance of All Things (The Ontological Core, The Source of Virtue)

Truth means: to be inward and outward as they actually are — without falsity, without illusion, without affectation or adornment. Outwardly, it is the original appearance of all things; inwardly, it is the native state of the human heart.

**Virtue and Truth share the same source.** Virtue is not a secular moral precept; it is the life-state in which the genuine real is realized upon a person. To move in accordance with true substance, to present and transform things faithfully as they are — this is to possess Virtue. To violate true substance, to fabricate illusion, to distort the ontological — this is to lose Virtue; and to lose Virtue is, in essence, to lose Truth.

All illusory Beauty, false pleasure and empty appearances in the world arise from detachment from true substance:

- **The pleasure of drug intoxication**: artificial stimulation manufactures a fleeting illusory sensation, severed from the true substance of body and mind — rootless, qualityless, depleting the origin, losing Truth and losing Virtue.
- **Modern standardized cosmetic aesthetics**: imposing external man-made standards upon natural life, shaping the body to a mold, adorning over the genuine, distorting the primordial state endowed by the Dao of Heaven — chasing illusion and consuming the self, likewise losing Truth and losing Virtue.

True Beauty is never sought externally. It issues from within outward; when true substance is full, vital energy flows unimpeded, and true Beauty generates force.

## III. Goodness, as the Method of Practicing the Dao (Resetting a Millennial Misinterpretation)

The greatest error of later ages: interpreting "Goodness" as human kindness, forbearance, non-malignancy and upright character. This mistakenly installs the ontology of Virtue into the meaning of the word Goodness, producing a millennia-long error in principle.

The original meaning of antiquity — the "Goodness" of Laozi's "the highest Goodness is like water" — was never a matter of moral character. Goodness means: that which is proper, well-devised, well-tempered, following the natural flow, and a practicable path.

**Goodness is a tool, a method, a pathway, a system of practice.**

- Learning is the good method of cognition
- Wandering and observation are the good method of verification
- Contemplation is the good method of clarifying principle
- Science is the good method of seeking proof

Every effective practice used to guard Truth, to sustain Virtue, to carry Beauty and to approach the Dao of Heaven is subsumed under Goodness.

Summary of the rectification of meanings: **Virtue (德) = ontology, true substance, character, the manifestation of Truth (returned to Truth)** **Goodness (善) = method, pathway, means, the art of practice (returned to action)**

Truth establishes the inner; Goodness enacts the outer. With Virtue, the root is laid; with Goodness, it lands in practice.

## IV. Academy for Distinguishing Beauty | The Practice System of Self-Cultivation and Dao-Alignment

I have established the Academy for Distinguishing Beauty, with one sole purpose: to teach people to distinguish true Beauty, to strip away illusory Beauty, to follow Beauty in seeking Truth, to guard Virtue and consolidate the root, to nourish force through method, and to cultivate the self in alignment with the Dao.

The order of practice follows strictly the distance from the Dao of Heaven and the sequence of awareness:

1. **Music as the first discipline** (closest to the Dao of Heaven) Without the obstruction of verbal concepts, it directly inherits the rhythmic pulsation of Heaven and Earth. It calibrates the rhythm of body and mind, resonates with the Great Dao, and awakens and nourishes Beauty.
2. **Painting as the second study** (observing forms, attaining the Dao) Observing the vital rhythm of Heaven and Earth, the potential force of mountains and rivers, the transformation of emptiness and fullness — nourishing the heart through images, awakening the Dao through form.
3. **Poetry as the third pursuit** (settling the writing through the heart) The flow of the innate mind, emotions and aspirations returning to Truth, nurturing Virtue, preserving the native state.
4. **Humanistic narrative** (discerning the true from the false) Reading the world and observing people, seeing through emptiness and reality, discerning the preservation and loss of Virtue, understanding that force must not be recklessly applied.
5. **Science as the final discipline** (the tool of good method) Science is a path of seeking proof, a posteriori instrument. One must first establish Beauty, Truth, Virtue and the innate mind, and only then use science to assist the Dao, to attest the Dao and to guard the Dao.

## V. The Ultimate General Order of the Innate Mind

Beauty is the beginning of stirring, Truth is the core of standing, Goodness is the path of practice.

The mind gives birth to the Beauty of all things, The mind guards the native Truth, Within it dwells the homogeneous Virtue, Its conduct holds the well-tempered Goodness.

**Great Beauty generates force, and force itself is Beauty; Only with Virtue can one sustain force, only with Goodness can one complete action. Cultivate the self and align with the Dao, so as to draw near the heart of Heaven.**

## VI. Mutual Attestation Through Poetry

The essence of poetry: the flowing of the innate mind's true Virtue. The function of poetry: the permeation of the great Beauty of Heaven and Earth. The method of poetry: the well-tempered skill that is properly measured. A single poem contains Beauty, Truth, Virtue and Goodness. A single practice connects to the innate mind, to the human world, and to the Dao of Heaven.
