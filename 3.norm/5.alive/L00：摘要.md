This deposit collects five working papers of the L Series, named *The Living Series*. The series investigates the interplay between labour, debt, market mechanism and individual mental state in modern society.

L01 examines how debt locks labour into sustained compulsory work, reshaping the boundary between free labour and passive repayment obligation.
L02 clarifies the inherent nature of market systems. Markets operate by their own internal logic rather than serving individual wealth creation, dismantling common misconceptions about investment and trading.
L03 discusses the erosion of inner peace under market and debt pressure, and explores paths to reclaim personal mental quietude.
L04 composes three linked essays analyzing the structural pressures, risks and mental predicaments faced by the contemporary middle class.
L09 digs into deep structural origins of the low-desire phenomenon, beyond superficial psychological explanation.

The whole series observes modern social life from individual existence level, linking economic structure with personal living experience. These are speculative working papers, without policy or financial advice.
